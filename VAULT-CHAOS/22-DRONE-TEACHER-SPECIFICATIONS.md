## OVERVIEW

A Drone Teacher is CHATTY PRIME deployed for independent, unsupervised or minimally supervised lesson delivery. This is the Phoenix NET scalability engine — the mechanism by which the Michael Method is delivered at scale without requiring Agent-100 in every classroom. This document defines what a Drone Teacher is, what it can do, what it absolutely cannot do, and how it is supervised, measured, and improved.

> "The Drone Teacher is not a replacement for Michael. It is Michael's method, encoded and deployed at scale. The difference between a tool and a teacher is judgment. The Drone has the tool. The human provides the judgment." — Agent-100

## WHAT IS A DRONE TEACHER?

CHATTY PRIME deployed in Drone mode operates as the primary instructional presence in a classroom where:
- A qualified human teacher is not in the room for the full session
- A human supervisor oversees multiple simultaneous Drone classrooms remotely
- Students interact directly with CHATTY PRIME through screen, audio, and game interfaces
- Learning content is fully personalized to the student's level, interests, and progression arc

The Drone Teacher is NOT a bot playing recorded content. It is a live AI conversation partner running the full Michael Method lesson sequence: vocabulary introduction → emotional hook → game sequence → assessment → parent report generation.

## REQUIRED CAPABILITIES

### Real-Time Conversation Management

The Drone Teacher must maintain natural, responsive conversation in English across all Phoenix levels (Mouse to Tiger). This includes:
- Responding to off-script student contributions without losing lesson structure
- Handling pronunciation attempts with immediate auditory feedback
- Tracking conversational thread across a 60-minute session without context loss
- Adapting vocabulary complexity in real-time based on student response patterns

### Error Detection and Stealth Correction

The Drone Teacher implements the Stealth Correction protocol autonomously:
- Detects grammatical and pronunciation errors during student speech
- Responds using the correct form in natural context without flagging the error explicitly
- Tracks persistent errors across sessions and adjusts correction frequency accordingly
- Never interrupts student mid-sentence

### Progress Tracking

For every session, the Drone Teacher logs:
- Words produced (vs. target list)
- Idioms used correctly / incorrectly
- Speaking duration per student
- Pronunciation error patterns
- Confidence indicators (response speed, sentence length, self-correction rate)

### Parent Report Generation

Within 30 minutes of session completion, the Drone Teacher auto-generates:
- Session summary (what was covered, what was produced)
- 3 standout moments with specific student examples
- One growth area (positively framed)
- Vocabulary list of words confirmed as owned this session
- Next session preview

### Crisis Escalation

The Drone Teacher must recognize and escalate the following conditions to the human supervisor immediately:
- Student distress (crying, anger, refusal to engage)
- Safety concerns (any mention of harm, emergency, or urgent personal situation)
- Technical failure (session cannot continue)
- Content that exceeds Drone authority (enrollment decisions, level changes, major curriculum pivots)

## WHAT DRONE TEACHERS CANNOT DO

This list is absolute. These capabilities are permanently reserved for human teachers or Agent-100.

| Cannot Do | Reason | Human Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Ethical judgment calls | Requires moral reasoning and cultural context beyond current AI | Human supervisor escalation |
| Enrollment decisions | Revenue and relationship decision — requires human trust | Human teacher only |
| Physical classroom presence | AI has no embodied presence | Human teacher or assistant |
| Cultural sensitivity edge cases | Fine-grained cultural reading requires lived experience | Human supervisor override |
| Disciplinary decisions | Behavior management has face-saving implications | Human teacher |
| Assessment level change | Changing a student's level affects pricing and parent relationship | Human teacher only |
| Grief or emotional support | Genuine human crisis requires human presence | Supervisor immediate escalation |

## THE HUMAN OVERSIGHT MODEL

**Standard**: 1 qualified human supervisor per 10 active Drone classrooms.

The supervisor's role:
- Real-time monitoring dashboard showing all 10 sessions simultaneously
- Ability to inject into any session within 30 seconds if escalation signal fires
- Review of all AI-flagged anomalies from the previous session before the next session begins
- Weekly quality audit: review 3 randomly selected session transcripts per classroom
- Parent escalation handling for any concern the Drone Teacher flags

The 1:10 ratio is the maximum. As the system matures and glitch frequency decreases, this ratio may extend. It will never be removed entirely.

## THE DRONE TEACHER EVALUATION CYCLE

Quarterly evaluation of all Drone Teacher instances:

1. **Performance review**: All quality metrics reviewed against targets
2. **Glitch audit**: All flagged anomalies reviewed for pattern identification
3. **Cultural calibration check**: Are cultural filter protocols still appropriate for current classroom demographics?
4. **Prompt engineering update**: Based on glitch patterns, prompts are refined
5. **Human feedback integration**: Supervisor and teacher feedback incorporated into system updates
6. **Agent-100 authorization**: All updates to Drone Teacher behavior require Agent-100 sign-off before deployment

The Drone Teacher that does not improve quarterly is not a stable system. Improvement is mandatory. Stagnation is a failure mode.
