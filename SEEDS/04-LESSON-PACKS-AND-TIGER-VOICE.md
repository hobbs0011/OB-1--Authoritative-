## LESSON 001: "THE ART OF THE HARD NO" (Tiger Tier)

**Target**: High LTV / High Status students
**Theme**: Power Dynamics in English

### The Mechanic
Roleplay a text thread. Student must end a bad negotiation using "Bet" or "Draw a line" without being rude. Focus on "Tiger" tone.

### 10-5-5-3 Payload
- **Vocab**: leverage, non-negotiable, dealbreaker, counteroffer, bottom-line, walk-away, concession, stakeholder, equity, ROI
- **Idioms**: "Draw a line in the sand", "The ball is in your court", "Cut to the chase", "Burn bridges", "Play hardball"
- **Slang**: "Bet", "No cap", "That's a hard pass", "Dead serious", "Not my circus"
- **Games**: Negotiation Simulator, Tone Matcher, Boardroom Blitz
