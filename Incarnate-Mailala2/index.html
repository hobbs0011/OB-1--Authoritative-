Got Questions?

Everything you need to know before you drop in.

Where are you located?

175/16 Pham Ngu Lao, District 1, Ho Chi Minh City. 2 min walk from Bui Vien Walking Street.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in from 11:30 AM. Check-out by 1:00 PM. Late check-in? No problem — the rooftop bar is open late.

Do I need to book in advance?

For dorms, walk-ins are usually fine. For private rooms and weekends, book ahead via Hostelworld or WhatsApp.

Is the jerk chicken really that good?

Yes. Authentic Jamaican marinade, grilled fresh to order, 25–30 minutes. Pre-order via WhatsApp to skip the wait.

Can I host events at the rooftop?

Absolutely. Birthday parties, pub crawls, group bookings — message us on WhatsApp and we'll sort it.
