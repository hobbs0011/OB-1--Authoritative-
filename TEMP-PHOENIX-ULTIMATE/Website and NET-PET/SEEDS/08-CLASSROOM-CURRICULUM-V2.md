## The Weekly Lesson Structure

Every NET lesson follows the 10-5-5-3 physics. No deviations. Vocabulary delivery (10 words), idiom injection (5 idioms), slang contextualization (5 slang terms), and 3-game rotation. 60 minutes. Non-negotiable.

## Vocabulary Selection Criteria

Each word must pass four gates:
1. **Frequency Rank** — Top 3,000 most-used English words in business/daily conversation
2. **Contextual Utility** — Usable in 3+ real-life situations this week
3. **Emotional Resonance** — Words with weight (ambition, momentum, deadline)
4. **Cross-Tier Application** — Mouse can survive, Cub can practice, Tiger can deploy with precision

## Idiom Ranking System
Ranked by: Street Frequency + Professional Utility + Memorability

## Slang Tier — Contextual Gates
- **Street Slang** — Tiger use in peer contexts
- **Professional Slang** — Cub and Tiger deployment in corporate settings

## The 3-Game Rotation: Competition → Collaboration → Creation

## Tier Adaptation
| Tier | Complexity | Depth | Freedom |
|------|-----------|-------|---------|
| Mouse | Simplified, visual aids | Core words only | Structured prompts |
| Cub | Standard, some autonomy | Context + idioms | Semi-guided |
| Tiger | Full + self-moderation | All 10+5+5 | Creative freedom |

## Weekly Pacing — 4-Week Month
- Week 1: Foundation — introduce anchor vocabulary
- Week 2: Application — same vocab in new contexts
- Week 3: Stress — vocab under pressure (debates, roleplay)
- Week 4: Mastery — student-led review, Tiger-generated content
