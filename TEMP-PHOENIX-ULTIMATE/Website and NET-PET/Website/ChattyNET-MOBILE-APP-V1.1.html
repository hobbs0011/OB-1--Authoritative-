FAQ
What does NET PET actually track?
+
Your daily listening minutes, speaking minutes, and whether you’ve hit the 45/15 goal for the day. It also tracks your streak and mood state (neutral, tough love, proud).
What happens if I miss a day?
+
If you miss your goal, the next day NET PET resets your streak and switches into Tough Love mode. It doesn’t punish you – it just refuses to lie about your progress.
Do I have to log exact minutes?
+
No. The buttons are designed for quick logging. Each tap is a chunk of time: 15 minutes for listening, 5 minutes for speaking. You can tap multiple times to match your real study.
Is this a replacement for a teacher?
+
No. NET PET is the daily accountability partner. The real transformation comes from the combination of human teacher, NET lessons, and this AI coach watching your habits.
