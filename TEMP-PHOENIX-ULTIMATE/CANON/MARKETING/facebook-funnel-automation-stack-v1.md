## Context

Michael identified two tools on Facebook — "Magnus" (actually **Manus**) and **Meta AI** — and asked for deep research on whether these or any other tools can automate his 4-stage sales funnel for Natural English Training.

## Michael's 4-Stage Sales Funnel (Verbatim)

- **Stage 1: Free Evaluation** → level test, Jolly Phonics proficiency, proposed action plan (all free)
- **Stage 2: Free Report** → written report of findings from the evaluation
- **Stage 3: Delivered with Free Class** → report delivered alongside a free trial lesson
- **Stage 4: Close** → convert to paying student

### Voice Rules

- Kids AND adults
- Free evaluation hook
- Vietnamese for Lakeview groups, English for personal profile

## Tool Analysis

### 1. Manus (Meta's AI Agent) — FREE
- **What Michael saw as "Magnus"** — it's actually Manus, acquired by Meta
- **What it does:** AI agent for competitor analysis, content planning, ad creative generation, 30-day content calendars, can turn FB Page into landing page
- **Available on:** Meta Business Suite, WhatsApp Business (rolling out)
- **Cost:** Free with Meta ads
- **Funnel use:** Content creation and strategy layer. NOT a funnel automation engine.
- **Best for:** Creating ad creatives, analyzing competitor posts, planning content calendar
- **Limitation:** No automated conversation flows or lead nurturing sequences
- **Source:** https://manus.im/programs/meta/mkt, https://www.facebook.com/business/news/manus-joins-meta-accelerating-ai-innovation-for-businesses

### 2. Meta Business AI — FREE
- **What it does:** AI chatbot in Messenger and WhatsApp. Learns from page posts, ads, content. Auto-responds to customer inquiries 24/7.
- **Setup:** Meta Business Suite → Inbox → Business AI. No coding needed.
- **Cost:** Free with Facebook/Instagram ads
- **Funnel use:** Handles inbound DM questions automatically ("How much?", "Where?", "What age groups?")
- **Best for Stage 1:** Auto-responding to evaluation inquiries
- **Limitation:** Limited funnel logic — answers questions but doesn't guide through stages
- **Source:** https://www.facebook.com/business/ai/business-ai

### 3. ManyChat — FREE (500 contacts) / $15/mo Pro — 🏆 #1 PICK
- **What it does:** Full Messenger/IG/WhatsApp chatbot automation. Visual flow builder. Facebook Ads Trigger. Automated DM sequences. Payment collection.
- **Free plan:** Up to 500 contacts, core automation features
- **Pro plan:** $15/mo for 500 contacts, scales with list size
- **Funnel use:** THE primary automation engine for the 4-stage funnel
  - Stage 1: FB Ads Trigger → bot qualifies lead → books evaluation
  - Stage 2: After eval → automated message with report link
  - Stage 3: Automated booking for free trial class
  - Stage 4: Follow-up sequence after class → conversion push
- **Key feature:** Facebook Ads Trigger — someone clicks your ad → instant Messenger conversation
- **Source:** https://manychat.com/pricing, https://help.manychat.com/hc/en-us/articles/14281372007196-Facebook-Ads-Trigger

### 4. Chatfuel — $20/mo (no free plan as generous)
- **What it does:** Similar to ManyChat. 60+ templates. ChatGPT-powered smart replies. Visual flow builder.
- **Cost:** Starts $20/mo
- **Advantage over ManyChat:** Better keyword grouping, more templates
- **Disadvantage:** More expensive for multi-channel, less generous free tier
- **Verdict:** Good but ManyChat wins on value for NET's size

### 5. Meta Lead Ads (Instant Forms) — FREE (pay for ads) — 🏆 USE WITH ManyChat
- **What it does:** Native Facebook ad format with built-in forms. Collects name/phone/email without leaving Facebook.
- **Cost:** Free feature (you pay for the ad spend)
- **Funnel use Stage 1:** "Book Your Free Evaluation" form right in the ad
- **Key benefit:** Zero friction — lead never leaves Facebook
- **Source:** https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/ad-objectives/lead-generation

### 6. Systeme.io — FREE (3 funnels, 2000 contacts, unlimited emails) — 🏆 #1 for Landing Pages
- **What it does:** Full funnel builder + landing pages + email automation + course hosting
- **Free plan includes:** 3 sales funnels, 10 funnel steps, 2000 contacts, unlimited emails, 1 blog, 1 course, 1 automation rule
- **Funnel use:**
  - Build landing pages for each stage
  - Email nurture sequences
  - Stage 2 report delivery via automated email
  - Sales page for conversion
- **Source:** https://systeme.io/pricing

### 7. Make (formerly Integromat) — FREE tier
- **What it does:** Automation glue. Connects FB Lead Ads → CRM → email → calendar
- **Cost:** Free tier available
- **Funnel use:** Auto-sync leads from FB ads to your systems
- **Note:** Official Meta Business Partner
- **Source:** https://www.facebook.com/business/marketing-partners/make

### 8. Zapier — FREE (100 tasks/mo)
- **What it does:** Same concept as Make. Connects FB Lead Ads to spreadsheets, email, CRM.
- **Cost:** Free for 100 tasks/month
- **Funnel use:** Simpler alternative to Make for basic lead routing

## Recommended Free Stack

> **ManyChat (free)** + **Meta Lead Ads** + **Meta Business AI** + **Systeme.io (free)** + **Manus for content**

**Total cost: $0** until 500 contacts on ManyChat, then $15/mo. Everything else stays free.

### What this stack delivers:
- ✅ Automated lead capture on Facebook (Meta Lead Ads)
- ✅ Messenger chatbot qualifying and nurturing (ManyChat)
- ✅ Landing pages for each funnel stage (Systeme.io)
- ✅ Email automation for report delivery and follow-up (Systeme.io)
- ✅ AI auto-responses for general inquiries (Meta Business AI)
- ✅ AI content creation for posts and ads (Manus)

## Stage-by-Stage Implementation Map

### Stage 1 — Free Evaluation
1. **Meta Lead Ads** → Instant form: "Book Free Level Test" right in the FB ad
2. **ManyChat** → FB Ads Trigger catches clicks → automated Messenger flow qualifies → books eval
3. **Meta Business AI** → Handles DM questions about the eval
4. **Systeme.io** → Landing page backup for link-in-bio

### Stage 2 — Free Report
1. **ManyChat** → After eval, trigger: "Your report is ready!" with download link
2. **Systeme.io** → Host report as landing page or PDF, email automation delivers it

### Stage 3 — Free Class (delivered with report)
1. **ManyChat** → "Here's your report + your free trial class is booked for [date]"
2. **Systeme.io** → Email sequence confirms booking, sends reminder

### Stage 4 — Close
1. **ManyChat** → Follow-up sequence after free class: "How was your lesson? Ready to continue?"
2. **Systeme.io** → Sales page with pricing, automated email nurture for non-converters
