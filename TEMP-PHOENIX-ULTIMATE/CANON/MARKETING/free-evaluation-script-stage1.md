# 🎯 Free Evaluation Script — Stage 1

> **Purpose:** What Michael does during the free evaluation. This is the FIRST real interaction after the DM booking. Everything is free. No close attempt. Build trust, demonstrate expertise, let them experience the difference.
>
> **Voice:** Personal neighbour. You're helping a friend's kid, not running a corporate assessment center.
>
> **Duration:** 30-45 minutes (kids) / 20-30 minutes (adults)
>
> **Location:** Michael's teaching space in Lakeview City, or online via Zoom/Google Meet

---

## Before They Arrive (2 min prep)

- Review their DM conversation — what did they say about their goals?
- For kids: note the age, current school, any English exposure mentioned
- For adults: note their job, why they want English, any frustration points
- Have evaluation materials ready (see checklists below)
- **Mindset:** This person trusted you enough to show up. Respect that.

---

## Part 1: Welcome & Warm-Up (5 min)

### For Kids (with parent present)

**Vietnamese:**
> "Chào [tên phụ huynh], chào [tên bé]! Cảm ơn đã ghé qua. Con ngồi đây nè — thoải mái nhé, không có gì phải lo đâu."

**English (to parent):**
> "Thanks for coming by! I know it can feel weird bringing your kid to meet some random neighbour who teaches English — but honestly, this is just a chat. No pressure, no tests that matter. I just want to see where [child's name] is at so I can give you a real picture."

**To the kid (switch to simple English + Vietnamese mix):**
> "Hey [name]! Do you like games? / Con thích chơi game không?"

**Goal:** Kid relaxes. Parent sees you're human, not a robot teacher.

### For Adults

**Vietnamese:**
> "Chào [tên]! Cảm ơn đã dành thời gian. Thoải mái nhé — đây không phải bài kiểm tra. Mình chỉ muốn hiểu anh/chị đang ở đâu để mình có thể đưa ra lời khuyên thực tế."

**English:**
> "Thanks for coming! Relax — this isn't a test. I've been teaching in Lakeview for 2 years now and honestly the best evaluations are just conversations. Tell me about yourself — what do you do, and why are you thinking about English right now?"

**Goal:** They talk. You listen. Note their comfort level, vocabulary range, pronunciation patterns.

---

## Part 2: Level Assessment (10-15 min)

### For Kids (Age 4-7)

**Jolly Phonics Proficiency Check:**

| Check | What You Do | What You're Looking For |
|-------|------------|------------------------|
| Letter sounds | Show flashcards: s, a, t, i, p, n | Can they produce the sound? (not letter name) |
| Blending | Show: c-a-t, s-i-t, p-i-n | Can they blend 3 sounds into a word? |
| Segmenting | Say "dog" — ask them to break it apart | Can they hear individual sounds? |
| Letter formation | Give them a pencil, ask them to write their name | Fine motor + letter awareness |
| Tricky words | Show: the, I, he, she, we | Do they recognize any sight words? |
| Listening | Tell a simple story (3 sentences) | Do they understand basic English? |

**Record:** Which sounds they know, which they don't. This becomes the action plan.

**Vietnamese to parent (while kid works):**
> "Mình đang kiểm tra xem bé đã biết những âm nào rồi. Jolly Phonics dạy 42 âm — hầu hết trường ở VN chỉ dạy bảng chữ cái thôi, nên bé có thể biết chữ A là 'ây' nhưng chưa biết âm 'æ' trong 'cat'. Đây là lỗ hổng phổ biến nhất."

### For Kids (Age 8-12)

| Check | What You Do | What You're Looking For |
|-------|------------|------------------------|
| Reading aloud | Give a short paragraph (grade-appropriate) | Fluency, pronunciation, comprehension |
| Conversation | Ask about school, hobbies, friends | Spontaneous speech, grammar patterns |
| Writing | Write 3 sentences about their family | Spelling, sentence structure |
| Grammar spot-check | "Tell me what you did yesterday" | Past tense usage |
| Vocabulary | Picture descriptions (10 images) | Word range |
| Phonics gap check | Read nonsense words: flib, grop, snid | Can they decode unknown words? |

### For Adults

| Check | What You Do | What You're Looking For |
|-------|------------|------------------------|
| Free conversation | "Tell me about your typical day" (3 min) | Fluency, vocabulary, confidence level |
| Listening | Play a 1-min audio clip, ask 3 questions | Comprehension at natural speed |
| Reading | Short article (150 words), discuss | Reading speed, comprehension |
| Grammar probe | "Describe your dream vacation" + "Tell me about your last vacation" | Tense control |
| Pronunciation | Read 10 sentences with common VN problem sounds (th, r, l, ed endings) | Specific sound issues |
| Workplace English | Role-play: email reply, phone call, meeting intro | Functional English level |

**Vietnamese (during assessment):**
> "Tốt lắm! Anh/chị đang làm rất tốt. Mình chỉ muốn xem thêm một chút nữa thôi..."

**RULE:** Never make them feel stupid. Every response is "good, and here's what we can build on." Mouse to Tiger — they're the mouse right now. Your job is to make them feel safe.

---

## Part 3: Jolly Phonics Deep Check — Kids Only (5 min)

This is your **differentiator**. No other teacher in Lakeview does this.

**What you check:**
1. **Phase 1 sounds** (s, a, t, i, p, n) — foundation
2. **Phase 2 sounds** (ck, e, h, r, m, d) — building
3. **Phase 3 sounds** (g, o, u, l, f, b) — expanding
4. **Digraphs** (sh, ch, th, ai, ee, oa) — blending mastery
5. **Tricky words per phase** — sight word bank

**Record format (simple grid):**

```
Phase 1: ✅✅✅✅✅✅ (6/6)
Phase 2: ✅✅✅❌❌❌ (3/6)
Phase 3: ❌❌❌❌❌❌ (0/6)
Digraphs: not tested yet
Tricky words: the ✅ / I ✅ / he ❌ / she ❌
```

**Vietnamese to parent:**
> "Đây là kết quả — bé đã nắm được [X] âm trong tổng số 42 âm. Phần lớn trẻ ở VN bị thiếu phần [Y]. Đây không phải lỗi của bé — chỉ là chương trình ở trường chưa dạy đến. Mình có thể lấp lỗ hổng này trong [Z] tuần."

---

## Part 4: Action Plan Presentation (5-10 min)

This is where you deliver VALUE. They leave with a clear picture — even if they never come back.

### For Kids

**Vietnamese:**
> "Dựa trên kết quả, đây là tình hình của [tên bé]:"

**Present the 3-part action plan:**

1. **Hiện tại bé đang ở đâu (Where they are)**
   - "Bé biết [X] âm trong 42 âm Jolly Phonics"
   - "Bé có thể / chưa thể blend (ghép âm) để đọc"
   - "Vocabulary range: khoảng [X] từ"

2. **Cần làm gì (What needs to happen)**
   - "Lấp lỗ hổng phonics Phase [X] đến Phase [Y]"
   - "Tăng vocabulary qua stories và games"
   - "Build confidence để bé dám nói — đây là phần quan trọng nhất"

3. **Lộ trình đề xuất (Proposed path)**
   - "Nếu học [X] buổi/tuần, mình ước tính bé sẽ hoàn thành 42 âm trong [Y] tuần"
   - "Sau đó chuyển sang reading fluency và conversation"
   - "Mình sẽ gửi chi tiết trong báo cáo"

### For Adults

**Vietnamese:**
> "Dựa trên buổi nói chuyện, đây là đánh giá của mình:"

1. **Current level:** "Anh/chị đang ở khoảng [A2/B1/B2] theo khung châu Âu"
2. **Key gaps:** "Pronunciation: [specific sounds]. Grammar: [specific patterns]. Vocabulary: [specific areas]"
3. **Proposed path:** "[X] buổi/tuần, tập trung vào [specific skills] trước. Mình sẽ viết chi tiết trong báo cáo."

### The Key Line (Vietnamese)

> "Mình sẽ gửi cho anh/chị một báo cáo chi tiết trong vài ngày tới — hoàn toàn miễn phí. Trong đó sẽ có đầy đủ kết quả đánh giá và lộ trình đề xuất. Không có gì phải quyết định hôm nay cả."

### The Key Line (English)

> "I'll send you a detailed report in the next few days — completely free. It'll have everything from today plus a proposed learning path. No need to decide anything today."

---

## Part 5: Close the Evaluation (2 min)

**DO NOT sell. DO NOT mention pricing. DO NOT push.**

**Vietnamese:**
> "Cảm ơn đã dành thời gian! [Tên bé] rất giỏi — mình thấy tiềm năng rất lớn. Mình sẽ gửi báo cáo sớm nhé. Có gì thắc mắc cứ nhắn mình."

**English:**
> "Thanks for coming by! [Name] did great — I can see real potential. I'll get that report to you soon. Any questions in the meantime, just message me."

**Walk them out like a neighbour, not a salesperson.**

---

## After They Leave (5 min)

1. **Write down notes immediately** — don't trust your memory
2. **Fill in the report template** (Stage 2 artifact) — do it while it's fresh
3. **Log the lead** — name, date, kid/adult, level, next step
4. **Schedule the report delivery** — aim for 2-3 days (builds anticipation)

## Voice Reminders

| ✅ DO | ❌ DON'T |
|-------|----------|
| "Bé làm tốt lắm!" | "Bé cần cải thiện nhiều" |
| "This is a really common gap" | "You should have learned this already" |
| "I see real potential here" | "You're at a low level" |
| "Let me show you something cool" | "Now we'll test your grammar" |
| "No rush — take your time" | "We need to move faster" |
| Be the neighbour | Be the institution |
