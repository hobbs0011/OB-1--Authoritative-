## Purpose

VAULT-NEXUS is the cross-vault integration and handshake protocols vault. Every handshake between vaults, every cross-vault dependency, every multi-model handoff, every integration protocol lives here. Nexus does not store data. Nexus does not make decisions. **Nexus moves data and routes decisions between the 9 vaults of Phoenix.**

Nexus is the connective tissue of the Phoenix system. Without Nexus, each vault is an island. With Nexus, the nine vaults form one sovereign intelligence network. Every integration is logged. Every handshake is verified. **No silent integrations. No undocumented dependencies.**
