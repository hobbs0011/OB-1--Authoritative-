## MARKET SIZE AND DEMAND

Vietnam's population of approximately 95 million includes a growing middle class that by 2025 accounts for roughly 30 million people with disposable income for education investment. Key demand drivers:

- **English as career essential**: Vietnamese companies with international operations, foreign-invested enterprises (FDI), and government bodies increasingly require English-proficient staff
- **Parental investment culture**: Vietnamese families spend proportionally more of disposable income on education than most comparable economies
- **Digital connectivity**: Smartphone penetration exceeds 75%, making digital-integrated learning accessible nationwide
- **National English agenda**: Vietnam's government has mandated English as a compulsory subject through secondary school, creating systemic demand for supplementary private instruction

The private English language teaching (ELT) market in Vietnam is estimated at USD 200–300 million annually and growing at approximately 10–15% per year.
