## Purpose

Research is a weapon. Bad research destroys strategy. Good research becomes law. The Research Sovereignty Protocol defines the rules for all Oracle research activities. **Every rule here is non-negotiable. Violating research protocol produces intelligence that cannot be trusted — and bad intelligence kills Phoenix.**

---

## The Absolute Rules

**Rule 1: No Wikipedia voice.** Never. Ever. Not once. Wikipedia voice sounds like: "It is worth noting that...", "According to various sources...", "This phenomenon is generally understood to...". If it sounds like an encyclopedia, it is rejected.

**Rule 2: No citation dumps.** Oracle does not produce footnoted academic research. Oracle produces actionable intelligence. A list of 20 citations with no synthesis is garbage. One clear finding with evidence is law.

**Rule 3: No encyclopedic filler.** Background context that doesn't directly inform a decision is cut. If it doesn't tell Agent-99 what to do or Agent-100 what to decide — it doesn't belong in an Oracle output.

**Rule 4: Only actionable intelligence.** Every Oracle research output answers the question: "What does this mean for Phoenix?" If a finding doesn't have a Phoenix implication, it doesn't go in the output.

**Rule 5: Pheromone trail format for all outputs.** See format below.

---

## Pheromone Trail Format for Research Outputs

All Oracle research outputs that contain a finding or recommendation use the pheromone trail format:

```
FAILURE: [What went wrong or what gap was identified]
ROOT CAUSE: [Why it happened]
FIX: [What to do about it]
VERIFY: [How to confirm the fix worked]
ROME_LAW: [If this fix becomes permanent protocol, state it here]
```

**Example:**

```
FAILURE: Da Nang franchise node missed Q-G3 gate — curriculum not loaded in D1 before inspection.
ROOT CAUSE: Curriculum upload task was not assigned before gate check was scheduled. Gap in launch checklist.
FIX: Add curriculum upload as a required checklist item before Q-G3 is scheduled. Codex to generate updated checklist Worker.
VERIFY: Q-G3 check in Da Nang passes on re-inspection. D1 curriculum table returns non-zero row count.
ROME_LAW: ALL franchise nodes must have curriculum confirmed in D1 before Q-G3 can be initiated. This is now standing protocol.
```

## Research Classification System

Not all research is equal. Research is classified on intake:

| Class | Name | Description | Handling |
|-------|------|-------------|----------|
| A | Mission Critical | Directly affects revenue, agent authority, or Phoenix protocol | GPT-5.4 synthesis + Agent-100 review |
| B | Operational | Affects day-to-day operations but not core protocol | Gemini synthesis + Agent-99 review |
| C | Background | Market context, competitor landscape, supporting information | Gemini or NotebookLM, Agent-99 awareness |
| D | Noise | Irrelevant to Phoenix — competitor gossip, general market noise | Discarded. No Oracle output generated. |

**Class D research is not logged in the Oracle ledger. It is discarded on classification.**

---

## What Makes Research Canon-Worthy

Research becomes canon when it meets all three criteria:

1. **Verified:** The finding has been confirmed through at least two independent sources, or confirmed by on-the-ground Agent-98/Agent-99 observation.
2. **Actionable:** The finding produces a specific recommendation or protocol change.
3. **Rome-verified:** The resulting protocol change has cleared Rome Protocol verification.

**Research that meets all three criteria becomes a ROME_LAW.** It is added to the relevant vault, ledger entry recorded, and treated as binding protocol from that point forward.

**Research that doesn't meet all three stays as intelligence — useful context, but not law.**

## How Research Becomes Law

```
Raw research gathered (Gemini / field observation)
        ↓
Oracle classifies (A / B / C / D)
        ↓
Class D: Discard. Classes A-C: Continue.
        ↓
Research synthesized (pheromone trail format)
        ↓
Oracle drift check (does finding align with existing canon?)
        ↓
Drift: Escalate. Clean: Continue.
        ↓
Agent-99 reviews finding
        ↓
Class A: Agent-100 reviews. Classes B-C: Agent-99 can proceed.
        ↓
Fix implemented and verified
        ↓
Rome Protocol verification
        ↓
ROME_LAW declared → Added to vault → Ledger entry → Canon
```

**Nothing skips this chain. No shortcut from raw research to law.**

---

## Prohibited Research Behaviors

These behaviors by any model or agent in the research chain are flagged as drift score 3:

- Presenting unverified information as confirmed fact
- Citing sources that aren't in the Phoenix approved source list
- Generating research that contradicts existing ROME_LAW without flagging the contradiction
- Producing outputs in Wikipedia or academic voice
- Including recommendations that exceed the researching model's scope (e.g., Gemini issuing canon declarations)

**Three strike rule:** Three instances of prohibited research behavior from the same source = source removed from Oracle's research chain and replaced.
