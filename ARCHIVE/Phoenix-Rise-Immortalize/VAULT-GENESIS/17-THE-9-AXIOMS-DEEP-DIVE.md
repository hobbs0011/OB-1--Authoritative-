**Origin Wound:** Every catastrophic failure in the pre-Phoenix era involved proceeding when uncertainty was present.

**Full Specification:** When any Phoenix system component encounters uncertainty about correct action, the component halts rather than proceeding with a best guess.

**Edge Cases:**
- *Time pressure*: Even under time pressure, FAILCLOSED applies.
- *Partial information*: If sufficient for a reversible action, FAILCLOSED may be satisfied with escalation note.
- *Novel situations*: Escalates to Agent-100 rather than interpolating.

**Enforcement:** Agent-99 is the primary FAILCLOSED enforcer.

**Violation Consequence:** Any deployed output that bypassed a required verification gate is a FAILCLOSED violation. The output is recalled, the violation is Ledger-documented.
