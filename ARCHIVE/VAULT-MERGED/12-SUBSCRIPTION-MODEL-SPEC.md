## Why Pricing Is Locked

Price drift destroys brand trust. Negotiation signals that the price was never real. NET's prices are round, confident, and final — the same way a Tiger speaks. Discounts do not exist. Scholarship exceptions require Michael's direct approval and are never advertised.

- **Zalo Pay** — primary for Vietnamese individual students
- **Bank Transfer** — VietcomBank, Techcombank accepted

All payments monthly in advance. No pay-later. No installments.

---

## Refund Policy

No refunds. Students are pre-qualified via TMST intake assessment. If a student is enrolled, they were evaluated and accepted. The assessment process replaces the refund process — bad fits are filtered before payment, not after.

---

## Enterprise Package — BlueTech Structure

Corporate clients receive:
- Dedicated cohort of 10-20 employees
- Custom scheduling (lunch sessions, after-work)
- Monthly HR reporting with tier progression data
- CHATTY PRIME configured for corporate vocabulary (meetings, emails, presentations)
- Quarterly review with Michael or senior teacher

---

## Pricing Psychology

2M, 3M, 5M — not 1.9M, 2.9M, 4.9M. Round numbers signal confidence. Charm pricing (ending in 9) signals discount retail. NET is not discount retail. The price is what it is because the product is what it is.

---

## Annual Payment Incentive

Students who pay 12 months upfront receive one free month (Month 13). No other discount. Annual payment is presented as a loyalty reward, not a price reduction.
