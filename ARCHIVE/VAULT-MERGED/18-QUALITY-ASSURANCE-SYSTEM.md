## The Lesson Quality Rubric

Every NET lesson is scored out of 100 across five weighted dimensions:

- **10-5-5-3 Compliance** (30 pts): Were all 10 vocabulary items, 5 idioms, 5 slang terms, and 3 games delivered?
- **Student Engagement Score** (25 pts): Measured by participation frequency, response latency, and voluntary contribution rate.
- **Teacher/AI Behavior Score** (20 pts): Stealth correction applied correctly, emotional translation active, tribal energy maintained.
- **Learning Outcome Clarity** (15 pts): Did students demonstrate usage of at least 60% of target items by lesson end?
- **Student Sentiment Index** (10 pts): Post-lesson micro-survey — 3 questions, 30 seconds, logged automatically.

**Passing score: 72/100.** Below this threshold triggers remediation.

---

## The Post-Lesson Audit

Within 2 hours of lesson close, SyncCore runs the automated audit:

1. **10-5-5-3 Compliance Check** — AI cross-references lesson transcript against the content blueprint. Each missing item flagged.
2. **Engagement Signal Review** — Participation timestamps, silence gaps, and crosstalk events logged.
3. **Teacher Behavior Scan** — AI flags any direct correction events (as opposed to stealth correction) and rates emotional tone.
4. **AI Performance Review** (AI-delivered lessons only) — CHATTY PRIME response accuracy, pacing, and recovery from student confusion.

---

## AI-Powered 10-5-5-3 Compliance Checking

CHATTY PRIME maintains a live compliance ticker during every lesson. Each target item is marked DELIVERED only when a student uses it correctly in context — not when the teacher introduces it. This prevents delivery-without-absorption drift.

---

## Student Feedback as Drift Signal

Feedback patterns that indicate quality problems:
- Three or more students rate "too fast" in the same week → pacing drift.
- Sentiment score drops two weeks consecutively → content fatigue or teacher energy collapse.
- Zero voluntary contributions in a session → engagement system failure.

## Remediation Protocol

When a lesson scores below 72:

1. **Flag issued** — Teacher and SyncCore alerted within 24 hours.
2. **Root cause analysis** — Which rubric dimension failed and why?
3. **Targeted re-training** — Specific corrective brief issued (not generic feedback).
4. **Re-audit** — Next lesson monitored at elevated scrutiny.
5. **Escalation** — Three consecutive failures in one month triggers Michael review.

---

## QA: AI vs. Human-Delivered Lessons

Human-delivered lessons are audited via transcript and teacher self-report. AI-delivered lessons (CHATTY PRIME) are audited in full — every exchange logged, every response scored. AI QA is stricter: a score below 80 triggers an immediate model behavior review.

---

## The Weekly Quality Report

Delivered every Monday to Michael:
- Overall fleet QA average
- Lowest-scoring lesson with root cause
- Highest-scoring lesson (celebrate wins)
- Drift signals detected and remediation status
- Content items with lowest student usage rates (candidates for replacement)

---

## The QA Loop

QA findings feed directly into the Content Factory. Consistently low-scoring vocabulary items are retired. Games with low engagement are redesigned. Idiom sets that produce confusion get simplified. The system is self-correcting — every failure is a curriculum improvement trigger.
