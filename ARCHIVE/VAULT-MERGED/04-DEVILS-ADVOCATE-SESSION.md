## ANT / PHEROMONE ANALOGY

1. An ant (drone) finds something valuable (successful conversion, solved bug)
2. It walks back following the trail it left (pheromones = audit data)

4. Other ants from the colony pick up that line and follow it
5. Each ant that follows makes it stronger
