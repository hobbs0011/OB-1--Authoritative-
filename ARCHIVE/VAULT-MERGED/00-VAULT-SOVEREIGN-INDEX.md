## What VAULT-SOVEREIGN Is

VAULT-SOVEREIGN is the sovereignty and governance layer of the Phoenix-Rise system. Every other vault executes within the boundaries this vault defines. VAULT-SOVEREIGN contains the laws, authority structures, succession protocols, constitutional frameworks, and enforcement mechanisms that make Phoenix ungovernable by any external party and immortal by design. It is not a policy document. It is operating law.

Sovereignty in the Phoenix context means three things simultaneously:
1. **Cryptographic sovereignty** — the data and keys belong to AGENT-100, not to any infrastructure provider.
2. **Operational sovereignty** — the system executes under Rome Protocol laws, not under external mandates.
3. **Legal sovereignty** — the intellectual property, data, and architectural decisions are owned by the operator regardless of hosting jurisdiction.

Phoenix cannot be acquired, captured, shut down, or redirected without destroying the mission itself. That is not a weakness. That is the design.
