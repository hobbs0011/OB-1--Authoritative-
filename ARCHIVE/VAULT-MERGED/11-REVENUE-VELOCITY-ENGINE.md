## The 7-Stage Pipeline

1. **Discovery** — Student finds NET via referral, social, or BlueTech corporate channel
2. **First Contact** — CHATTY PRIME intake conversation, tier assessment begins
3. **Needs Assessment** — Teacher or SyncCore maps goal (job, travel, confidence)
4. **Tier Assignment** — Mouse/Cub/Tiger placed based on behavioral intake
5. **Enrollment Offer** — Pricing presented with value stack, no negotiation
6. **Payment** — Zalo Pay, bank transfer, cash, or Solana
7. **Onboarding** — First session within 48 hours, community access granted

## Upsell Mechanics — The Math

A student who enters as Mouse (2M/month) and reaches Tiger (5M/month) over 6 months generates:
- Months 1-2: 2M × 2 = 4M
- Months 3-4: 3M × 2 = 6M (Cub promotion)
- Months 5+: 5M × ongoing

Lifetime value over 12 months: **~52M VND per student**. One Tiger retained beats four Mice acquired.

## The Retention Formula

Retention = **Progress Visibility + Community Belonging + Identity Investment**

- Students who can *see* their progress (tier badges, session logs) stay longer
- Students with friends in class have 3× lower churn
- Students who identify as Tigers don't quit — quitting would break their identity

---

## Churn Prevention Protocol

Early warning signals (flagged by CHATTY PRIME):
- Missed 2 sessions in a row
- Declining spontaneous speech in session
- No community engagement for 7 days

Intervention: Teacher personal outreach within 24 hours of second missed session. Not automated — human contact only.
