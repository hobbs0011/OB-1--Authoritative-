## The Monthly Assessment Format

Every student undergoes a monthly assessment. Duration: 20 minutes. Administrator: CHATTY PRIME (AI-delivered) with teacher observation. The assessment is embedded inside a game session — students do not know they are being formally assessed. This is the stealth assessment technique.

Structure: Three game rounds designed specifically to elicit target behaviors across all five competencies. No written tests. No anxiety triggers. Just play — with precision measurement underneath.

---

## The Five Competencies

1. **Vocabulary Retention Rate** — Percentage of target vocabulary from the past month used correctly without prompting during the assessment session.
2. **Idiom Usage Frequency** — Number of idioms used naturally across the 20-minute session.
3. **Speaking Fluency Score** — Average response latency and sentence completion rate (AI-measured in real time).
4. **Pronunciation Accuracy** — Flagged mispronunciations per 100 words spoken (AI phoneme analysis).
5. **Confidence Index** — Behavioral observable: voluntary initiation rate, recovery speed after errors, eye contact maintenance (teacher-rated 1–10).

---

## Stealth Assessment Technique

Students are told: "Today we're playing the Champion Round." The games are identical in format to regular sessions but the content is drawn from the past 30 days of lessons. Students compete, laugh, and perform — while every utterance is logged, scored, and stored. Assessment without dread.

## Student Progress Report

One page. Visual format. Delivered digitally within 48 hours of assessment.

Contents:
- Tiger Meter trajectory graph (monthly scores connected).
- Five competency radar chart (visual strengths and gaps).
- Three wins highlighted in green.
- One growth edge identified (framed as opportunity, not failure).
- Current tier badge displayed prominently.

---

## Parent Progress Report

Executive summary format — parents are busy:
- **Overall Progress %** — Single number representing weighted competency average vs. last month.
- **Next Milestone** — What the student needs to achieve for tier advancement.
- **Recommended Action** — One specific parent behavior that accelerates progress.

## Data Privacy in Student Records

What is stored: session transcripts (anonymized for AI training), competency scores, Tiger Meter scores, point totals, badge history, parent contact log. What is never stored: full name + contact + score in a single unsecured file. The Ledger is access-controlled: Michael (full access), teachers (own students only), parents (their child only).

---

## The Assessment Feedback Loop

Assessment results feed directly into the lesson plan for the following month. If 60% of a class scores below threshold on idiom usage, idiom density increases in next month's content. If pronunciation accuracy is consistently low on specific phonemes, CHATTY PRIME adds targeted pronunciation drills. The system is self-correcting.

---

## Comparative Benchmarking

Students see how they compare to peers at the same tier — anonymously. "You are in the top 30% of Cubs this month." This is motivational data, not shaming data. Percentile framing drives ambition without destroying confidence.

---

## The Annual Learning Trajectory Report

At the 12-month mark, every student receives a full-year arc report: where they started, where they are, how far they've traveled. This report is the most powerful retention and referral tool NET produces. Parents who see their child's year-in-review almost always re-enroll.
