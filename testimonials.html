16 years old. Parent-forced enrollment. Slouching, no eye contact. "This is boring" at minute 7.

Spoke for 4 consecutive minutes explaining the TikTok algorithm — in English. First voluntary sentence of the entire enrollment.

The Corporate Climber

Junior accountant, 22. Whispered when unsure. Froze completely on "What is your greatest weakness?"

Completed a 90-second mock interview answer. Unprompted. Volume: conversational. First time she laughed mid-answer.

Sales manager, 28. Tiger level. Confident but had vocabulary gaps under pressure. Defaulted to Vietnamese fillers in high-stakes moments.

Completed a 5-minute negotiation simulation with zero vocabulary prompts. First correct use of "walk away position" in context.
