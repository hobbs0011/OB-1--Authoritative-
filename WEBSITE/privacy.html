Privacy Policy

How Natural English Training handles your data

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

Natural English Training collects only the information you voluntarily provide through our assessment forms, classroom tools, and communication channels. This may include your name, age, school or workplace, English learning history, audio recordings, and written responses.

How we use it

Your data is used solely to provide English assessment, personalised feedback, and follow-up communication from Michael Hobbs or authorised NET staff. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Audio and media

Audio recordings made during the Tiger Assessment remain on your device until you choose to download and submit them. Any media you submit is reviewed only by Michael Hobbs for assessment purposes and is not used for any other purpose without your explicit consent.

Data retention

Assessment submissions are retained for the duration of the student relationship and for a reasonable period thereafter for reference. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting hobbs@naturalenglishtraining.com.

Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your data. The assessment runs primarily in your browser. Submitted data is transmitted directly to NET's secure channels and is not stored on third-party servers beyond necessary processing.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please email hobbs@naturalenglishtraining.com.
