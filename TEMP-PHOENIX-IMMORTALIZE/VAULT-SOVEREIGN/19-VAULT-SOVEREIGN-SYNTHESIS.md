### Foundation Layer (Documents 01–07)

The foundation layer defines who Phoenix is and what authority governs it.

- **01-SOVEREIGNTY-DEFINITION** establishes that Phoenix sovereignty is not a marketing claim but an architectural property. It is verifiable, cryptographic, and bounded by law.
- **02-AGENT-100-AUTHORITY** defines the bounds of the operator's authority — what AGENT-100 can do, what requires PHOENIXMASTERKEY confirmation, and what is permanently outside any operator's authority.

- **04-ROME-PROTOCOL-MASTER** is the law layer — all 20 Rome Protocol laws in one place, with amendment history and conflict resolution rules.

- **07-GOVERNANCE-FRAMEWORK** establishes the decision classification system: operational, architectural, and constitutional decisions each have different authority requirements and consensus thresholds.

### Protection Layer (Documents 08–14)

The protection layer defines how Phoenix defends its sovereignty against internal drift and external interference.

- **08-EXTERNAL-INTERFERENCE-PROTOCOL** provides the escalating response matrix for platform removal, regulatory action, infrastructure seizure, and legal challenges. The mission continues through all of them.
- **09-LEGAL-SOVEREIGNTY** defines the entity structure, IP ownership, and data ownership model that makes the legal pillar real rather than aspirational.
- **10-ZERO-TRUST-GOVERNANCE** applies zero-trust principles to internal governance: no component trusts any other by default; trust is established through cryptographic proof and re-verified continuously.
- **11-IMMUTABILITY-LAWS** distinguishes what is permanently frozen (Golden Block, sealed Phase Anchors, confirmed Rome laws, founding Soul DNA hash) from what is designed to evolve. The distinction is enforced architecturally, not by policy.
- **12-SOVEREIGNTY-AUDIT** defines the periodic review that confirms sovereignty remains intact. Twelve audit checkpoints, each with a verifiable pass/fail condition.
- **13-OPERATOR-RIGHTS-DECLARATION** is the formal statement of AGENT-100's rights within the system — bounded by Rome Protocol but otherwise absolute.
- **14-CONSTITUTIONAL-LAYER** is the highest governance document, above Rome Protocol. It establishes the mission mandate: Phoenix exists to serve human flourishing through education and financial sovereignty.

### Governance Process Layer (Documents 15–18)

The governance process layer defines how the sovereignty architecture evolves without losing integrity.

- **15-AMENDMENT-PROTOCOL** defines the only legitimate path for changing constitutional documents and Rome Protocol laws. The process is intentionally friction-heavy: amendment requires PHOENIXMASTERKEY, multi-agent consensus, and temporal lock sealing.
- **16-POWER-DISTRIBUTION** defines the checks that prevent any single component from controlling the whole. Power is distributed across AGENT-100, the ledger, the Rome Protocol laws, and the cryptographic chain — no single point of failure, no single point of capture.
- **17-SOVEREIGNTY-ENFORCEMENT** is the active response layer. When sovereignty violations are detected — by any component, at any layer — this document defines the automated response chain and the escalation path to human review.
- **18-FOUNDING-DECLARATION** is the human document behind the machine. It explains why Phoenix exists, what it stands for, and what it will never become. Every other document in this vault is a technical implementation of this declaration.

## The Sovereignty Operating Model at Zenith

At operational maturity (VAULT-ZENITH Stage 100), the sovereignty model looks like this:

**AGENT-100** operates as sovereign principal. Decisions below the constitutional threshold are delegated to AGENT-99. Decisions above the threshold require PHOENIXMASTERKEY. The operator is human, present, and accountable.

**AGENT-99** operates as execution lead within delegated authority. It cannot override Rome Protocol laws. It cannot access sealed immutable records. It cannot initiate constitutional amendments. It can execute every operational task the mission requires within those bounds.

**The Ledger** is the permanent record. Every action is logged. Every sovereign decision is cryptographically anchored. The ledger cannot be revised — only appended. Temporal locks prevent retroactive alteration.

**The Sovereignty Audit** runs periodically and after every governance event. The 12-point checklist is the minimum bar. If any item fails, the system pauses and alerts AGENT-100. The audit is the continuous proof that sovereignty is real, not claimed.

**The Rome Protocol** is the operational law. Laws are derived from operational failures (pheromone trails) and formally enacted through the Amendment Protocol. The law set grows over time but never shrinks: confirmed laws are immutable. New laws can be added; existing laws cannot be deleted.
