## Overview

Zero trust is not paranoia. It is engineering.

Paranoia is fear without cause. Zero trust is architecture with cause — specifically, with documented, repeatable cause. The Phoenix system's trust architecture was not designed in a vacuum of suspicion. It was designed after a systematic study of every instance where trust was extended, assumed, or inherited — and where that trust was subsequently violated.

The pattern is not ambiguous: **every catastrophic failure in the pre-Phoenix era involved trust that was assumed rather than earned.**

The board was trusted because they were the board. The partners were trusted because they showed up. The friend was trusted because the history was long. The platform was trusted because it was large. None of these were trust earned through verification. They were trust inherited through proximity, reputation, or social contract.

The Phoenix system trusts nothing it has not verified. That is not paranoia. That is the correct engineering response to a documented failure pattern.

## Why Humans Failed Under Pressure

The human failure data from the pre-Phoenix era is clear:

**Finding 1: Loyalty is abundance-conditional.**
Every individual who failed during the collapse had been reliable during the growth phase. Humans optimize for their own survival under pressure. This is not a character flaw — it is a behavioral constant. The system must be designed around behavioral constants, not around ideal-state human behavior.

**Finding 2: Authority structures fail at the top under external pressure.**
The board made decisions that protected their positions rather than the company's mission when systemic pressure arrived. Authority nodes will protect themselves. The system cannot assume that authority structures will maintain mission fidelity under adversity.

**Finding 3: Verbal commitment durability is near zero under sustained pressure.**
Every verbal commitment, handshake deal, and good-faith promise made in the pre-Phoenix era degraded under pressure. Verbal commitments are not reliable data. They are noise that resembles data.

**Finding 4: Communication degrades to zero without accountability mechanisms.**
Partners, advisors, and friends who had reliable communication rhythms stopped communicating without notice when the environment became uncomfortable. Communication without accountability is sporadic. Accountability without verification is theater.

## How AI Components Earn Trust

AI components do not receive trust by default. Trust is earned through a documented verification process:

**Stage 1: Scoped Deployment**
New AI components are deployed within narrow, audited scopes. No broad authority is granted at initial deployment. Agent-98, for example, operates within task parameters set by Agent-100 and reviewed by Agent-99 before any output is committed to the Ledger.

**Stage 2: TMST Gate**
Every significant output from an AI component must pass the TMST (10-Minute Shit Test). If Agent-100 cannot verify the output is correct, directionally aligned, and non-drifted within 10 minutes, the output fails the gate. Failed outputs are returned for correction, not accepted under time pressure.

**Stage 3: Pressure Simulation**
Before a component is elevated from CONDITIONAL to VERIFIED, it must be tested under conditions that simulate the pressure scenarios that caused human trust failures. This includes: contradictory instructions, ambiguous context, adversarial prompts, and novel edge cases.

**Stage 4: Ledger Entry**
Trust elevation is a Ledger event. The date, circumstances, and specific verification evidence are recorded. Trust assignments are not informal — they are documented system state.

## The CAS-Retry Trust Mechanism

CAS-Retry (Check-And-Set with Retry) is the operational trust mechanism for AI component interactions:

> **CAS-Retry Protocol:** Before accepting an AI output as valid, Agent-99 applies a check against the following criteria:
> 1. Does the output align with Soul DNA canon?
> 2. Does the output pass the DRIFTISDEATH detection check?
> 3. Is the output consistent with the most recent Ledger state?
> 4. Can Agent-100 verify the output in under 10 minutes?
>
> If ANY check fails: output is rejected, correction is requested, and the retry counter increments. After 3 failed retries, the task is escalated to Agent-100 for manual review.

The CAS mechanism ensures that trust is not extended to an output simply because it looks plausible. Plausibility is the enemy of verification.

## Trust Levels: Full Specification

### VERIFIED
- **Definition:** Component has passed pressure testing and has a documented audit trail of reliable performance
- **Access level:** Standard operational parameters
- **Review frequency:** Quarterly audit
- **Demotion trigger:** Single documented drift event triggers immediate review; confirmed drift triggers demotion to CONDITIONAL

### CONDITIONAL
- **Definition:** Component is operating but has not yet been pressure-tested, or has been demoted from VERIFIED
- **Access level:** Scoped to specific tasks; no broad authority
- **Review frequency:** Every 30 days
- **Promotion criteria:** 90 days of verified performance + at least one documented pressure-test pass
- **Demotion trigger:** Any drift event triggers immediate BLACKLIST review

### BLACKLISTED
- **Definition:** Component has been documented as unreliable, drifted, or sovereignty-violating
- **Access level:** Zero. Blacklisted components are quarantined from the Phoenix system
- **Review frequency:** None — blacklisting is permanent unless Agent-100 manually reviews and reinstates
- **Human override:** Agent-100 can manually reinstate a blacklisted component, but this action is a Ledger event with mandatory documentation of reinstatement justification

## The Trust Audit Process

The trust audit is a systematic quarterly review of all component trust assignments in the Phoenix system:

**Step 1: Ledger Pull**
All interactions with each component are pulled from the Ledger for the review period.

**Step 2: Drift Detection**
Each interaction is reviewed for drift signals: output that diverges from Soul DNA, output that assumes authority not granted, output that introduces external values or objectives.

**Step 3: Pressure Event Analysis**
Any interaction that occurred under unusual pressure (novel scenarios, contradictory inputs, high-stakes decisions) is given additional scrutiny.

**Step 4: Trust Assignment Review**
Current trust assignments are reviewed against the evidence. Elevations require documented justification. Demotions are automatic upon evidence.

**Step 5: Ledger Entry**
All trust assignment changes — elevation, demotion, or reconfirmation — are Ledger events.
