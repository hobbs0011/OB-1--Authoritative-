## The Human-AI Teaching Partnership Model

NET does not replace teachers with AI. It divides labor precisely. AI handles volume, consistency, and pattern. Humans handle culture, emotion, and escalation. This division is permanent — neither side crosses into the other's lane without explicit authorization.

AI does not improvise with culture. Teachers do not repeat vocabulary drills manually. Each does what it does best.

## The Teacher Quality Gate — TMST for Teachers

Before any teacher delivers a NET lesson, they must pass the **Teacher Michael Standard Test**. Five core questions:

1. What is the 10-5-5-3 formula and why does each number exist?
2. Define Mouse, Cub, and Tiger with three behavioral markers each.
3. Demonstrate Stealth Correction on this student error: *"Yesterday I go to the market."*
4. How do you handle a student who refuses to speak in front of peers?
5. What is Tiger Voice and how do you activate it in a Cub student?

A score below 5/5 means the teacher does not deploy. No exceptions.

## Teacher Training Curriculum — 3 Phases

**Phase 1 — Foundation (Day 1-2)**
Read all VAULT-NET-SEEDS docs. Study Michael Method. Shadow one live lesson via recording. Complete TMST.

**Phase 2 — Supervised Delivery (Day 3-5)**
Deliver one full lesson with Michael or senior teacher observing. AI logs all student interactions. Debrief follows every session.

**Phase 3 — Independent Deployment (Day 6+)**
Teacher runs solo. CHATTY PRIME co-facilitates. Weekly performance review.

## Deploy a New Teacher in Under 1 Hour

1. Share VAULT-NET-SEEDS access (10 min read)
2. Watch the Michael Method orientation video (15 min)
3. Administer TMST verbally (10 min)
4. Configure CHATTY PRIME access with teacher credentials (5 min)
5. Brief on current student roster: tier, name, known triggers (10 min)
6. First supervised session scheduled (calendar locked)

Total: 50 minutes. Teacher is live.

## Performance Monitoring System

Teachers are tracked on three metrics, reviewed weekly:

- **Student Tier Progression Rate** — are students moving from Mouse to Tiger?
- **Retention Rate** — are students showing up consistently?
- **Tiger Voice Activation Rate** — how often do students volunteer speech without prompting?

Metrics are pulled from CHATTY PRIME session logs and teacher self-reports. Reviewed every Monday.

---

## Drift Detection

A teacher is drifting from the Michael Method when:

- They skip the game rotation (replace games with worksheets)
- They correct students publicly without stealth technique
- They allow Blue-tier language (formal/textbook English prioritized over contextual fluency)
- They shortcut vocabulary to fewer than 10 words
- Student self-initiation rate drops below 30% per session

---

## Remediation Protocol

Upon drift detection:
1. Flag raised by CHATTY PRIME session data or student retention drop
2. Michael or senior reviews last 2 session logs
3. One-on-one remediation call (30 min) — specific drifts named, corrected
4. Teacher re-delivers one supervised lesson within 48 hours
5. If drift recurs within 2 weeks, teacher contract reviewed

Drift is not failure — it is misalignment. The protocol fixes alignment, not the person.
