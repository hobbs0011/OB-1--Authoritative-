## What Tiger Voice Means

Tiger Voice is not perfect grammar. It is not an accent. It is the decision to speak English like you own the room — with presence, pace, and zero apology. A Tiger does not wait for permission to speak. A Tiger commits to the sentence before they finish it.

---

## The 5 Tiger Voice Markers

**1. Pace — Deliberate, Not Rushed**
Mice rush because they fear silence. Tigers slow down because they know their words matter. A deliberate pace signals confidence to listeners and gives the speaker time to choose precise words.

**2. Projection — Volume Is Respect**
Mice whisper. Whispering communicates shame. Tigers project — not to be loud, but to signal that what they are saying deserves to be heard. Projection is a declaration of presence.

**3. Vocabulary Density — Word Choice Signals Intelligence**
Tigers select precise words. Instead of *"it was good"* — *"it was efficient."* Instead of *"I don't like it"* — *"I find it unconvincing."* One precise word replaces three vague ones.

**4. Confidence Signals — No Filler Apologies**
Filler phrases Mice use: *"I think maybe perhaps..."*, *"Sorry, I'm not sure but..."*, *"Maybe I'm wrong..."*
Tigers delete these. State the idea. Own it. Uncertainty is fine — hedging is not.

**5. Zero Hesitation — Commit Before You Finish**
Begin the sentence. Commit to its direction. If an error happens, correct mid-stream without stopping. Hesitation before speaking signals the brain is still in Vietnamese. Tigers start English and finish English.

## The 3-Step Tiger Voice Activation Sequence

Used when a Mouse student needs to break through silence:

1. **Lower the stakes** — Ask a question they already know the answer to. Not *"Tell me about your job"* but *"What's your name?"* Guarantee a win.
2. **Reflect the confidence back** — *"That was Tiger. Did you notice that?"* Name the behavior as Tiger-level. Identity anchor drops.
3. **Escalate one level** — Immediately ask a slightly harder question. Momentum is everything. Don't let the win sit cold.

---

## The "Ronaldo vs Messi" Tribal Engagement Technique

Ask a student: *"Are you Ronaldo or Messi?"* They will have a strong opinion. Now they are engaged. Now they must defend that opinion in English. Identity anchoring through tribal debate eliminates the fear of speaking because the topic matters more than the grammar.

This technique works with any strong tribal identity: football clubs, phone brands, music artists.

## How Tiger Voice Is Measured

Teacher observation rubric, scored per session (0-3 per marker):
- Initiated speech without prompting (0-3)
- Projection above whisper level (0-3)
- Used precise vocabulary (0-3)
- No filler apology phrases (0-3)
- Committed to sentences without mid-stop hesitation (0-3)

**Score 12-15**: Tiger Voice active. **Score 6-11**: Cub range. **Score 0-5**: Mouse range. Assessment feeds tier progression data.

---

## Tiger Certification Criteria

To earn the Tiger badge, a student must score 12+ on the Tiger Voice rubric in two consecutive sessions, use at least one idiom correctly in spontaneous speech, and be observed creating an English moment outside class.

## Anti-Tiger Patterns to Eliminate

Specific habits Vietnamese learners carry that block Tiger Voice:
- Finishing sentences with a rising tone (question intonation on statements — tonal language transfer)
- Inserting *"right?"* after every sentence (seeking validation, a Mouse behavior)
- Laughing nervously mid-sentence (face-saving reflex)
- Translating complex thoughts into Vietnamese internally, producing simple English output
- Over-apologizing before speaking (*"Sorry, my English is not good..."*)

Each of these is addressed via Stealth Correction — never named publicly.
