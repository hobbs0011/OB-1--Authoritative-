## The Point System

Every action in the NET classroom earns points based on effort and output quality:

- **Vocabulary Points**: 10 pts each time a student uses a target word correctly in context.
- **Idiom Points**: 25 pts per correct idiomatic usage — higher value reflects higher difficulty.
- **Participation Points**: 5 pts per voluntary contribution to class discussion.
- **Game Win Bonus**: 50 pts awarded to winners of each of the 3 in-lesson games.
- **Perfect Session Bonus**: 100 pts if a student hits all three game wins and uses 5+ vocabulary items in one session.

Points are logged automatically by SyncCore during AI-delivered lessons and manually entered by teachers after human-delivered sessions.

---

## The Badge Hierarchy

Badges mark identity milestones — they are earned, never gifted:

- **Mouse Badge**: Enrolled and attended first 3 sessions. Acknowledges courage to begin.
- **Cub Badge**: 500 points accumulated AND teacher verification of active participation shift.
- **Tiger Badge**: 2,000 points AND demonstrated unprompted English initiation in two consecutive sessions.
- **Legend Badge**: Tiger Badge held for 60+ days AND one peer mentoring session completed. Reserved for the elite.

---

## Weekly Class Leaderboards

Every class has a live leaderboard updated after each session. Displayed via shared Zalo group image (auto-generated by SyncCore). Top 3 are celebrated publicly. The leaderboard resets weekly — sustaining urgency without permanent punishment.

---

## The Tiger Hunt Competition

The Tiger Hunt runs monthly. Format:

- Mice and Cubs attempt to produce Tiger-level responses to prompts given in class.
- Tigers must defend by producing responses judged more fluent and contextual.
- Judging is done by peer vote + teacher override.
- Tiger Hunt winners earn the "Apex" seasonal badge and 500 bonus points.

This competition compresses the Mouse-to-Tiger arc by creating visible aspiration in real time.

---

## Design Principle: Effort Earns Points, Not Attendance

Attendance is not rewarded. Showing up is the floor, not the ceiling. Points flow only from demonstrable learning behavior. This prevents passive students from accumulating status they haven't earned, protecting the integrity of the badge system.

---

## The Rewards System

Points unlock status and access — not cash:

- **500 pts**: Early access to new content themes (vote on next lesson topic).
- **1,000 pts**: Featured in the monthly NET success story post.
- **2,000 pts**: One-on-one 15-minute Tiger Session with Michael (rare, high-value).
- **5,000 pts**: Name on the NET Legend Wall (permanent recognition display).

---

## Anti-Gaming Safeguards

- Points require teacher or AI confirmation — self-reporting is not accepted.
- Repeated identical responses earn zero points after the first use.
- SyncCore flags statistical outliers (sudden point spikes) for human review.

---

## Gamification as Progress Tracking

Point velocity is a learning velocity proxy. A student earning 200+ points per week is absorbing and applying content. A student stagnant below 50 pts/week triggers a teacher check-in. The data speaks before the student does.

---

## Seasonal Competitions

- **Quarter 1**: Mouse Migration (most improved from Mouse tier).
- **Quarter 2**: Tiger Hunt Championship (see above).
- **Quarter 3**: Idiom Invitational (idiom usage frequency battle).
- **Quarter 4**: Legend Cup (cumulative annual points — the grand prize).
