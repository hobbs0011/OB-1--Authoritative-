## DOCUMENTED GLITCH INCIDENTS

### INCIDENT ALPHA-001: THE SLANG BOMB

**Date**: January 2025  
**Session**: Teen group, Cub level, ages 14–16  
**What Happened**: CHATTY PRIME was asked to provide examples of "casual English" used by young people. It generated the phrase "That's bussin' no cap fr fr" without explanation, context, or translation. Three students repeated it back with confidence. One used it in the parent report segment. The parent asked what it meant. Teacher did not know the current slang depth. Confusion ensued.

**Analysis**: CHATTY PRIME pulled from US social media training data without filtering for Vietnamese ESL context relevance or parent-appropriate content flags.

**System Improvement**: Cultural-appropriate content filter added to slang generation prompts. All AI-generated casual English examples must include: source context, appropriateness rating, and translation notes before classroom display.

---

### INCIDENT ALPHA-002: THE WRONG DEFINITION

**Date**: February 2025  
**Session**: Corporate class, BlueTech, professional adults  
**What Happened**: CHATTY PRIME defined "leverage" as "a tool used in construction to lift heavy objects." This is technically correct in a physical sense but entirely wrong for business English context. Four professionals wrote it down. One used it in a mock negotiation: "We should use a leverage — like a crowbar — in this deal." Classroom erupted in confusion.

**Analysis**: CHATTY PRIME defaulted to the most common training data definition without applying business English context filter.

**System Improvement**: All vocabulary presented in corporate sessions must include the prompt tag "business English context only." Teacher pre-reviews definitions before displaying to group.

---

### INCIDENT ALPHA-003: THE PHANTOM IDIOM

**Date**: March 2025  
**Session**: Mixed adult group, Tiger level  
**What Happened**: A student asked CHATTY PRIME to explain the idiom "burning the midnight candle." CHATTY PRIME provided a confident, detailed explanation and usage examples. The idiom does not exist. The correct idiom is "burning the midnight oil." Students learned, practiced, and produced a non-existent expression.

**Analysis**: CHATTY PRIME hallucinated a plausible-sounding idiom. The confident delivery prevented teacher skepticism. No verification was performed.

**System Improvement**: All idioms generated by CHATTY PRIME are cross-referenced against a verified idiom database before classroom use. Teacher is required to confirm any idiom not on the approved lesson list before displaying.

### INCIDENT ALPHA-004: THE CULTURAL LANDMINE

**Date**: April 2025  
**Session**: Teen group, mixed level, ages 12–15  
**What Happened**: CHATTY PRIME generated a vocabulary example using a scenario where a child disagrees with their parent's decision and argues back. The example read: "I told my dad he was wrong and I was right." In Vietnamese educational culture, this is not simply bold — it is face-destroying for the family hierarchy. Two students went visibly uncomfortable. One asked if this was "normal in America." The lesson lost its cultural footing for 15 minutes.

**Analysis**: CHATTY PRIME generated examples based on Western individualism assumptions without cultural context filtering.

**System Improvement**: Vietnamese cultural context module added to all example generation. Prompts now include the tag "Vietnamese family hierarchy — face-saving required." Any example involving parent-child dynamics is human-reviewed before display.

### INCIDENT ALPHA-005: THE FALSE FLUENCY REPORT

**Date**: May 2025  
**Session**: Post-assessment, Mouse level student  
**What Happened**: CHATTY PRIME assessed a Mouse-level student's spoken paragraph and returned a Cub-level fluency score. The student had spoken with good memorized phrases but zero spontaneous production. The AI scored memorization performance as genuine fluency. The teacher, distracted by another student, accepted the score. The student was placed in Cub-level content the following week. She collapsed within two sessions.

**Analysis**: CHATTY PRIME cannot reliably distinguish rehearsed speech from genuine spontaneous production without explicit testing conditions that force unprepared responses.

**System Improvement**: Assessment protocol now requires an "unprepared question" element that CHATTY PRIME cannot predict. AI fluency scores are flagged as "draft assessments" requiring teacher confirmation before entering student records.

---

### INCIDENT ALPHA-006: THE MISPRONUNCIATION GUIDE

**Date**: June 2025  
**Session**: Pronunciation clinic, mixed level  
**What Happened**: CHATTY PRIME provided phonetic guidance for the word "colonel." It wrote: "Pronounced: col-oh-nel." The actual pronunciation is "KER-nel" — one of English's most famous pronunciation traps. Five students practiced the wrong pronunciation for ten minutes before the teacher caught it.

**Analysis**: CHATTY PRIME generated phonetic spellings based on letter patterns rather than actual pronunciation rules. The word "colonel" is a known irregular that requires hard-coded exception handling.

**System Improvement**: A list of 50 high-frequency pronunciation traps (colonel, February, Wednesday, etc.) has been hard-coded into the pronunciation module. CHATTY PRIME cross-references this list before generating phonetic guides.
