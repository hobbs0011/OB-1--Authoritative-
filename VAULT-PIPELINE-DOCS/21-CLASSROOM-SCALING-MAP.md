## Overview

Scaling a classroom is not the same as scaling a product. Every threshold breaks something different. This document maps the four phases of NET's growth architecture, the specific failure points at each transition, and the systems required to survive each jump.

---

## Phase 1 — 1 Classroom (The Origin State)

**Trigger**: Michael operating solo with CHATTY PRIME support.

At Phase 1, everything is manual. Lesson planning, student tracking, parent communication, enrollment — all human-executed. CHATTY PRIME handles conversational practice sessions and post-class vocabulary reinforcement. Quality is highest here because the creator is in the room. The danger is dependency: the system only works when Michael is present.

**What breaks here**: Nothing. But nothing scales either.

---

## Phase 2 — 5 Classrooms (The First Fracture)

**Trigger**: Demand exceeds Michael's physical capacity. Second teacher hired.

At 5 classrooms, the Michael Method must be transmittable. If it lives only in Michael's instincts, the business cannot grow. This phase demands:
- A teacher training protocol (written, testable, repeatable)
- First QA observations (Michael auditing lessons he doesn't teach)
- CHATTY PRIME standardized for non-Michael classrooms
- A weekly coordination meeting structure

**What breaks at 1→5**: **Quality**. New teachers default to traditional ESL. Stealth Correction is abandoned. Emotional Translation is skipped. Students notice.

**Fix**: The Michael Method Certification — no teacher operates without passing TMST (The Michael Standard Test).

---

## Phase 3 — 20 Classrooms (The Infrastructure Threshold)

**Trigger**: Manual coordination becomes impossible. SyncCore orchestration required.

At 20 classrooms, SyncCore becomes the operational brain — scheduling, flagging underperforming sessions, tracking aggregate student progress, routing CHATTY PRIME configurations to the right classroom types. Human oversight remains, but humans cannot manage 20 simultaneous lesson threads without AI support.

**What breaks at 5→20**: **Teacher supply**. The pipeline of qualified, Michael-Method-certified teachers dries up faster than demand grows.

**Fix**: A teacher academy — a formal pipeline producing 3-4 certified teachers per month through accelerated mentorship and CHATTY PRIME-assisted training drills.

---

## Phase 4 — 100 Classrooms (The National Presence)

**Trigger**: Provincial expansion. NET becomes a brand, not just a classroom.

At 100 classrooms, the franchise model activates. Owned locations cannot be managed from a single operations center. Master franchisees carry the brand in each province, governed by non-negotiable Michael Method standards, CHATTY PRIME as the non-negotiable AI layer, and SyncCore as the quality enforcement mechanism.

**What breaks at 20→100**: **Culture**. The emotional core of NET — the Tiger Voice, the transformation mission — gets diluted by franchise operators who optimize for throughput over transformation.

**Fix**: Cultural encoding. VAULT-CHAOS doctrine flows into every franchise agreement. CHATTY PRIME carries the soul. No operator can configure CHATTY PRIME to skip Emotional Translation.
