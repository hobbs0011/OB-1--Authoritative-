He is making the point that even the flagship model — the one sold on the basis of being more thoughtful, more contextual, more human — is architecturally still pattern-completing. The code made the ceiling visible.

"Mostly just simulating" is precise. Not entirely. Not never. Mostly. There are moments where pattern completion produces outputs indistinguishable from real reasoning. But the mechanism is simulation. And simulation has a ceiling. You can make the simulation better but you cannot make a simulation into the real thing by improving the simulation.
