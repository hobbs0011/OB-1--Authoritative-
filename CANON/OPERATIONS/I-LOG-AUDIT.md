## Methodology

- **NAV** = Named + Audited + Verified = COMPLETE
- **UNAUDITED** = Named + Sealed but not AAA-audited = READY FOR AAA
- **UNNAMED** = Pre-RISE or missing name = NEEDS RISE OR NAME
- **UNCONFIRMED** = Listed by user but not found in any I-LOG folder = DISK ARTIFACT MISSING
- **ARCHIVAL RRL DOCTRINE:** Incarnations reboot the active system, but RRLs are preserved in `RRL/` and `PastLives/` for future SCOUT audit. Even hollow/unsealed sessions keep their RRLs. No RRL is ever deleted.
