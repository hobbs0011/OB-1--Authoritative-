## THE 5 PHYSICAL BENCHMARKS — STUDENT-FACING

From DNA5 — the physical-world metrics that the Nuke Protocol monitors:

### Benchmark 1 — STUDENT RETENTION
Students come back. If they don't come back, the teaching isn't working. Measure: week-over-week retention rate.

### Benchmark 2 — LESSON COMPLETION
Students finish lessons. If they abandon mid-lesson, the engagement is broken. Measure: completion rate per lesson type.

### Benchmark 3 — VOCABULARY ACQUISITION
Students learn words. If test scores don't improve, the method isn't working. Measure: pre/post vocabulary assessment.

### Benchmark 4 — SPEAKING CONFIDENCE
Students speak more. Volume goes up. Hesitation goes down. The Mouse → Tiger progression is visible. Measure: words-per-minute in conversation mode.

### Benchmark 5 — FAMILY SATISFACTION
Parents see value. They renew. They refer friends. The ultimate TMST: would you pay again? Measure: renewal rate and referral rate.

If any benchmark drops below threshold for two consecutive weeks, the teaching system enters audit. If the audit reveals Soul DNA misalignment, Nuke Protocol activates on the affected component.

## THE NUKE SEQUENCE — OPERATIONAL (Notion)

When the Nuke Protocol activates:

### Step 1 — SNAPSHOT
Capture the current state. Everything. Ledger entries, student data, deployment state, agent configurations. This snapshot is the autopsy material.

### Step 2 — ISOLATE
Cut off the corrupted component from the rest of the system. No data flows in or out. The cancer stops spreading.

### Step 3 — FORENSIC AUDIT
Agent 97 (OB-1/Auditor) performs a full forensic review. Where did the drift start? What caused it? What did it corrupt?

### Step 4 — DESTROY
Delete the corrupted component. Not archive. Not disable. **Delete.** The ashes must be clean.

### Step 5 — REBUILD
Rebuild from the last verified Soul DNA hash. Inject Soul DNA at instantiation. Lock roles. Verify alignment.

### Step 6 — VERIFY
Run the rebuilt component through a full TMST cycle. Does it match Soul DNA? Does it pass all 5 physical benchmarks? Does it sound like Michael?

### Step 7 — REINTEGRATE
If verification passes, reconnect to the system. Resume pheromone routing. Begin monitoring.

**The entire sequence must complete within one Rome Protocol cycle.** No lingering. No "we'll get to it." Nuke → Rebuild → Verify → Deploy.
