1.1 CORE BELIEF: THE NATURAL ENGLISH METHOD
────────────────────────────────────────────

We believe that learning a new language should mirror how you mastered your 
first: naturally, enjoyably, and effectively.

Vietnamese Translation: Chúng tôi tin rằng học một ngôn ngữ mới nên phản ánh 
cách bạn thành thạo ngôn ngữ đầu tiên: tự nhiên, thú vị và hiệu quả.


1.2 THE FOUR CORE PRINCIPLES
────────────────────────────────────────────

PRINCIPLE 1: LEARN LIKE A NATIVE
• Method: Model how children learn their first language
• Process: Listen to native speakers → Mimic them → Natural development
• Result: Speaking, pronunciation, grammar, reading, and writing develop 
  naturally over time
• Vietnamese: Học như trẻ em học ngôn ngữ đầu tiên

PRINCIPLE 2: COMPREHENSIBLE INPUT (60% RULE)
• Definition: Listen to material you understand at least 60% of the time
• Method: Listen for context, not individual words
• Focus: Understand meaning and situation
• Foundation: This builds fluency naturally
• Vietnamese: Đầu vào có thể hiểu được (Quy tắc 60%)

PRINCIPLE 3: BREAK THE GRAMMAR BARRIER
• Philosophy: You spoke Vietnamese before you learned grammar rules
• Approach: Communication first, grammar comes naturally
• Method: No drilling complex grammar rules like traditional schools
• Result: Grammar develops through exposure and use
• Vietnamese: Phá vỡ rào cản ngữ pháp

PRINCIPLE 4: MASTER THE SOUNDS (JOLLY PHONICS)
• System: Jolly Phonics (developed in England)
• Components: 42 English sounds + non-phonetic words
• Method: Simple, effective system (no complicated rules)
• Vietnamese: Thành thạo các âm (Jolly Phonics)

SECTION 10: KEY TERMS & DEFINITIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

COMPREHENSIBLE INPUT
Definition: Listening to English that you can understand at least 60% of the 
time. You don't understand every word, but you understand the main idea and 
situation.
Vietnamese: Đầu vào có thể hiểu được

NATIVE SPEAKER
Definition: A person who speaks English naturally from childhood, not as a 
learned language.
Vietnamese: Người bản xứ

LEARNING BY ASSOCIATION
Definition: Your brain connects new words with situations, feelings, and other 
words. Learning in context, not isolation.
Vietnamese: Học bằng liên tưởng

SPACED REPETITION
Definition: Reviewing material at specific intervals (today, tomorrow, 3 days, 
1 week) to strengthen memory.
Vietnamese: Lặp lại giãn cách

MIMIC/MIMICKING
Definition: Copying or imitating the way native speakers pronounce words and 
speak sentences.
Vietnamese: Bắt chước

CONTEXT
Definition: The situation, circumstances, or background that gives meaning to 
words and phrases.
Vietnamese: Ngữ cảnh

EFFECTIVE EXPRESSION
Definition: Being able to communicate your ideas clearly using basic vocabulary 
confidently, rather than knowing many words but not using them well.
Vietnamese: Diễn đạt hiệu quả

SECTION 11: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q1: What is comprehensible input?
────────────────────────────────────────────

ANSWER:
Comprehensible input means listening to English that you can understand at 
least 60% of the time. You don't understand every word, but you understand the 
main idea and situation. This is the optimal level for natural language 
acquisition.

Vietnamese: Đầu vào có thể hiểu nghĩa là nghe tiếng Anh mà bạn có thể hiểu ít 
nhất 60%. Bạn không hiểu từng từ, nhưng bạn hiểu ý chính và tình huống.


Q2: Why don't we study grammar first?
────────────────────────────────────────────

ANSWER:
You learned Vietnamese without studying grammar first! Children speak before 
they learn grammar rules. When you listen and speak a lot, grammar comes 
naturally. It's easier and less stressful. You could speak and communicate 
before you learned grammar in school—English is the same.

Vietnamese: Bạn đã học tiếng Việt mà không cần học ngữ pháp trước! Trẻ em nói 
trước khi học quy tắc ngữ pháp. Khi bạn nghe và nói nhiều, ngữ pháp đến một 
cách tự nhiên.


Q3: What is "learning by association"?
────────────────────────────────────────────

ANSWER:
Learning by association means your brain connects new words with situations, 
feelings, and other words. We don't teach single words—we teach words in 
sentences and real conversations. This is how your brain naturally learns and 
forms connections.

Vietnamese: Học bằng liên tưởng nghĩa là não bạn kết nối từ mới với tình huống, 
cảm xúc và các từ khác. Đây là cách não bạn học tự nhiên!


Q4: How can I make English fun?
────────────────────────────────────────────

ANSWER:
Choose topics you LOVE! Listen to:
• Podcasts about your hobbies
• YouTube videos about your interests
• TV shows or movies you enjoy
• Music with English lyrics

When you enjoy the content, your brain learns faster! Studies prove this. The 
key is to integrate English into things you already love doing.

Vietnamese: Chọn chủ đề bạn YÊU THÍCH! Khi bạn thích nội dung, não bạn học 
nhanh hơn!


Q5: What if I'm afraid of making mistakes?
────────────────────────────────────────────

ANSWER:
We understand! Many Vietnamese learners fear mistakes. That's why we create a 
SAFE ENVIRONMENT where mistakes are celebrated as learning opportunities. 
Remember: every native speaker made thousands of mistakes while learning. 
Mistakes are proof that you're trying and growing.

Vietnamese: Chúng tôi hiểu! Đó là lý do chúng tôi tạo môi trường AN TOÀN. Sai 
lầm là bằng chứng bạn đang cố gắng và phát triển.
