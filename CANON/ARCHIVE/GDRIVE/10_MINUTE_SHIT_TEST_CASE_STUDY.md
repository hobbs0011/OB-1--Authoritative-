## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**The Concept:** If a candidate can't complete a basic task in the time it takes to use the bathroom (literally while on the toilet), they're disqualified.

**Why It Works:** Separates executors from excuse-makers in 10 minutes instead of 10 days.

**The Standard:** Tasks that should take 10 minutes maximum using AI properly.

**The Filter:** Anyone who takes hours, makes excuses, or questions the approach = instant disqualification.

## THE PHILOSOPHY

### Core Principle:

**"If you can't do this while taking a shit, you can't work for us."**

This isn't crude - it's precise. It means:
1. Task should be THAT simple with proper AI use
2. Can be done on phone, anywhere, anytime
3. No excuses about "needing my computer" or "needing time"

- ✅ **Hustle mentality** - Obsessed with finishing fast or making excuses?

### What It Filters Out:

- ❌ People who overthink simple tasks
- ❌ People who need "perfect conditions" to work
- ❌ People who make excuses instead of using resources
- ❌ People who can't follow written instructions
- ❌ People who question the system instead of executing

## IMPLEMENTATION PROTOCOL

### Step 1: Initial Contact

**Message includes:**
- "Simple 1-hour test task (unpaid)"
- "If you pass, start at 50k/hour"
- "Fast, accurate work gets bonuses"

**Red flags in response:**
- "Can you explain more?" ← Should just try it
- "What if I fail?" ← Fear-based, not confidence
- "Is this paid?" ← Didn't read "unpaid test"
- **Green flag:** "Send me the task"

### Step 2: Send The Shit Test

**Instructions format:**
```
TASK: Create a Google Form with these 5 questions:
1. Name (text)
2. Age (number)
3. City (dropdown: Hanoi, HCMC, Danang)
4. Experience level (1-5 scale)
5. Comments (paragraph)

Connect this form to a Google Sheet.
In the Sheet, add a formula to calculate the AVERAGE of the Experience level responses.

Submit the form link + sheet link when done.

Time limit: None. But this should take 10-20 minutes if you use AI.
```

**What we DON'T include:**
- Step-by-step instructions
- Screenshots
- Video tutorials
- "Here's how to use ChatGPT"

**Why:** We want to see if they figure that out themselves.

### Step 3: Evaluate Results

**Timing Matrix:**

| Time Taken | Verdict | Reason |
|------------|---------|--------|
| 0-15 min | AUTO-HIRE | Exactly what we want |
| 15-30 min | STRONG PASS | Good enough, might be cautious |
| 30-60 min | CONDITIONAL | One more task to confirm |
| 60-120 min | LIKELY FAIL | Too slow unless perfect quality |
| 120+ min | AUTO-REJECT | Kenneth-tier, disqualified |

**Quality Matrix:**

| Quality | + Fast (10-15m) | + Medium (30m) | + Slow (60m+) |
|---------|----------------|----------------|---------------|
| Perfect | HIRED | HIRED | CONDITIONAL |
| Good | HIRED | CONDITIONAL | FAIL |
| Poor | CONDITIONAL | FAIL | FAIL |

### Step 4: Feedback Loop

**For PASSES:**
- "You're hired. Here's your first real task (paid)."
- No praise, no explanation
- Just next task

**For FAILS:**
- "Thank you for your time."
- No explanation, no feedback
- Just move on

**Why no feedback?**
- Our operators self-correct using AI
- If they need us to explain why they failed, they failed for the right reason
- We're building autonomous drones, not training employees

## CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS

### What Makes The Shit Test Work:

**1. Simplicity:**
- Task must be genuinely simple
- 10 minutes with AI is not negotiable
- If it takes 30 minutes, task is too complex
- **Test the test on yourself first**

**2. AI-Dependency:**
- Task should be annoying without AI
- Task should be trivial with AI
- This filters for AI adoption naturally

**3. Clear Success Criteria:**
- "Form must have these 5 questions"
- "Sheet must calculate AVERAGE"
- No ambiguity on what "done" means

**4. Zero Hand-Holding:**
- No "here's how to use ChatGPT"
- No step-by-step guide
- They figure it out or they don't
- **Self-service is the test**

### Common Mistakes To Avoid:

**❌ Making task too hard:**
- "Build a full database with user auth"
- Even with AI, this is hours
- Shit test ≠ hazing

**❌ Giving too many instructions:**
- 10-page PDF on "how to complete this"
- Defeats the purpose
- Want to see if they can figure it out

**❌ Being unclear on success:**
- "Make it look nice"
- "Use your creativity"
- These are not shit-testable

**❌ Accepting slow completions:**
- "Well, they got it done eventually..."
- No. Speed is the test.
- 2 hours for a 10-minute task = fail
