# 📚 NET TEACHING BIBLE V1

## 1. PEDAGOGICAL CORE

### 1.1 Four Pillars

**PILLAR 1: LISTEN & UNDERSTAND**
- **Time:** 45 minutes daily
- **Content:** What student LOVES (sitcoms, podcasts, YouTube)
- **Rules:**
  - English subtitles only (no Vietnamese)
  - Must understand 60%+ of content
  - No pausing, no translating
  - Passive absorption, not analysis

**Why It Works:**
Your brain learns by pattern recognition. Natural exposure builds automatic comprehension. This is how babies learn.

**PILLAR 2: SPEAK & MIMIC**
- **Time:** 15 minutes daily
- **Actions:**
  - Shadow native speakers (repeat out loud)
  - Master 42 English sounds
  - Speak LOUDLY (tiger, not mouse)
  - Record yourself and compare

**Why It Works:**
Speaking builds muscle memory. Shadowing gives you natural rhythm. Volume training breaks psychological barriers.

**PILLAR 3: LEARN IN CONTEXT**
- **Method:**
  - Never memorize isolated words
  - Learn through real sentences
  - Spaced repetition: 0 → 1 → 3 → 7 days
  - Natural associations, not translation

**Why It Works:**
Your brain remembers meaning through context, not translation. Real sentences stick; word lists don't.

**PILLAR 4: CONFIDENCE FIRST**
- **Environment:**
  - Weekly buddy practice
  - Monthly networking events
  - Community support
  - Safe space for mistakes

**Why It Works:**
Fear is the biggest barrier. Social support turns anxiety into excitement. Practice with peers before strangers.

---

### 1.2 The 60% Rule
**If student understands less than 60% of content: TOO HARD. Go easier.**
**If student understands more than 90% of content: TOO EASY. Go harder.**

**Sweet spot: 60-80% comprehension.**

This is the "flow state" where learning happens fastest.

---

### 1.3 Mouse → Tiger Model

**MOUSE BEHAVIORS:**
- Quiet, mumbles, apologizes constantly
- Translates every sentence mentally
- Studies grammar endlessly, speaks rarely
- Freezes when foreigners approach
- Believes: "I'm not good enough"

**TIGER BEHAVIORS:**
- Loud, direct, owns mistakes

## 2. LESSON ARCHITECTURE

### 2.1 The 10-5-5-3 Pattern (90-Minute Class)

**SEGMENT 1: WARM-UP (10 min)**
- **Goal:** Break ice, activate English brain
- **Activities:**
  - "Weekend stories" round-robin
  - Current mood check-in
  - Quick vocab review from last session

**SEGMENT 2: INPUT (5 min)**
- **Goal:** Present new content (grammar, vocab, pattern)
- **Method:** 
  - Show, don't lecture
  - Use real examples (movie clips, tweets, conversations)
  - Keep it SHORT

**SEGMENT 3: GUIDED PRACTICE (5 min)**
- **Goal:** Students try new content with support
- **Method:**
  - Teacher leads drill
  - Choral repetition
  - Error correction in real-time

**SEGMENT 4: FREE PRACTICE (3 min × 20 rounds = 60 min)**
- **Goal:** Students speak freely, apply content
- **Method:**
  - Pair/group conversations
  - Role-plays, debates, storytelling
  - Teacher circulates, listens, notes errors
  - NO interruption during speech

**SEGMENT 5: FEEDBACK (10 min)**
- **Goal:** Correct common errors, reinforce wins
- **Method:**
  - List 3-5 common mistakes on board
  - Praise 3 students for specific wins
  - Quick recap of lesson focus

### 3.2 Echo Chamber (Listening Problem)

**PROBLEM:**
Student hears English, but it sounds like "mush" — no clear words, just noise.

**DIAGNOSIS:**
Not enough exposure to natural speed, rhythm, and intonation.

**SOLUTION:**
- 45 minutes daily of content at 60-80% comprehension
- English subtitles to "decode" the sounds
- Repeat same content multiple times until clarity emerges

**ARC:**
Week 1: "It's too fast"
Week 4: "I'm starting to hear words"
Week 8: "I can follow most conversations"

---

### 3.3 Translation Trap (Thinking Problem)

**PROBLEM:**
Student translates Vietnamese → English mentally, causing:
- Slow speech
- Unnatural grammar
- Mental exhaustion

**DIAGNOSIS:**
Never learned to "think in English" — only translation.

**SOLUTION:**
- Use NET PET app in English interface
- Think out loud in English (even alone)
- Ban Vietnamese in class/buddy sessions
- Context learning (no translation allowed)

**ARC:**
Week 1: "I can't think without translating"
Week 6: "I'm starting to think in English for simple things"
Week 12: "I dream in English sometimes"

## 4. CLASSROOM MANAGEMENT

### 4.1 Crisis Scripts (10 Types from CHATTY_DUMP)

**1. SILENCE BREAKER**
**When:** No one speaks, dead energy
**Script:** "Okay, 30-second rule. Everyone talks for 30 seconds about [random topic]. No thinking. Just talk. Go."

**2. PERFECTIONIST STOP**
**When:** Student keeps restarting sentences
**Script:** "Stop. Say it messy. I don't care about perfect. Just finish the sentence."

**3. TRANSLATION BLOCK**
**When:** Student says "How do you say [Vietnamese word]?"
**Script:** "Describe it in English. No translation. Use the words you know."

**4. ENERGY RAISE**
**When:** Class is low energy, bored
**Script:** "Stand up. Shake it out. Now say this sentence LOUD: [give phrase]. LOUDER. Good. Sit down."

**5. TECH FAIL**
**When:** Zoom crashes, audio cuts, video freezes
**Script:** "No tech? No problem. We go phone call. Or we do it live next time. Don't stress."

**6. RUDE STUDENT**
**When:** Student disrespects teacher or peers
**Script:** "Hey. Not okay. We're respectful here. Try again."

**7. CRYING STUDENT**
**When:** Student breaks down from frustration
**Script:** "It's okay to cry. English is hard. But you're doing great. Take 5 minutes. Come back when ready."

**8. GRAMMAR LAWYER**
**When:** Student argues about grammar rules
**Script:** "You're right about the rule. But native speakers say it this way. We're learning real English, not textbook English."

**9. GHOST STUDENT**
**When:** Student misses 2+ sessions without warning
**Script:** [Text] "Hey, I noticed you've been gone. Everything okay? No judgment. Just checking in."

**10. LEVEL GAP**
**When:** One student is way ahead/behind the group
**Script:** "You're doing great, but I think you'd be happier in [higher/lower group]. Let's move you next month."

---

### 4.2 Corrections Protocol

**NEVER interrupt during speech.**

**DURING PRACTICE:**
- Listen and take notes
- Mark patterns, not every error

**AFTER PRACTICE:**
- Give feedback on 3-5 patterns
- Praise specific wins
- Demonstrate correct form
- Have class repeat

**EXAMPLE:**
"I noticed everyone said 'I'm boring' when you meant 'I'm bored.' In English:
- Boring = makes others bored
- Bored = you feel bored
Try again: 'I'm bored.'"

---

### 4.3 Buddy System Rules

**MATCHING:**
- Similar level (max 1 level difference)
- Opposite energy (quiet + loud works well)
- Complementary goals (one wants confidence, one wants fluency)

**SESSION STRUCTURE (30 min):**
- 5 min: Check-in (how's your week?)
- 20 min: Free conversation (no topic limit)
- 5 min: Feedback exchange (what did partner do well?)

**LOCK-IN PERIOD:**
- Buddies commit to 4 weeks minimum
- Can't switch partners unless critical issue
- Teacher monitors but doesn't join
