## REQUIRED STRUCTURE

### **4-ROOM PROGRESSION**

**ROOM 0: IDENTITY SCAN**
- Voice confidence check
- Agent profile setup
- Mission briefing: "You have 60 seconds to pitch. Make every word count."

**ROOM 1: INTEL BRIEF**
- Download 3 power phrases:
  - "The problem we're solving is..."
  - "Here's what makes this different..."
  - "The ROI timeline is clear..."
- Each phrase = card that glows **GOLD** when clicked (downloaded)
- Must download all 3 before proceeding

**ROOM 2: THE GAUNTLET**
- Live simulation: CEO just asked "So what's your pitch?"
- 60-second timer countdown (visual bar)
- Student must structure their pitch in 3 parts:
  1. **Problem** (0-20 seconds)
  2. **Solution** (20-45 seconds)  
  3. **Close** (45-60 seconds)
- Waveform shows "speaking confidence" visualization
- Button clicks advance through pitch sections
- Too fast = rushed/nervous, too slow = losing attention, perfect = executive presence

**ROOM 3: CONTRACT SIGNED**
- Success screen: "PITCH APPROVED — FUNDING SECURED"
- Gold stamp visual
- Debrief prompts for teachers
