## 4) FOUR PILLARS PEDAGOGY

### 4.1 Pillar 1: Learn Like a Native

**Principle:** Natural acquisition > grammar drilling  
**Method:**
- Sound and meaning before rules
- Context-based learning
- Minimal explicit grammar
- Immersion methodology
- 60% comprehension threshold (Krashen's i+1)

**Application:**
- Students speak from Day 1
- No translation drills
- Real-world scenarios only
- Native materials (movies, podcasts)

### 4.2 Pillar 2: Comprehensible Input (60% Rule)

**Principle:** Content must be 60-80% understandable

**Thresholds:**
- Below 60% = student lost
- 60-80% = optimal growth zone
- Above 90% = no challenge, no growth

**Daily Practice:**
- 45 minutes listening
- 15 minutes speaking/writing
- Use subtitles strategically: VN → EN → None
- Choose content student ENJOYS

### 4.3 Pillar 3: Jolly Phonics (42 Sounds)

**Principle:** Systematic pronunciation training

**Sound Groups:**
- Group 1: s, a, t, i, p, n
- Group 2: c/k, e, h, r, m, d
- Group 3: g, o, u, l, f, b
- Group 4: ai, j, oa, ie, ee, or
- Group 5: z, w, ng, v, oo, oo
- Group 6: y, x, ch, sh, th, th
- Group 7: qu, ou, oi, ue, er, ar

**Physical Training:**
- Hand movements for muscle memory
- Exaggerated mouth positions
- Recording/playback for self-correction
- Minimal pairs practice (bit/beat, ship/sheep)

### 4.4 Pillar 4: Spaced Repetition

**Principle:** Review intervals for long-term retention

**Schedule:**
- Today → 1 day → 3 days → 1 week → 1 month
- Automation via flashcard systems
- Focus on high-frequency words first
- Muscle memory for natural speech
