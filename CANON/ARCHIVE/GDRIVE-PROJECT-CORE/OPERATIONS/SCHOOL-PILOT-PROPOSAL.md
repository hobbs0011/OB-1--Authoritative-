## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

NET PET AI is an **AI-powered English commitment enforcement system** designed for Vietnamese schools and educational centers. Unlike generic tutoring apps, NET PET AI uses daily accountability pressure (parent/boss notifications) to drive behavior change.

**Key Differentiator**: Students can't quit silently. Parents and supervisors stay informed.

**Pilot Scope**: 50–100 students, 3–6 months, measured by:
- Daily login rate (target: >70%)
- Streak completion (target: >80% multi-day streaks)
- Pronunciation improvement (pre/post phoneme testing)
- Retention rate (target: >85% after Month 2)

## THE PROBLEM WE SOLVE

### Vietnamese Education Context
1. **Skill Plateau**: Students study English for years but cannot speak fluently.
2. **Quit Culture**: 60% of students drop out of online/app-based learning within 2 weeks.
3. **Zero Accountability**: Parents pay tuition but never see real proof of progress.
4. **Teacher Burnout**: Teachers manually track 50+ students, repeat corrections endlessly.

### Why It Happens
- **No Continuity**: AI tutoring apps have no memory. Each session starts from zero.
- **No Pressure**: Students feel no social cost for quitting (unlike real classrooms).
- **No Transparency**: Parents can't verify if their money is actually improving their child's English.

---

## OUR SOLUTION: THE "ACCOUNTABILITY MOAT"

### Layer 1: Daily Speaking Loop (AI + Memory)
- Student opens app → AI (OBI) listens to English speaking (2–5 mins)
- OBI corrects mistakes immediately + tracks weak areas
- AI generates **Personalized 5-Page Report** (progress, weak sounds, next drills)
- Students feel heard; improvement is visible

### Layer 2: Parent/Boss Notifications (Zalo Pressure)
- Day 1 of inactivity: No message (grace period)
- Day 2: Zalo message: "Minh hasn't done his English drill today. This affects his grade."
- Day 3: "Minh is 2 days behind. His peers are improving faster."
- Day 7: "Minh hasn't practiced in a week. Phonics grade will be affected."

**Why Zalo?** Vietnamese parents/bosses check Zalo instantly. Fear of "losing face" (Mặt mũi) is the behavioral driver.

### Layer 3: Cryptographic Proof (The Ledger)
- Every interaction is **immutably logged** with timestamp + hash anchor
- Teachers/parents can see full audit trail of learning
- Proof of learning becomes sellable to employers or universities
