# 🏫 OBI — School Sales Playbook (HCMC Fast Close)

**Goal:** Close first 3 schools by Month 2  
**Revenue per school:** 5M–20M VND/month  
**Sales cycle:** 2–3 weeks  

---

## 🎯 STEP 1: TARGET IDENTIFICATION (Week 1)

### School Profile (Ideal)

**Type:**
- English centers (90% of revenue)
- Private schools (secondary)
- Corporate training centers (bonus)

**Location:** HCMC preferred (you're there)

**Size:**
- 20–100 students minimum
- 5–20 teachers

**Signal:**
- Active Facebook page (marketing-minded)
- Advertise classes (revenue-focused)
- Have WhatsApp or Zalo group
- Students have smartphones

### How to Find Them

**Method 1: Google Maps Search**
```
Search: "trung tâm tiếng anh quận 1 quận 3 quận 4"
Or: "English center HCMC"
Get list of 50+ schools
```

**Method 2: Facebook Groups**
```
Join: "Tiếng Anh HCMC" groups
Identify schools recruiting
Direct message principals
```

**Method 3: Your Network**
```
Ask current students: "What English centers do you know?"
Teacher referrals
```

### Priority Tier

**Tier 1 (Easy close — 10 schools):**
- Small centers (20–50 students)
- Struggling with retention
- Owner = teacher (lower decision barrier)

**Tier 2 (Medium — 10 schools):**
- Medium centers (50–150 students)
- Director + management team
- More bureaucracy

**Tier 3 (Hard — 5 schools):**
- Large centers (150+ students)
- Corporate decision-making
- High revenue but slow close

**💡 Start with Tier 1. Fastest close rate = 3–4 weeks.**

## 📞 STEP 2: THE OUTREACH (Week 1–2)

### Cold Outreach Script (WhatsApp/Facebook)

**Template 1 — Direct & Honest:**
```
Hello [Principal Name],

I'm building an AI system to help students practice 
English speaking daily (without teacher time).

Would save your school 5–10 hours/week of tutoring.

Result: students speak 40% better in 30 days.

Worth 15 minutes to demo?

I can visit your school Tuesday or Wednesday.

—Michael
```

**Why This Works:**
- Short (respects time)
- Specific benefit (time savings)
- Specific evidence (40% better)
- Easy "yes" (just a meeting)
- Offers to do the work (visit them)

### Response Rate Expectation
- Send to 30 schools
- ~40% reply (12 schools)
- ~50% want to meet (6 schools)
- ~2–3 will pilot

**Template 2 — Warm Intro (Preferred):**
```
Hi [Mutual Contact],

I'm working with [Principal Name]'s English center 
on a student speaking practice system.

Could you intro us? I think this could save them 
hours of work per week.

Thanks,
Michael
```

## 🎯 STEP 3: THE DEMO MEETING (Week 2)

### Pre-Meeting Preparation (1 day before)

**Email to confirm:**
```
Hi [Principal Name],

Confirmed for [DATE] at [TIME].

I'll show:
1. How OBI works (5 min)
2. Live demo with a student (3 min)
3. Your school's use case (5 min)
4. Pricing (2 min)

Bring a smartphone (I'll demo live speech recognition).

See you then,
Michael
```

### In-Person Demo Flow (20 minutes max)

**Minute 0–2: The Hook**
```
Principal, your challenge is:

Students use apps like Duolingo for 1 week, then quit.

Why? Because apps are easy to ignore.

OBI solves that by:
1. Making speaking mandatory (not optional)
2. Remembering their mistakes
3. Forcing repetition tomorrow
4. It feels like a teacher, not an app
```

**Minute 2–5: Live Demo**
```
Take out phone.
Open OBI app.
Speak a sentence with a mistake.

Show:
- Instant correction
- Explanation
- Drill
- Streak tracking

Say: "This is what your students see every day."
```

**Minute 5–10: School Use Case**
```
"Here's what happens at your school:

Monday class:
- Students learn past tense
- They leave

Same day:
- Send them OBI link
- They practice 5–10 min
- OBI drills past tense

Tuesday:
- You teach again
- Students REMEMBER past tense
- Your teaching is 3x more effective

Result:
- Students improve 40% faster
- Higher retention (they feel progress)
- Parents see homework being done (from logs)
- Teacher workload DOWN (OBI handles drill work)
"
```

**Minute 10–15: Pricing**
```
"This works for schools of any size.

For your school (let's say 40 students):

Option 1:
$5M/month (all-in)
→ Access for all 40 students
→ Teacher dashboard
→ Parent reports
→ Homework tracking

Option 2:
Try 2 weeks free
→ See results
→ Then decide

Which works for you?"
```

**Minute 15–20: Objection Handling**

**Common Objection 1: "Is it really that good?"**
```
Answer: "No idea if YOUR students will use it. 
That's why you try it free.

But here's the thing: most students who practice daily 
improve. That's not OBI magic, that's how English works.

OBI just makes them practice daily."
```

**Common Objection 2: "How much does it cost?"**
```
Answer: "For 40 students: 5M/month.
That's 125k per student.

vs. hiring a tutor: 1M/month per student.

Or if you teach it yourself: it frees up 5–10 hours.

Your time is worth more than 5M."
```

**Common Objection 3: "We're already using [Duolingo/Cambly]"**
```
Answer: "Good, keep using them.

OBI is different:
- You pick the weaknesses
- OBI drills ONLY those
- Students see progress faster (not random exercises)

Think of OBI as your enforcement layer ON TOP of 
what you're already doing.

Try it parallel with what you have."
```

**Body:**
```

OUTCOME:

PAID PHASE (if pilot succeeds):
- $5M/month for up to 40 students
- Unlimited classes
- Full teacher + parent dashboard
- 24/7 support

Timeline:
- Agreement by: [NEXT MONDAY]
- Pilot starts: [TUESDAY]
- Results ready: [2 WEEKS LATER]

Next step: You approve, and I set up 30 min on [DATE]
```

### Close Rate Mechanics

**From 6 demo meetings:**
- 2–3 say "yes" immediately (easy close)
- 2–3 say "let me think"
- 1 says "no"

**For the "let me think" group:**
- Follow up in 3 days
- "Principal, I'm adding 1 more school next week. 
   Spots are limited. Decision time?"
- Creates urgency

**Target:** 50% of demos = pilots = 3 pilots in Month 1

## 💬 OBJECTION PLAYBOOK (Real Responses)

### "I need to think about it"
→ "I get it. Let me ask: what would make you MORE comfortable? 
   Is it seeing results, or the commitment? If results, let's do the 2-week pilot."

### "It's too expensive"
→ "vs. what? Hiring an English teacher: 50M+/month. 
   vs. Cambly: 20M+/month. OBI is 5M and fixes the forgetting problem."

### "We already use [competitor]"
→ "Perfect. This is not replacement, it's addition. 
   it fixes the homework problem THEY don't solve. 
   Try it for 2 weeks alongside. No conflict."

### "Our teachers won't like it"
→ "Teachers LOVE it because it gives them 5 hours back per week. 
   No more grading homework that students skip. 
   Let me train them."

### "Will students actually use it?"
→ "Unknown. That's why we pilot for free. 
   If they don't, you lose nothing. 
   If they do, everyone wins."
