## 🚨 PIVOT TRIGGERS (When to Change Course)

### Red Flag #1: DAU <40% by Week 2

**What it means:** Students aren't using OBI (or we can't activate them)

**Response:**
- Pause new sales
- Debug engagement (lessons too hard? broken? boring?)
- Fix and re-launch pilots

**Adjust plan:** Month 1 extends to Month 2

### Red Flag #2: 0 of 3 Pilots Convert to Paid

**What it means:** Product doesn't solve the problem OR pricing is wrong

**Response:**
- Full debrief: "Why not?" (Listen, don't defend)
- Options:
  - Lower price by 50%
  - Add features they asked for (1-week sprint)
  - Pivot to different school type (maybe English centers aren't the fit)

**Adjust plan:** Month 2 becomes "fixes and re-approach"

### Red Flag #3: Cost of Goods >20% of Revenue

**What it means:** Deepgram/infra is unsustainable

**Response:**
- Optimize Deepgram model (lower quality settings)
- Cache responses (reduce API calls)
- Negotiate volume discount with Deepgram
- If still wrong: pivot to different tech (lower cost speech rec)

**Adjust plan:** Back up to Month 1 (narrow scope)

### Red Flag #4: You Burn Out

**What it means:** 300+ hours/month is unsustainable

**Response:**
- First: Delegate sales prospecting (Month 2 week 1)
- Second: Hire ops contractor (Month 2 week 3)
- Third: Move to part-time ops if needed, focus on product

**Adjust plan:** Extend timeline to 120 days, bring in team earlier

## 🎯 ROLE OF METRICS

### Track These Weekly

1. **DAU** (Daily Active Users)
   - Formula: (unique students logging in / total students) × 100
   - Target: Month 1 → 55%+, Month 2 → 65%+, Month 3 → 70%+
   - Action: If <50%, engage more, make harder

2. **Accuracy Trend** (Speaking accuracy week-to-week)
   - Formula: (Week 2 %correct - Week 1 %correct)
   - Target: +3% per week minimum
   - Action: If flat, lessons are too easy (no challenge)

3. **Revenue** (Contracts signed)
   - Formula: (Sum of all active school contracts)
   - Target: Month 1 → 0, Month 2 → 10M+, Month 3 → 40M+
   - Action: If behind, re-examine close rate (is messaging wrong?)

4. **Pipeline** (Schools in process)
   - Formula: (Schools in demo OR pilot OR negotiation OR waiting)
   - Target: Always have 2–3x pipeline of desired new schools
   - Action: If depleted, increase prospecting
