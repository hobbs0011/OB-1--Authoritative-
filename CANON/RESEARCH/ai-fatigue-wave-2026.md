# AI FATIGUE — THE NEXT WAVE
**Source:** SOV100 Michael Hobbs, Run 117, 2026-06-12
**Type:** Market intelligence / product thesis
**L79 HUMAN SOURCE — operator-observed, not model-assumed**

---

## The Observation

> *"People have AI fatigue. The winners in this next wave will be the ones who use AI but don't make it feel like it."*
> — Michael Hobbs, Run 117

AI fatigue is already here. The market is saturated with ChatGPT wrappers, "AI-powered!" product labels, and tools that perform helpfulness while delivering nothing personal. Users are exhausted by the uncanny valley of obvious AI — the over-explained responses, the simulated warmth, the forgotten context.

---

## The Winning Pattern

The next wave winners: **invisible AI**. The engine is AI. The experience is human.

Not: "Our AI tutor will help you learn English!"
But: "Obi knows you haven't practiced final consonants in 4 days. Let's work on that today."

The product doesn't announce itself. It just shows up prepared. It remembers. It moves forward.

---

## Application to NET

- Obi is not an "AI tutor" — Obi is the teacher who knows your name, your patterns, your open threads
- The Actualizer cron brief means Obi boots prepared, not blank
- The student never feels processed — they feel taught
- The AI is the engine. The relationship is the product.

---

## The Scale Inversion

At mass scale, invisible AI is impossible — the personalization math doesn't work. Anthropic can't do it. OpenAI can't do it.

A small sovereign operation with 2-10 students CAN do it. The Actualizer concept works BECAUSE we're small. Personalization that costs Anthropic millions costs us pennies.

**Being small is the structural advantage in the AI fatigue era.**
