## Summary

### Meeting Purpose

This was a demonstration and test of Notion's meeting notes feature. Hobbs explained how the feature works and then created several intentionally complex tasks to test whether Notion could accurately capture and summarize them.

### Notion Meeting Notes Feature Explanation

- The feature can be left on for 30 minutes to 90 minutes
- It listens to conversations and automatically remembers all tasks and details
- After the meeting, it will remind participants of all action items
- The system will download and write clear details of the work, create summaries, and translate audio to text files

### Test Tasks Created

To test the system's capabilities, Hobbs created several humorous and intentionally confusing tasks involving coffee delivery, birthday singing, cat play dates, phone call chains, selling bird saliva, and company-wide emails. There was some translation and discussion of these tasks among participants.

The meeting concluded with Hobbs noting they had said enough confusing things and wanted to see how Notion captured everything.
