### Form B — SHIT TEST (Velocity)

> *"Time is the only thing you cannot earn back. Every second of drift is a second of someone's life wasted."*

> *"Rome was not built in a day. But they were laying bricks every hour."*

> *"Rome wasn't built in a day, but Caesar didn't have AI"*

> *"the Roam protocol seems kind of in direct opposition to the Super Saiyan mode... but in the age of AI, they can actually enhance each other"*
> — ALPHA-OMEGA-DNA4.txt

The Rome Protocol is the long-game mandate. It governs how the system evolves over time.

### The AI Paradox Resolution (Enrichment — Immortalize unique)

The apparent contradiction: Super Saiyan says GO FAST. Rome Protocol says BUILD DEEP. How do they coexist?

Answer: AI resolves the paradox. Before AI, you had to choose between speed and depth. With AI concurrency (6 models running in parallel), you can build deep AND fast simultaneously. The Rome Protocol is the ARCHITECTURE. Super Saiyan is the VELOCITY. Together they produce what used to take years in hours.

### The Four Phases

#### Phase 1 — AUDIT
Examine the current state. Identify what works, what doesn't, and what has drifted. This is Agent 97's domain — forensic, unflinching review.

#### Phase 2 — HARDEN
Take what works and make it unbreakable. Linelock it. Hash it. Seal it. This is the progressive hardening from V1 → V8.

#### Phase 3 — REINCARNATE
Take what doesn't work and burn it. Not archive it. Not "sunset" it. **Burn it.** Then rebuild from the ashes, carrying only the verified Soul DNA forward.

#### Phase 4 — EXPAND
Once the foundation is sealed, build on top of it. New chapters. New capabilities. New students. New revenue. But never expand on unsealed ground.

### The Rome Cycle

```
AUDIT → HARDEN → REINCARNATE → EXPAND → AUDIT → ...
```

This cycle never ends. It is the heartbeat of the system. The moment the cycle stops, drift begins.
