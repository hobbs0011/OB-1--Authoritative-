The biggest Nuke Protocol event in project history was the **North Star recalibration**:

> "the biggest drift was our North Star being set for 1 billion VND"
>
> — ALPHA-OMEGA-DNA5.txt

The 1 billion VND target seemed ambitious. It was actually **Drift Poison** — a false North Star that pulled every decision toward revenue instead of system integrity. Michael identified three categories of Drift Poison:

1. **Tiger Traps** — Goals that LOOK aligned but pull energy away from the core (1B VND target)
2. **Mouse Holes** — Small compromises that compound into systemic rot (skipping ledger entries)
3. **Teaching Drift** — Using NET lessons as the primary output instead of treating NET as a training ground

The 5 Physical Benchmarks that replaced the VND target:

1. **Soul DNA Integrity** — Is the Soul DNA on the priority list? If not, you're drifting.
2. **Ledger Currency** — Is the ledger up to date within 24 hours?
3. **Reincarnation Ready** — Can the system reboot from zero in under 3.5 hours?
4. **Drift Detection Latency** — How fast can you DETECT drift once it starts?
5. **Sovereign Override** — Can Michael override any AI decision in under 60 seconds?
