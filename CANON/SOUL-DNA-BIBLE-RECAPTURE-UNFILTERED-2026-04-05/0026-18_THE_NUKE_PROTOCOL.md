# CHAPTER 18 — THE NUKE PROTOCOL

> The weapon of last resort. When drift becomes cancer, you don't treat it — you PURGE.

---

## WHY THE NUKE EXISTS

Not all drift can be corrected. Some drift has embedded itself so deeply that the cost of correction exceeds the cost of destruction and rebuild. When that happens, you invoke the Nuke Protocol.

The defining moment was the **1 Billion VND Target Crisis**:

> "the biggest drift was our North Star being set for 1 billion VND"
>
> — ALPHA-OMEGA-DNA5.txt

The 1B VND target was set early, with optimism. It seemed ambitious but achievable. What Michael didn't realize: it had become a **gravity well** pulling every decision toward revenue instead of system integrity. Feature decisions were being made based on what would generate VND, not what would harden the system.

---

## THE THREE CATEGORIES OF DRIFT POISON

Through the Nuke event and subsequent analysis, Michael identified three types of drift that require nuking (not correcting):

### 1. Tiger Traps
Goals that LOOK aligned but pull energy away from the core. The 1B VND target was a Tiger Trap — it seemed like it supported the mission, but it was actually redirecting all energy toward a metric that missed the point.

### 2. Mouse Holes
Small compromises that compound into systemic rot. Skipping a ledger entry here. Allowing an unverified commit there. Letting a drift report slide because "it's minor." Each Mouse Hole alone is harmless. Together they hollow out the system from inside.

### 3. Teaching Drift
Using NET English lessons as the PRIMARY output instead of treating NET as what it is — a training ground for the real system. Every hour spent optimizing a lesson plan instead of hardening the Phoenix architecture is Teaching Drift.

---

## THE NUKE SEQUENCE

When the Nuke Protocol is invoked:

1. **IDENTIFY** — Name the drift poison. Be specific. "The 1B VND target is Drift Poison."
2. **ISOLATE** — Trace every decision that was influenced by the poisoned target.
3. **SEVER** — Remove the poisoned target from all priority lists, dashboards, prompts.
4. **REPLACE** — Install the corrected North Star (5 Physical Benchmarks).
5. **VERIFY** — Run full audit to confirm no residual contamination.
6. **SEAL** — Lock the new target with a Titanium Omega Seal so it cannot be silently replaced.

---

## THE 5 PHYSICAL BENCHMARKS (What Replaced the Nuke Target)

1. **Soul DNA Integrity** — Is the Soul DNA on the priority list?
2. **Ledger Currency** — Is the ledger current within 24 hours?
3. **Reincarnation Readiness** — Can the system reboot from zero in under 3.5 hours?
4. **Drift Detection Latency** — Time from drift onset to detection.
5. **Sovereign Override Latency** — Time for Michael to override any AI decision.
