## 🧠 What Is This Game?

**"Survive a Brainrot"** is a Roblox survival game where players fight waves of meme-themed zombie enemies by **answering English questions**. Correct answers deal damage. Wrong answers hurt you. The better your English gets, the stronger you become.

It's designed for the Thai market (and global), targeting Roblox's **151.5 million daily players** — 70% on mobile.

**The pitch:** A zombie survival game that secretly teaches English. Players think they're gaming. They're actually learning.

**Teaching Law:** Mouse → Tiger. Start easy, build confidence, then challenge.

---

## 🚂 The Story: 99 Days on The Train

You and your squad of **5 players** board **The Train** — a moving fortress rolling through a post-apocalyptic wasteland overrun by "Brainrot" zombies.

Your mission: **Survive 99 days.**

The Train has three sections:
- **Engine Car** — up front, keeps you moving
- **Shield Car** — middle, your defense
- **Living Quarters** — back, your home base

Every day brings new waves of enemies. Every few days, you stop at locations like the **Rusted Depot** where you scavenge for supplies while fighting off hordes. Boss fights happen every 5 waves.

**If you make it to Day 99, you complete the journey.** Then you can Prestige (reset) and do it all again — harder, with permanent bonuses.

## ⚔️ How Combat Works

This is **not** a typical shooter. Here's the loop:

1. 🧟 **An enemy appears**
2. 📝 **You get an English question** matched to your skill level
3. ✅ **Answer correctly** → You deal damage to the enemy + earn coins
4. ❌ **Answer wrong** → YOU take 15 damage + your combo resets
5. 🔥 **Build combos** — every correct answer in a row increases your damage (up to 3x)
6. ⚡ **English Power-Up** — 5 correct answers charges a special attack that deals 5x damage for 5 seconds
7. 💀 **If you die** → Auto-respawn after 3 seconds. You keep your progress. No punishment for dying.

**Key stats:**
- Player health: **100 HP**
- Base damage per correct answer: **10**
- Wrong answer damage to you: **15**
- Boss fights require **3 correct answers** to kill
- Bosses have **5x health**

## 🐭 The 5 Difficulty Tiers (Player Classes)

Your difficulty tier determines what questions you get and how much damage you deal. You **start as a Mouse** and work your way up:

| Tier | Name | Damage Multiplier | Time Limit | What It Means |
|------|------|-------------------|------------|---------------|
| 1 | 🐭 **Mouse** | 1.0x | 15 seconds | Super easy questions. "What does Hello mean?" Build confidence here. |
| 2 | 🐱 **Cat** | 1.2x | 12 seconds | Basic grammar and opposites. Getting warmer. |
| 3 | 🐺 **Wolf** | 1.5x | 10 seconds | Sentence structure, tenses. Real English skills. |
| 4 | 🦁 **Lion** | 2.0x | 8 seconds | Advanced vocabulary like "ubiquitous". Serious power. |
| 5 | 🐯 **Tiger** | 3.0x | 6 seconds | Literary devices, complex grammar. Maximum damage. You're a beast. |

**How you move up:** Get above 70% accuracy AND answer faster than 60% of your time limit → you tier up every 10 correct answers.

**How you move down:** Drop below 40% accuracy → you step down one tier. No shame. Mouse → Tiger is a journey.

## 🌊 The Wave System (How 99 Days Play Out)

The game runs in **infinite waves**, with the narrative framed as 99 days:

- **Waves 1–4** → 🐭 **Mouse Waves** — Easy enemies, learn the controls
- **Wave 5** → 💀 **FIRST BOSS** (Sigma Boss appears) + 🐱 Cat Waves begin
- **Wave 10** → 💀 Boss + 🐺 **Wolf Waves** — things get real
- **Wave 15** → 💀 Boss
- **Wave 20** → 💀 Boss + 🦁 **Lion Waves** — danger zone
- **Wave 25** → 💀 Boss
- **Wave 30** → 💀 Boss
- **Wave 35+** → 🐯 **Tiger Waves** — maximum difficulty

**Each wave:**
- Starts with enemies: **5 + (2 × wave number)**, maxing at 25 enemies
- Boss appears every **5th wave**
- **8-second rest** between waves (gets shorter as you progress)
- Enemy health **scales up 15% per wave**
- Spawn rate gets faster each wave

**Rewards per wave:**
- Regular wave clear: **50 Survival Coins** per player
- Boss wave clear: **150 Survival Coins** (50 base + 100 boss bonus)
