June 29, 2026 — Oktava English Class Session Transcript
Teacher: Michael (Oktava English Class)
Student: Anh Minh
Topic: Teaching Methodology — KISS, Two Brains, Answer Formula, Active Listening

---

Michael: "Broken English that communicates is better than perfect grammar that freezes."
Anh Minh: "Yes, I understand that."
Michael: "That's a start — but an answer needs one detail plus a question back. 'Yes, I understand that, and I can give an example.'"
Anh Minh: "So instead of just 'Yes,' I should say 'Yes, I understand that, and I can give an example about my own experience.' Then ask 'What about you?'"
Michael: "Exactly. The Answer Formula: Answer + one detail + ask back. That's the #1 weakness fix."
Anh Minh: "What about when I don't know the word?"
Michael: "You hear what you KNOW, not what you don't. 20-30% comprehension is enough. Lock onto what you know, treat unknowns as noise."
Anh Minh: "So I should not try to translate every word?"
Michael: "No translation. No Vietnamese — speaking, thinking, reading, writing — during class or study. No exceptions."
Anh Minh: "That's hard."
Michael: "It's hard because your Vietnamese brain wants to help. But the Vietnamese brain and the English brain do NOT mix. If your strong arm always helps the weak arm, the weak arm never develops."
Anh Minh: "Like lifting weights — you need the weak arm to work on its own."
Michael: "Exactly. 1 move 10,000 times beats 10,000 moves once. Master 500-1,000 high-frequency words in 10 contexts."
Anh Minh: "What about the scam centers?"
Michael: "[INTERNAL — NOT STUDENT-FACING] Vietnam ESL centers sell appearance over results. Russian teachers = cheap + attractive. Students never complain — they just quit silently. Our counter: graduate students in 1-2 years, cheaper, 10-100x more effective, transparent progress."
Anh Minh: "So the business model is real results, not vanity credentials."
Michael: "Correct. Good ideas don't put food on the table, so we ship real results as the business model."
Anh Minh: "What about the app — OB / NET-PET-AI?"
Michael: "Streaks for daily practice, buddy system for peer speaking, AI memory that knows each student's vocab and SRS windows. Multi-role accountability — employer, parent, spouse can log in and see daily engagement."
Anh Minh: "The Vietnamese fear of losing face is the compliance lever?"
Michael: "Yes. Public visibility of progress. Streaks, dashboards, transcripts, audio records — all visible to the people who matter."
Anh Minh: "What's the beta plan?"
Michael: "Free users, 6-12 month testimonials, then investor pitch. Burn about $100/month, scale to $1,000/month."
Anh Minh: "And the KISS principle?"
Michael: "Keep It Simple, Stupid. Get to the point. Directly. Even if it's broken."
Anh Minh: "Thank you. I practiced a lot."
Michael: "Thank you. That's a perfect response — compliment deflection done right."
