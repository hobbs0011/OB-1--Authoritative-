COUNTER-PROPOSAL — EMPLOYMENT TERMS

To: Anh Bảo Nguyễn & Anh Minh Nguyễn

From: Michael Hobbs

Proposed Title: Head of English Curriculum and Instruction

Date: August 4, 2026

Why I Am Here

I want to be transparent about something honest up front: kindergarten is not where I naturally want to teach. My dream is corporate business English and corporate contracts — that is where my skills, experience, and heart are. But I am not at CASA because it was the only option. I am at CASA because of Anh Minh and Anh Bao. You are people I want to learn from. I believe that if I work hard, show up consistently, and prove my value, you will reward me, empower me, and mentor me toward the level I want to reach. You cannot put a price tag on that kind of opportunity.

That said, I do love the kids. It is genuinely rewarding working with them, even if it is not my natural lane. And I am willing to put everything I have into this role.

The probation started July 15th and is now concluding. I have been in the schedule and I have a clear picture of what works and what does not. Below is my honest assessment and proposal.

The Sacrifice

I maintain a small number of private business English clients at 600,000 VND/hour — significantly above what the CASA offer works out to on any structure. I am not here because I cannot earn more elsewhere. I am here because I see the path you are building and I believe in it. I am trading short-term income for long-term growth, and I accept that trade. I mention it not as a bargaining chip but as context: when I ask for adjustments to the structure, it is not because I do not value what you are offering. It is because I need the role to be sustainable so I can deliver the results you want over the long haul.

Full-Time — My Proposal

I want to accept the full-time role. I am proposing 120 hours per month at approximately 440,000 VND/hour — which lands right around 50,000,000 VND/month, the same salary you offered. I am not asking for more money. I am asking for a structure where the hours I am present are hours I can genuinely contribute.

Here is the exact schedule I am proposing:

Morning block (Mon–Fri): 7:00–8:45 AM — drop-off, parent interactions, morning Montessori with Cosmos and Cloudy classes.

30-minute unpaid break 8:45–9:15 AM

Morning continuation: 9:15–10:30 AM

Afternoon block (Mon–Fri): 2:30–5:30 PM — English classes for Cosmos, Windy, Clover, and Cloudy, plus playtime and pickup with parents.

Evening classes (Tue & Thu): 5:30–7:00 PM — with a quick 5:00–5:30 PM break for a bite to eat.

= 6 hours of active contribution per day (Mon/Wed/Fri), 7.5 hours (Tue/Thu), totalling ~120 hours/month

Within that 6 hours a day, I am handling everything that matters: drop-off and parent relationships, morning Montessori with the youngest classes, teaching all four core English class groups (Cosmos, Windy, Clover, Cloudy), playtime English conversation — which is where real language acquisition happens — and pickup engagement with parents. And the Tuesday and Thursday evening classes on top of that.

That leaves me a 10:30 AM–2:30 PM window in the middle of the day — four hours to step away, recharge, eat properly, and prepare for the afternoon. That gap is what makes the rest of the day sustainable. Right now, without that break in the actual schedule, the hours stack up and by Thursday I am running on adrenaline. At 120 concentrated hours, every minute counts. At 160 drained hours, the last 40 are a shadow of the first 120.

I also need to be transparent about something that affects my energy: I am currently living in a hostel with seven other people. Sleep is a luxury some nights. The move into an apartment near CASA is happening and it will help — but I do not want that to sound like 160 hours is the goal. Even with a good night's sleep and a short commute, 120 hours per month is the number I want as the benchmark for the full two-year contract. That is what lets me show up every day at the level you need. If that is not workable for CASA, tell me, and we will work from there. But I am not asking for 120 as a temporary crutch — I am asking for it as the structure.

On the part-time offer: I would take 80 hours at 36,000,000 VND/month if it came down to avoiding burnout. But at 80 hours I would not be able to deliver the things that make the biggest difference — the parent relationships during drop-off and pickup, the natural English conversation training during playtime, the continuity across all four class groups. Those are the levers that rebuild enrollment. Full-time at 120 hours lets me pull those levers. Part-time at 80 does not.

I am not trying to milk CASA. I know hiring a foreign teacher is a major expense and the centre needs to stay lean. I am trying to find a structure that is sustainable, where I can give you everything I have without burning out in six months.

Teaching Assistant

I hire, train, and manage the teaching assistant. The assistant reports to me — not to Miss Hien. Two-week unpaid trial, then paid from day 15 if he is learning and performing. If he is not progressing, I reserve the right to let him go and I will recruit his replacement myself.

Curriculum Authority

As Head of English Curriculum and Instruction, I hold final authority over all English teaching materials, methods, assessment, and parent communication. Cambridge templates are a reference, not a mandate — they inform my decisions but do not override them. I design the curriculum around language the children actually use in play and daily life, not vocabulary selected because it looks impressive on a brochure. If a curriculum recommendation says "teach programming to two‑year‑olds," that recommendation will be superseded by age‑appropriate methodology.

Anh Minh makes the final business decisions. I make the final English decisions. That is the division.

Classroom & Professional Boundaries

  • Observation: Anh Minh or Anh Bảo may observe my classes at any time with advance notice. No unscheduled walkthroughs. No peer evaluation by staff without classroom experience. No photographs or video of me or the students without my explicit permission for each instance.
  • Feedback: All feedback flows through Anh Minh and Anh Bảo. No behind-the-back reporting through other staff.
  • Management stays out of the classroom during instruction except in emergencies. Full methodology control rests with me.

Reporting Structure — Contract Language Required

I need this written into the contract, not just agreed verbally. I answer to Anh Minh for all pieces related to teaching, curriculum, and product delivery. Miss Hien has no authority over the English program — no supervision of my teaching, no management of the assistant, no evaluation of my classroom performance, and no role in curriculum decisions. This is not a negotiation point. It is a condition of my accepting the role.

Work Permit & Visa

The school covers all work permit costs for full-time employees. I accept this. On the two-year repayment clause: if I voluntarily resign before completing two years, I will reimburse the school for the work permit expense on a prorated basis — not a full clawback. If I complete the two-year contract, no repayment. Visa: two-year guarantee tied to performance and contract completion. I accept this.

Probation

One month at 80% salary is agreed. It started July 15th and is now concluding. I need the updated contract reflecting the final terms before that window closes.

What I Am Asking For

  1. 120 hours/month at approximately 440,000 VND/hour, landing at 50,000,000 VND/month — the same salary you offered. The reduction from 160 comes from restructuring the day into a focused 6-hour active block (7:00–10:30 AM and 2:30–5:30 PM) with a genuine break in the middle, plus the evening classes on Tuesday and Thursday. This is the benchmark for the full two-year contract, not a temporary arrangement.
  2. Reporting structure in the contract: I answer to Anh Minh on all teaching, curriculum, and product delivery matters. Miss Hien has no authority over the English program.
  3. Curriculum authority: Final say on English materials, methods, assessment, and parent communication, consistent with the Head of English Curriculum and Instruction title. Cambridge is a reference, not a mandate.
  4. Teaching assistant: I hire, train, and manage. The assistant reports to me. Two-week trial, then paid. I can fire and replace if not progressing.
  5. Classroom boundaries: No unscheduled walkthroughs. No peer evaluation by non-teaching staff. No photos or video without explicit permission.
  6. Work permit repayment: Prorated if I resign before two years. No clawback if I complete the contract.
  7. Two weeks' notice for non-routine events so I can resettle my private clients. I am the decision-maker on my private schedule — I am not beholden to any other employer. You have my word I will prioritise CASA events. I just need the notice to make it happen cleanly.

I am not asking for more money. I am not asking for fewer responsibilities. I am asking for a schedule where the hours I am at CASA are hours where I can be fully present, fully switched on, and fully useful — and where the few hours I am not at CASA are enough to recover and come back the next day ready to do it again.

I am ready to sign once these terms are reflected in the contract. Let me know when you want to sit down and finalise.

Best regards,

Michael Hobbs
