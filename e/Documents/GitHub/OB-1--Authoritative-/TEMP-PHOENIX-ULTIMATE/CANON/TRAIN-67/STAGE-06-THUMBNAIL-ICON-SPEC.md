## 🔴 WHY THIS MATTERS

Roblox's ML system automatically A/B tests thumbnails per player. More thumbnails = more experiments = better CTR. Games with 8-10 thumbnails get significantly more impressions than games with 1-2.

**Thumbnail is the #1 CTR driver.** Players decide to click in <1 second.

## 🎨 GOSPEL 444 PALETTE (MANDATORY)

| Color | Hex | Use |
|-------|-----|-----|
| **Purple** | #a855f7 | UI elements, auras, magical effects, VIP |
| **Gold** | #f59e0b | Rewards, coins, highlights, premium |
| **Dark** | #0f0f1a | Backgrounds, shadows, cinematic depth |
| ❌ **Blue** | FORBIDDEN | NEVER. Not in thumbnails, not in icons, not anywhere |

## 🖼️ 10 THUMBNAIL CONCEPTS

### Required Variants (Roblox ML needs diversity)

| # | Concept | Composition | Dominant Colors | Target Emotion |
|---|---------|-------------|-----------------|----------------|
| 1 | **Hero Shot** | Player character (front-facing) with glowing Purple weapon, Gold particles, Dark bg | Purple + Gold + Dark | "I want to BE that" |
| 2 | **Action Scene** | Player mid-swing, Brainrot enemy exploding into Gold coins | Gold + Dark + Purple accents | "Intense combat!" |
| 3 | **Horde Overwhelm** | Player surrounded by 20+ brainrot enemies, Purple aura shield | Dark + Purple glow | "Can I survive THIS?" |
| 4 | **Loot Explosion** | Chest opening with items flying out, Gold light rays, Purple sparkles | Gold + Purple | "FREE STUFF" |
| 5 | **Train Journey** | Cinematic train on tracks, apocalyptic landscape, Purple sky | Dark + Purple + Gold accents | "Adventure awaits" |
| 6 | **Boss Fight** | Mega Brainrot (huge) vs tiny player, dramatic scale contrast | Dark + Red + Purple | "Epic challenge" |
| 7 | **English Power-Up** | Player with glowing Gold weapon after answering correctly, energy burst | Gold + Purple | "Learning IS power" |
| 8 | **Before/After** | Split screen: weak player (left) vs powered-up player (right) | Dark vs Gold+Purple | "This could be you" |
| 9 | **Squad Shot** | 4 diverse characters in action poses, team composition | Purple + Gold + Dark | "Play with friends" |
| 10 | **Rare Item Showcase** | Legendary weapon/aura floating with God rays, "LIMITED" text | Gold + Purple glow | "I NEED that" |

### Thumbnail Rules

1. **Maximum 3 colors per thumbnail** — Purple, Gold, Dark as base
2. **Large characters** — faces and weapons must be clearly visible at 150x150px (mobile)
3. **High contrast** — Dark background makes Purple/Gold pop
4. **Text sparingly** — max 3 words if any ("NEW UPDATE", "FREE PETS")
5. **No blue. Ever.** Not in sky, not in water, not in UI
6. **Render at 1920x1080** — Roblox auto-crops to multiple ratios

## 🎮 GAME ICON

| Attribute | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Size** | 512x512 px |
| **Style** | Centered character face or weapon, Purple glow, Gold accents |
| **Background** | Dark (#0f0f1a) gradient |
| **Must read at** | 64x64 px (search results on mobile) |
| **Text** | NONE — icons should be purely visual |
| **Frame** | Optional Purple glow border |

### Icon Concept
Close-up of the player character's face with a Purple glowing eye and a Rusty Pipe weapon silhouette. Gold particles around the edges. Dark gradient background. Must be instantly recognizable at tiny sizes.
