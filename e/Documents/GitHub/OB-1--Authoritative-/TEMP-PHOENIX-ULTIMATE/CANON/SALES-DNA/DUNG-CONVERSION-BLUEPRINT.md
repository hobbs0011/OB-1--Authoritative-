## The Organic Sales Funnel

### What Actually Happened

1. Parent searches for English tutor
2. Finds Michael (accidentally)
3. First message on Messenger
4. Relationship building (ask about kid, day, needs)
5. Anti-center positioning (name the enemy)
6. Story proof (7yo transformation)
7. AI value-add (daily practice, parent dashboard)
8. Pricing options (2x/wk or 1x + AI)
9. Free eval offer (removes all risk)
10. Booked session (same day)
11. Written evaluation report (24hrs)
12. Ongoing client

### How to Replicate It

**Stage 1 — Be Findable:**
- Lakeview Community Group post
- Zalo OA (oa.zalo.me)
- Google Business Profile
- LinkedIn profile
- Facebook (personal Gmail, slow build)
- Landing page on naturalenglishtraining.net

**Stage 2 — Convert in Chat (proven sequence):**
1. Respond fast (same evening)
2. Ask about the kid first
3. Name the enemy (centers)
4. Tell the 7yo story
5. Mention the AI app
6. Give pricing options
7. Offer free eval
8. Book it

**Stage 3 — Multiply Through AI:**
- OBI handles daily practice → parent sees progress → tells other parents
- Written eval reports are shareable social proof
- Each client = organic referral

## The Core Lesson

Michael's sales DNA is already elite. The problem was never selling — it was being findable. One accidental discovery converted in a single conversation. The entire strategy is: **make the accidents happen on purpose.**

Revenue-first doctrine: AI is the multiplier, not the product. Michael is the product. OBI makes Michael scale.
