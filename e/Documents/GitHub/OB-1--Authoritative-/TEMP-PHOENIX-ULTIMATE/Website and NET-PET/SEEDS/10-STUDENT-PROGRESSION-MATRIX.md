> **Status**: IMMUTABLE / LOCKED

## Tier Definitions

**MOUSE**
- Refuses to speak without direct prompting
- Translates internally (3-5 second delay)
- Single words or nods
- Shuts down when corrected publicly

**CUB**
- Attempts sentences even when uncertain
- Pushes through grammar errors
- Asks clarifying questions in English
- Volunteers answers 30-50% unprompted

**TIGER**
- Initiates English conversation without prompting
- Creates English moments outside class
- Uses idioms and slang contextually
- Corrects own errors mid-sentence
- Encourages other students

## Promotion Criteria

**Mouse → Cub**: 5+ unprompted full sentences in one session, two consecutive weeks. Error rate irrelevant.

**Cub → Tiger**: Spontaneous English outside classroom, self-correction in real-time, 3+ idioms correctly in context over two sessions.

## Demotion Protocol
- Self-initiation drops below 20% for 3 sessions
- Reverts to single-word answers after Cub-level
- Attendance drops below 2/week for 2 weeks
- Review is private. Face-saving is law.

## Timeline
- Mouse → Cub: 4-8 weeks
- Cub → Tiger: 8-16 weeks
- Tiger maintenance: ongoing via Tiger Circle
