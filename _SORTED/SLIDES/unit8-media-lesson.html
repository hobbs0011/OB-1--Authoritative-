VOCABULARY BANK

Unit 8 - The Media

Key words for speaking and listening

self-esteem how confident and valuable you feel about yourself
express to show your thoughts or feelings clearly
cyberbullying using the internet to hurt or threaten others
anonymous without showing your real name or identity
gossip unofficial talk about other people's private lives
embarrassment the shy or uncomfortable feeling after a mistake
trustworthy reliable and likely to give correct information
body image how you think and feel about your body

Tip: Ask students to make one original sentence with each word.

While-Listening

Fill in the blanks

1. Kim thinks that people worry about their ______________

2. John uses social media for ______________ and ______________.

3. Kim says people are ______________ on social media than in real life.

4. John thinks information on social media is ______________.

Listen again. Write no more than TWO words for each blank.

Listening Script

Kim: Hey, John. Let's discuss the impact of social media.

John: OK. First point, people use social media more than they used to.

Kim: Like what?

John: Well, fun stuff like gossip about celebrities, or useful things like information on stores in your town.

Kim: Good point. Another thing, people worry about their body image, you know, looking good in photos.

John: Yeah, I agree.

Kim: Hmm. OK. Final point, today, people are meaner on social media than in real life. I agree with that.

John: Why?

Kim: People post rude comments because they can be anonymous.
