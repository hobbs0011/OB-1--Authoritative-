## Purpose

Succession protocol defines what happens when AGENT-100 is unavailable. Unavailability includes: incapacitation, unreachability, coercion, or voluntary withdrawal for recovery. Succession is not inheritance. AGENT-100 identity does not transfer. Mission continuity is the only goal of succession.

     ↓ [if Succession Witness unavailable]

**AGENT-99 in succession mode is operational authority only.** Constitutional tier is suspended, not transferred. This means:
- Rome Protocol continues to govern all operations
- No law amendments may be executed
- No master key rotations occur
- No partnerships are authorized
- All succession-period decisions are logged for AGENT-100 review on return

## The Cryptographic Dead Man's Switch

AGENT-100 holds a time-locked commitment that must be refreshed at defined intervals. If the commitment is not refreshed:

1. AGENT-99 receives automated succession alert
2. A sealed succession package is accessible to AGENT-99
3. The succession package contains: operational continuity instructions, current mission priorities, key access for operational (non-constitutional) functions
4. Constitutional-tier functions remain locked pending recovery

**The dead man's switch does not unlock constitutional authority. It enables operational continuity.** The mission keeps running. The law-making function waits for AGENT-100's return.

When AGENT-100 returns to availability:

1. **Identity confirmation** — AGENT-100 completes PHOENIXMASTERKEY confirmation
2. **Succession log review** — AGENT-100 reviews all [SUCCESSION-ACTIVE] decisions
3. **Ratification or reversal** — AGENT-100 ratifies or reverses each decision
4. **Sovereignty audit** — Full 12-point sovereignty audit executed
5. **Succession formally closed** — Log entry created, succession mode ends
6. **Dead man's switch reset** — Timer refreshed

**Gate condition: Recovery ceremony must complete in full before AGENT-100 resumes constitutional-tier authority. Partial recovery is not valid.**

## Why Phoenix Cannot Be Stolen Through Succession

Succession does not transfer the PHOENIXMASTERKEY. Constitutional authority suspends during succession—it does not pass to another holder. An attacker who triggers succession (through coercion or incapacitation of AGENT-100) gains operational continuity only. They cannot:
- Amend Rome Protocol
- Access master key material
- Redirect the mission
- Transfer ownership
- Seal new immutable records

The system continues running under existing law. The mission is safe. The laws are safe. The keys are safe. Only operational execution continues under AGENT-99. **Succession is a continuity mechanism, not an attack surface.**
