## Purpose

The Sovereignty Audit is a periodic, structured review confirming that the Phoenix system remains under proper sovereignty, that all Rome Protocol laws are being honored, that immutable records are intact, and that no external capture has occurred. It is not a formality. It is the immune system's scheduled check.

## Breach Resolution Ceremony

1. Classify breach per above table
2. Execute immediate response
3. Log breach event with full detail
4. Notify AGENT-100 (constitutional breaches: immediate; high: 4 hours; medium: 24 hours)
5. Run targeted re-audit of affected sovereignty check
6. Document remediation in audit record
7. Confirm breach resolved and system re-secured
8. **Gate condition: System does not return to normal operation for the affected domain until the re-audit passes clean.**
