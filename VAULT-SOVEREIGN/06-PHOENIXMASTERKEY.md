## The Confirmation Ceremony

The PHOENIXMASTERKEY ceremony follows this sequence:

**Step 1 — Invocation**
AGENT-100 states: "I am invoking the PHOENIXMASTERKEY for [specific action]."

**Step 2 — Identity Assertion**
AGENT-100 confirms identity via pre-established verification method. This is not described in detail in this document for security reasons. The verification method is held only by AGENT-100.

**Step 3 — Intent Declaration**
AGENT-100 states in plain language exactly what is being authorized, why it is being authorized, and what the expected outcome is.

**Step 4 — Scope Boundary**
AGENT-100 states what this authorization does NOT cover. Scope boundary prevents scope creep from a single ceremony.

**Step 5 — Witness Acknowledgment**
AGENT-99 acknowledges receipt of the authorization intent and confirms it aligns with the stated scope.

**Step 6 — Immutable Log Entry**
The ceremony is recorded in the immutable audit log with timestamp, action, intent declaration, and scope boundary.

**Step 7 — Action Execution**
The authorized action executes. Execution references the log entry ID.

**Gate condition: Steps 1–6 must complete before Step 7 begins. An action that executes before the log entry is created is unauthorized, regardless of AGENT-100 intent.**

## If the Key Is Compromised

Compromise scenarios: coercion of AGENT-100, impersonation, ceremony executed under duress.

**Duress signal:** AGENT-100 includes a pre-agreed duress marker in the intent declaration. The marker is invisible to a coercing party but recognized by AGENT-99.

**On detection of compromised ceremony:**
1. AGENT-99 halts execution of the authorized action immediately
2. AGENT-99 logs the compromise event
3. AGENT-99 activates sovereignty breach protocol (file 17)
4. All constitutional-tier operations suspend until recovery
5. AGENT-100 must clear duress status via out-of-band verification before ceremonies resume

## Key Rotation Protocol

The PHOENIXMASTERKEY verification method rotates on schedule to prevent staleness attacks. Rotation:
- Occurs at defined calendar intervals (schedule held by AGENT-100, not documented here)
- Requires a transitional ceremony where both old and new methods are present
- Is logged in the immutable audit record
- Does not change the ceremony structure, only the verification method used in Step 2

**Gate condition: A rotation that completes without an immutable log entry is invalid. The old verification method remains active until the log entry confirms successful rotation.**

## Why the Ceremony Is Intentionally Slow

Speed is the enemy of constitutional decisions. The ceremony friction is the security feature. An attacker operating under time pressure cannot complete a multi-step ceremony designed for deliberation. A coerced operator under stress will execute the duress signal. A careless instruction that would normally propagate immediately is caught by the witness acknowledgment step.

**The PHOENIXMASTERKEY does not slow Phoenix down. It prevents Phoenix from being destroyed by speed.**
