About Michael Hobbs

Canadian educator. Systems thinker. AI-augmented operator.

Michael Hobbs is the founder of Natural English Training, a private English business built in Ho Chi Minh City around one core idea: confidence comes before polish. NET is not a faceless center. It is a direct, human-led system designed to move students from silence to power.

Who he is

A teacher with a systems brain

Michael describes himself as the idea guy who is also the builder. He does not come from a traditional coding background; he uses AI as an extension of judgment, speed, and execution.

What NET is

The anti-English-center

Natural English Training is built to avoid the usual center problems: overhead theater, textbook bloat, and students who can pass grammar exercises but freeze in real conversation.

What matters

Human truth over corporate varnish

The voice of the brand is intentionally direct. Students are not shamed for mistakes. They are calibrated, coached, and pushed toward real output.

Operating model

Built from scratch, then fortified with AI

NET is intentionally lean. Michael built the business as a private English operation with AI helping across curriculum, scheduling, diagnostics, marketing, and internal systems work.

The founder story matters because the brand is not meant to sound like a generic education startup. Michael built NET in Vietnam, shaped it around direct teaching experience, and kept the business close enough to the work that the message never drifts away from the student experience.

That same logic is now expanding into the website itself: one place for the assessment, one place for the founder story, one place for NET-PET-AI, and a shared visual language that ties the whole stack together.
