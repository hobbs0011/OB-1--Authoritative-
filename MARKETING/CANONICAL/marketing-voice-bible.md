## Voice Rules (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

### ✅ DO

1. **Write like you're chatting over the fence** — casual, warm, real
2. **Use "tôi" (I) and "bạn" (you)** — direct, personal address
3. **Share observations, not pitches** — "I noticed a lot of kids here struggle with..." not "We offer world-class..."
4. **Include both kids AND adults** — two lanes, never just one
5. **Lead with free** — Stage 1 (Free Evaluation) is always the CTA
6. **Show genuine care** — "I want to help your kid read confidently" not "Enroll now for 20% off"
7. **Use local context** — Lakeview City, District 2, the neighbourhood, the park, the pool area
8. **Be specific** — "Jolly Phonics" not "our proprietary method", "business English for your Zoom calls" not "professional communication skills"
9. **Acknowledge Vietnamese context** — "10 years studying English at school but still can't speak" is a shared experience, not a shame point
10. **End with an invitation, not a command** — "Message me if you want to chat about it" not "Register NOW"

### ❌ DON'T

1. **No corporate language** — "solutions", "leverage", "optimize", "world-class", "cutting-edge"
2. **No urgency pressure** — "limited spots", "only 3 days left", "prices going up"
3. **No comparison attacks** — never name or trash other schools/centers
4. **No perfect English expectations** — never imply someone's English is "bad"
5. **No jargon without explanation** — if using "Jolly Phonics", briefly say what it is
6. **No stock photos** — use real photos of Michael, the classroom, student work (with permission)
7. **No emoji overload** — 1-2 emojis max per post, used naturally
8. **No hashtag spam** — 0-2 relevant hashtags maximum
9. **No Google Translate Vietnamese** — must read naturally to a native speaker
10. **No "we"** — Michael is one person, not a company. Use "tôi" (I)

## Dual Language Strategy

### Vietnamese (Lakeview Facebook Groups)
- **Primary channel:** 8 Lakeview City apartment groups (~40K combined)
- **Tone:** Slightly more formal than English (Vietnamese social norms), but still warm
- **Address:** Use "anh/chị" (older brother/sister) for parents — respectful but friendly
- **Key phrases to use:**
  - "Tôi là hàng xóm của bạn ở Lakeview" (I'm your neighbour in Lakeview)
  - "Đánh giá miễn phí" (Free evaluation)
  - "Không bắt buộc gì cả" (No obligation at all)
  - "Nhắn tin cho tôi" (Message me)
- **Key phrases to AVOID:**
  - "Trung tâm Anh ngữ" (English language center) — we're NOT a center
  - "Đăng ký ngay" (Register now) — too salesy
  - "Ưu đãi" (Promotion/deal) — not our style
  - "Chuyên nghiệp" (Professional) when describing ourselves — let results speak

### English (Michael's Personal Profile)
- **Tone:** Very casual, storytelling, personal reflection
- **Address:** First person, like a blog post or personal update
- **Share teaching moments, cultural observations, wins
- **Less direct CTA** — the English posts build brand, the Vietnamese posts drive action

## The 4-Stage Funnel Voice Map

| Stage | Voice Goal | Key Phrase | Anti-Pattern |
|-------|-----------|-----------|--------------|
| **Post → DM** | Curiosity + trust | "Nhắn tin cho tôi nếu muốn tìm hiểu thêm" | "Đăng ký ngay!" |
| **DM → Free Eval (Stage 1)** | Warm, zero pressure | "Tôi muốn biết con bạn đang ở đâu" | "Book a session" |
| **Eval → Report (Stage 2)** | Helpful, informative | "Đây là những gì tôi phát hiện" | "Here's what you need to buy" |
| **Report + Free Class (Stage 3)** | Demonstrate value | "Hãy để con bạn thử một buổi" | "Sign up for our program" |
| **Close (Stage 4)** | Gentle, clear options | "Nếu bạn muốn tiếp tục..." | "Limited spots available!" |

## Content Archetypes (Approved Post Types)

### 1. The Observation Post
> "I've been living in Lakeview for 2 years now and I notice a lot of parents asking about English for their kids..."
- **Why it works:** Opens with shared experience, not a pitch

### 2. The Teaching Moment Post
> "Yesterday one of my students went from 'I can't' to reading a full sentence out loud. Jolly Phonics does that..."
- **Why it works:** Shows results through story, not claims

### 3. The Common Problem Post
> "Why do Vietnamese kids study English for 10 years but still can't hold a conversation?"
- **Why it works:** Addresses a pain point everyone knows, positions you as someone who understands

### 4. The Free Offer Post
> "I'm offering free English evaluations for kids (and adults!) in Lakeview. No strings attached..."
- **Why it works:** Direct, generous, zero pressure

### 5. The Before/After Post
> "6 months ago, this student would go silent when asked a question in English. Last week, they presented in front of their class..."
- **Why it works:** Transformation story = most powerful social proof

## Anti-Patterns Watchlist

| Anti-Pattern | Example | Fix |
|--------------|---------|-----|
| Corporate buzzword | "Unlock your child's potential" | "Help your kid feel confident speaking English" |
| Pressure close | "Only 5 spots left!" | "Message me whenever you're ready" |
| Self-aggrandizing | "Award-winning methodology" | "This is what I've seen work in 15 years" |
| Vague promise | "Transform your English skills" | "Read confidently in 3 months using Jolly Phonics" |
| Tone-deaf to VN context | "Your English is bad" | "Most people study English for years but don't get to practice speaking" |
| Brand speak | "At NET, we believe..." | "I've learned that..." |
| Hashtag spam | "#English #Learning #Kids #Vietnam #HCMC #D2" | "#LakeviewCity" (max) |
