### 4.2 The Four Core Principles

**PRINCIPLE 1: LEARN LIKE A NATIVE**
- Method: Model how children learn their first language
- Process: Listen → Mimic → Natural development

- Vietnamese: Học như trẻ em học ngôn ngữ đầu tiên

**PRINCIPLE 3: BREAK THE GRAMMAR BARRIER**
- You spoke Vietnamese before grammar rules
- Communication first, grammar comes naturally
- No drilling complex grammar rules
- Vietnamese: Phá vỡ rào cản ngữ pháp

**PRINCIPLE 4: MASTER THE SOUNDS (JOLLY PHONICS)**
- System: Jolly Phonics (developed in England)
- 42 English sounds + non-phonetic words
- Simple, effective system

## §13 FAQ BANK (BILINGUAL — READY FOR WEBSITE/MARKETING)

**Q1: What is comprehensible input?**
A: Listening to English you understand 60%+. You don't understand every word, but you get the main idea and situation.
VN: Nghe tiếng Anh mà bạn có thể hiểu ít nhất 60%.

**Q2: Why don't we study grammar first?**
A: You learned Vietnamese without studying grammar first! Children speak before grammar rules. Grammar comes naturally through listening and speaking.
VN: Trẻ em nói trước khi học quy tắc ngữ pháp.

**Q3: What is "learning by association"?**
A: Your brain connects new words with situations, feelings, and other words. We teach words in sentences and real conversations.
VN: Não bạn kết nối từ mới với tình huống, cảm xúc và các từ khác.

**Q4: How can I make English fun?**
A: Choose topics you LOVE — podcasts, YouTube, shows, music. When you enjoy content, your brain learns faster.
VN: Chọn chủ đề bạn YÊU THÍCH!

**Q5: What if I'm afraid of making mistakes?**
A: We create a SAFE ENVIRONMENT where mistakes are celebrated. Every native speaker made thousands of mistakes while learning.
VN: Sai lầm là bằng chứng bạn đang cố gắng và phát triển.
