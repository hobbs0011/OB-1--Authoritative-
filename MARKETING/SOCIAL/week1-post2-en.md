I've been teaching English in Saigon for a while now, and I see the same two things every single day:

**With adults:**
Vietnamese people are **way better at English than they think.**

I use Jolly Phonics — teaching kids to **hear and speak first**, the way children's brains naturally absorb language. No copying from the board. No memorising textbooks. Kids **pronounce correctly from the very first lessons** because I teach from the root sounds, not from a textbook.
