# Stage 3 — Free Class + Report Delivery Script
**Version:** 1.0
**Created:** 2026-03-31
**Voice:** Personal neighbour — NOT corporate
**Applies to:** Kids AND Adults

---

## Overview

Stage 3 is where the magic happens. You've already done the evaluation (Stage 1) and written the report (Stage 2). Now you sit down with them, walk through what you found, show them exactly how you teach, and let them *experience* the difference. No hard sell. Just show them.

By the end of Stage 3, they should be thinking: "I want more of this."

---

## Pre-Session Setup (5 minutes before)

### For Kids Sessions
- [ ] Print or have report ready on screen
- [ ] Prepare 2-3 Jolly Phonics sound cards relevant to their gaps
- [ ] Have a fun warm-up activity ready (flashcard game, picture match)
- [ ] Set up the teaching space — colourful, relaxed, not intimidating
- [ ] Have a small reward ready (sticker, stamp card)

### For Adult Sessions
- [ ] Print or have report ready on screen
- [ ] Prepare a short conversation topic based on their level and interests
- [ ] Have 3-4 vocabulary items ready that match their real-life needs
- [ ] Coffee/tea ready — keep it casual
- [ ] Quiet space, no distractions

---

## The Session Flow (45-60 minutes total)

### Part 1 — Report Walkthrough (10-15 minutes)

**Goal:** Show them you actually listened. Build trust. No jargon.

#### Opening (Neighbour Tone)

**For Parents (Kids):**
> "Cảm ơn chị/anh đã quay lại! Em đã hoàn thành báo cáo cho bé [name] rồi. Mình cùng xem nhé — em sẽ giải thích mọi thứ đơn giản thôi, không có gì phức tạp đâu."
>
> *English version:* "Thanks for coming back! I've finished [name]'s report. Let's go through it together — I'll keep it simple, nothing complicated."

**For Adults:**
> "Cảm ơn bạn đã quay lại! Mình đã viết xong báo cáo về trình độ của bạn rồi. Cùng xem nhé — mình sẽ nói thẳng luôn, không vòng vo đâu."
>
> *English version:* "Thanks for coming back! I've finished writing up your assessment. Let's look at it together — I'll be straight with you, no beating around the bush."

#### Walking Through the Report

**Do:**
- Point to specific examples from THEIR evaluation: "Remember when you said [X]? That tells me [Y]."
- Use their actual words and mistakes as teaching moments (gently)
- Highlight what they're ALREADY good at first — Mouse to Tiger
- Show the gap clearly but without making them feel bad
- Use the action plan to show a clear path forward

**Don't:**
- Read the report word-for-word at them
- Use grammar terminology they won't understand
- Compare them to other students
- Rush through it — let them ask questions
- Make the gaps sound impossible to fix

#### Key Phrases (Neighbour Style)

> "Bé [name] thực ra rất thông minh — nhìn này, bé đã biết [X] rồi. Chỉ cần sửa mấy chỗ nhỏ này thôi."
>
> *"[Name] is actually really smart — look, they already know [X]. We just need to fix these small things."*

> "Phát âm của bạn tốt hơn bạn nghĩ đấy. Mấy lỗi này rất phổ biến — hầu hết người Việt đều mắc. Sửa được hết."
>
> *"Your pronunciation is better than you think. These mistakes are super common — almost every Vietnamese speaker makes them. Totally fixable."*

---

### Part 2 — Live Teaching Demo (20-25 minutes)

**Goal:** Let them FEEL the difference. This is where they fall in love with the method.

#### For Kids — The Jolly Phonics Magic Show

Pick 2-3 sounds from their report's gap analysis. Teach them RIGHT NOW.

**Script:**
> "OK, bây giờ em sẽ dạy bé [name] luôn nhé — chị/anh xem bé học nhanh thế nào."
>
> *"OK, let me teach [name] right now — watch how fast they pick this up."*

**The Demo:**
1. **Sound introduction** (2 min per sound) — Use Jolly Phonics actions. Make it physical. Make it fun.
2. **Blending practice** (3 min) — Put sounds together into simple words. Celebrate every attempt.
3. **Quick game** (5 min) — Sound hunt, picture match, or silly sentence building. Kid should be laughing.
4. **Parent moment** — Turn to parent: "Thấy chưa? Bé vừa học được 3 âm trong 10 phút. Bình thường ở trường phải mất mấy tuần." / *"See? They just learned 3 sounds in 10 minutes. At school this usually takes weeks."*

**The Kill Shot (for parents watching):**
> "Đó là lý do em không dạy theo kiểu cũ. Phương pháp này dựa trên nghiên cứu — bé NGHE âm trước, rồi mới học chữ. Ngược lại với cách dạy ở trường."
>
> *"That's why I don't teach the old way. This method is research-based — they HEAR the sounds first, then learn the letters. It's the opposite of how most schools do it."*

#### For Adults — The Conversation Unlock

Pick a topic they mentioned caring about (work presentations, travel, daily conversations). Teach them something useful RIGHT NOW.

**Script:**
> "OK, mình thử luôn nhé. Bạn nói muốn tự tin hơn khi [their goal]. Mình sẽ dạy bạn một kỹ thuật ngay bây giờ."
>
> *"OK, let's try something right now. You said you want to be more confident when [their goal]. I'll teach you a technique right now."*

**The Demo:**
1. **Real scenario practice** (5 min) — Role-play a situation from their real life
2. **Error correction live** (5 min) — Fix 2-3 things in real-time, explain WHY not just WHAT
3. **Pronunciation drill** (5 min) — Pick their top 3 pronunciation issues from the report, drill them
4. **Before/after** (5 min) — Redo the same conversation from step 1. Let them HEAR their own improvement.

**The Kill Shot:**
> "Nghe thấy sự khác biệt chưa? Đó mới là 15 phút. Tưởng tượng sau 3 tháng."
>
> *"Can you hear the difference? That was just 15 minutes. Imagine after 3 months."*

---

### Part 3 — The Bridge (5-10 minutes)

**Goal:** Soft transition. NOT a hard close. Plant the seed, let it grow.

#### What to Say

**For Parents:**
> "Đó là cách em dạy. Mỗi buổi học đều như vậy — có mục tiêu rõ ràng, vui, và bé thấy kết quả ngay. Em đã ghi ra lộ trình cho bé trong báo cáo rồi — nếu chị/anh muốn, mình có thể bắt đầu tuần sau."
>
> *"That's how I teach. Every lesson is like this — clear goals, fun, and they see results right away. I've laid out a path for [name] in the report — if you're interested, we can start next week."*

**For Adults:**
> "Đó là phong cách dạy của mình. Không phải ngồi học sách giáo khoa — mình tập trung vào thứ bạn THỰC SỰ cần. Lộ trình mình ghi trong báo cáo rồi — bạn xem rồi cho mình biết nhé."
>
> *"That's my teaching style. No textbook grinding — we focus on what you ACTUALLY need. The path is in the report — have a look and let me know."*

#### What NOT to Say
- ❌ "So would you like to sign up?" (too salesy)
- ❌ "I have a special discount if you sign up today" (not neighbour)
- ❌ "Most of my students see results in X weeks" (corporate guarantee)
- ❌ Any pressure language

#### What TO Do
- ✅ Give them the report to take home
- ✅ Say "take your time, no rush"
- ✅ Mention you only have a few spots open (if true — never lie)
- ✅ Offer to answer any questions via Messenger/Zalo
- ✅ If they seem ready, naturally flow into scheduling: "Bạn muốn bắt đầu khi nào?" / *"When would you like to start?"*

## Post-Session (Within 2 hours)

1. **Send a thank-you message** via Messenger/Zalo:
   > "Cảm ơn chị/anh hôm nay nhé! Bé [name] học nhanh lắm — em rất vui. Nếu có câu hỏi gì thì cứ nhắn em bất cứ lúc nào."
   >
   > *"Thanks for today! [Name] picks things up so fast — I really enjoyed it. If you have any questions, just message me anytime."*

2. **Wait 24-48 hours** — If no response, follow up once:
   > "Chào chị/anh! Chị/anh đã xem lại báo cáo chưa? Nếu cần em giải thích thêm gì thì cứ nói nhé."
   >
   > *"Hi! Have you had a chance to look over the report? If you need me to explain anything, just say the word."*

3. **If they say yes** → Move to Stage 4 (Close Script)
4. **If they say no or ghost** → No pressure. Add to "check back in 2 weeks" list. They might come around later.

## Common Situations

### Parent wants to watch the whole lesson
> "Tất nhiên rồi! Chị/anh ngồi đây xem luôn nhé. Em muốn chị/anh thấy cách em dạy."
> *"Of course! Sit right here and watch. I want you to see how I teach."*

### Kid is shy and won't participate
> "Không sao đâu — mấy buổi đầu nhiều bé cũng vậy. Mình chơi trước đi, học sau."
> *"That's totally normal — lots of kids are like this at first. Let's play first, learn later."*
> → Switch to a game immediately. Lower the pressure. Let the kid warm up.

### Adult says "my English is too bad"
> "Mình nghe câu này suốt — và lần nào cũng sai. Bạn biết nhiều hơn bạn nghĩ. Vừa rồi bạn nói [quote something they said correctly]. Đó, tốt rồi đấy."
> *"I hear this all the time — and it's always wrong. You know more than you think. Just now you said [quote]. See? That's good."*

### They ask about price during Stage 3
> "Em sẽ nói chi tiết hơn sau nhé — hôm nay mình tập trung vào bé/bạn trước. Nhưng nói chung là em có mấy gói khác nhau, phù hợp với nhiều ngân sách."
> *"I'll go into detail on that after — today let's focus on [name/you] first. But generally I have a few different packages for different budgets."*
> → Don't avoid it, but redirect gently. Stage 4 handles pricing.

## Success Metrics

After Stage 3, the prospect should:
- [ ] Understand their current level and gaps (from report walkthrough)
- [ ] Have EXPERIENCED the teaching method firsthand
- [ ] Feel confident that improvement is possible
- [ ] Have a clear picture of what lessons would look like
- [ ] Feel zero pressure — just informed and excited
- [ ] Know how to reach you (Messenger/Zalo)
