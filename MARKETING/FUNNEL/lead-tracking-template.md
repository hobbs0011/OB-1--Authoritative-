## Follow-Up Schedule

| Stage | Follow-Up Timing | Method | Script |
|-------|-----------------|--------|--------|
| Stage 0 (no response to DM) | +24h, +72h, +7d | Messenger | Gentle check-in, no pressure |
| Stage 1 (eval booked, not done) | -24h reminder, +48h if missed | Zalo/Messenger | Reschedule offer |
| Stage 2 (report sent, no response) | +48h | Messenger | "Did you get the report? Happy to discuss" |
| Stage 3 (free class done, no close) | +24h, +7d | Messenger/Zalo | "How did [name] enjoy the class?" |
| Stage 4 (close attempt, undecided) | +3d, +7d, +14d | Messenger | Share relevant success story |

### Follow-Up Voice Rules
- Always neighbour tone — never salesy
- Maximum 3 follow-ups per lead per stage
- If no response after 3 attempts → mark as "cold" → revisit in 30 days
- Never make anyone feel pressured or guilty
