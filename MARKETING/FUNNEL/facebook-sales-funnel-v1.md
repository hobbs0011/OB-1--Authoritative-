## Michael's 4-Stage Sales Funnel (CANONICAL)

### Stage 1: Free Evaluation
- **What:** Level test, Jolly Phonics proficiency assessment, proposed action plan
- **Cost to prospect:** FREE — everything in this stage is free
- **Goal:** Assess the student's current level and show Michael's teaching approach
- **Trigger:** DM response from Facebook post → book free evaluation
- **Deliverable:** Verbal assessment + proposed learning path
- **Duration:** ~30 minutes
- **Location:** Lakeview City (in-person) or online

### Stage 2: Free Report
- **What:** Written report of findings from the Stage 1 evaluation
- **Cost to prospect:** FREE
- **Goal:** Provide tangible, professional value before asking for any commitment
- **Deliverable:** Written document covering:
  - Current English level (reading, writing, speaking, listening)
  - Jolly Phonics proficiency (for kids) / pronunciation gaps (for adults)
  - Specific areas for improvement
  - Recommended learning path and timeline
- **Tone:** Professional but warm — like a neighbour who happens to be an expert

### Stage 3: Delivered with Free Class
- **What:** Report delivered alongside a free trial lesson
- **Cost to prospect:** FREE
- **Goal:** Let the student EXPERIENCE Michael's teaching method firsthand
- **Flow:**
  1. Review report together (5 min)
  2. Live teaching demo using Michael Method (20 min)
  3. Student experiences the "speak loud and wrong > silent and right" approach
  4. Natural transition point — student has now felt the difference
- **Key principle:** Mouse → Tiger in action. Permission over perfection. Anti-shame.

### Stage 4: Close
- **What:** Convert to paying student
- **Cost to prospect:** Pricing presented here
- **Pricing (locked):**
  - Private 1-on-1: Based on frequency and goals
  - Small group (3-5 professionals): Business English focus
  - Kids classes: Jolly Phonics foundation + conversation
- **Objection handling:** Neighbour style, not sales pressure
- **Key objections to address:**
  - "Too expensive" → compare to language centers, highlight 1-on-1 attention
  - "No time" → flexible scheduling, lives in Lakeview
  - "My English is too bad" → that's exactly who Michael helps
  - "My kid won't like it" → no red pen, games, Tiger energy
- **Close style:** "Want to keep going? Here's how we'd work together."
