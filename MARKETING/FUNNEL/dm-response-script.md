# DM Response Script — Facebook Funnel Stage 1 Entry

> **Purpose:** When someone messages after seeing a Lakeview Facebook post
> **Goal:** Book them for FREE Evaluation (Stage 1)
> **Voice:** Warm neighbour, NOT corporate sales
> **Two lanes:** Adults (messaging for themselves) + Parents (messaging about their kid)
> **Created:** 2026-03-31 Run 163
> **Canon:** Michael's 4-Stage Funnel — Free Eval → Free Report → Free Class → Close

---

## 🔑 Key Principles

1. **Respond within 1 hour** — Facebook algorithm rewards fast responses with "Very Responsive" badge
2. **Mirror their language** — If they write in Vietnamese, respond in Vietnamese
3. **Never sell** — You're a neighbour offering to help, not a salesperson
4. **One goal only:** Book the Free Evaluation
5. **Keep it short** — Nobody reads long DMs
6. **Ask before telling** — Find out their situation before prescribing

Bạn rảnh khi nào tuần này? Mình linh hoạt lắm, sáng chiều tối đều được. 😊

Tuyệt vời! Vậy mình hẹn bạn [ngày/giờ] nhé. Mình sẽ gửi địa chỉ chính xác trước buổi gặp.

Nếu có gì thay đổi cứ nhắn mình nhé — mình ở ngay đây trong Lakeview thôi! 🏠

## ⚠️ Anti-Patterns (NEVER DO THIS)

| ❌ Don't | ✅ Do Instead |
|-----------|--------------|
| Send pricing in first message | Ask about their situation first |
| Use corporate language ("We offer comprehensive solutions...") | Talk like a neighbour ("Hey! Thanks for reaching out") |
| Send long paragraphs | Keep messages short, 2-4 sentences max |
| Push for commitment | Offer the free evaluation naturally |
| Mention Natural English Training brand name | Just be "Michael, your neighbour" |
| Copy-paste the same response to everyone | Mirror their specific situation |
| Respond after 24 hours | Respond within 1 hour |
| Talk about methodology in detail | Save that for the evaluation |

## 📋 Script Notes
- **Voice check:** Read every response aloud. Does it sound like you're talking to your neighbour over the fence? If not, rewrite.
- **Emoji usage:** Light — 1-2 per message max. 👋😊🏠 are the core three.
- **Response time:** Facebook rewards pages that respond within 1 hour with a "Very Responsive" badge.
- **Never mention pricing until Stage 4** — the entire funnel is about building trust first.
- **Adapt, don't copy-paste** — Use these as templates, personalise every response to the specific person.
