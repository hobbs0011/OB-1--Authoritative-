## 🧠 Gemini's Pedagogical Response (Derivative — NOT Michael's voice)

### Detective Mode vs. Flow Mode Framework

Gemini named what Michael described:
- **Detective Mode** — brain treats every unfamiliar word as a high-stakes puzzle. Hits "heck," stops, crashes.
- **Flow Mode** — rides the wave of the sentence, grabs known words (anchors), ignores noise.

### The Anchor Principle (Gemini's Expansion)

- Treat sentences as a **sea of words with anchors**, not a chain where one broken link stops everything
- Unknown word → stress → narrows focus → "deaf" to what follows
- Known words → connect dots → get the gist → stay in flow

### Drills Suggested by Gemini

1. **The Filter Game** — throw nonsense words into common sentences. "Please pass the flibber-gibbet salt." Did you hear 'salt'? Good. Ignore the rest.
2. **The 80/20 Rule** — 80% of words = 100% of meaning. The 20% is flavor text.
3. **Positive Reinforcement** — when confused, ask "What parts DID you recognize?" Prove the anchors were enough.
4. **Minimal Pairs** — Watch vs Wash, Much vs Mush (CH/SH distinction)
