## 3. Teaching Methodology — Method Bible Entries

### 3A. The Anchor Principle (Core Pillar)

**Problem:** Students operate in Detective Mode — treat sentences as chains where one broken link = total freeze.
**Solution:** Shift to Flow Mode — treat conversation as a sea of words. Grab anchors (known words), let noise slide.

**The Drill: Filter Game**
- Throw sentences laced with nonsense/"flibber-gibbet" words
- Ask: "Did you hear 'salt'? Good. Ignore the rest."
- Validate only on anchors caught

**The Metric: 80/20 Rule**
- 80% of words = 100% of meaning
- The 20% missed is flavor text

**The Tactic: Relaxation**
- Stress → Detective Mode (narrow focus)
- Relaxation → Flow Mode (wide focus)
- "Breathe and relax" — a stressed brain is deaf to context

### 3B. The Snowman Protocol

**Definition:** Freeze response. Student goes silent instead of saying "I don't know."
**Root cause:** Detective Mode crash — 30 programs running simultaneously (translation + fear + grammar checks)
**Cure:**
1. Label the behavior ("Frosty the Snowman") to create self-awareness
2. Install "secret code" escape phrases: "I don't know," "No problem," "Can you repeat?"
3. Prove that freezing is worse than any wrong answer

### 3C. Shock and Awe Toolkit

| Tool | Example | Purpose |
|------|---------|--------|
| Hyperbole | "Throw rocks at your window," "34 min 47 sec practice," "1M dong breath-holding bet" | Make rules memorable, test listening |
| Absurdist Roleplay | "Meatitarian," Russia prison, AI's "father" | Keep students off-balance, prevent scripted responses |
| Sarcasm | "Snowman," mocking 6 beers, "chicken fish" | Expose comprehension gaps via face reading |
| Cultural Deconstruction | "Bar" joke, "chop" vocabulary, gender stereotypes | Teach wordplay/double meanings, bridge sarcasm gap |
| AI Antagonism | Calling AI drunk/stupid/racist, father roleplay | Create chaos = unpredictable social cues |

### 3D. Western vs Eastern Humor Map

| Dimension | Western (Michael's Method) | Eastern (Student Baseline) |
|-----------|---------------------------|---------------------------|
| Humor type | Sarcasm, puns, absurdity, shock value | Logic riddles, literal interpretation |
| Example | "A man walks into a bar. Ouch." (double meaning) | "What two things never eaten before breakfast?" (temporal logic) |
| Processing | Ambiguity = funny | Ambiguity = confusion (freeze) |
| Failure mode | N/A | Becomes the Snowman at the business dinner |

**Fix:** Force Western media consumption (The Office) + relentless sarcasm exposure.

### 3E. The Office Protocol

- **Assignment:** Watch full first episode (1 hour)
- **Method:** Character study, not plot summary — "observe the people"
- **Goal:** Learn that meaning lives in what ISN'T said (camera looks, awkward pauses)
- **Pirate Protocol:** Can't find it on YouTube? "We have to become a pirate."
- **Business frame:** Humor = connection = business survival
