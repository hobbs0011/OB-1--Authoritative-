## PHASE 6 — 44 PHONICS + BEGINNER CONTENT (Steps 151–180)

151. Teacher transitions to phonics program: "let me show you the 44 phonics program"
152. Individual sound isolation: each sound gets its own lesson card
153. Sound grouping: sounds taught in related pairs (s/z, sh/ch, th/zh, etc.)
154. Group progression: students master sound groups before moving to full words
155. Sound drills: each sound repeated 5–10 times with increasing speed
156. Minimal pairs: pairs of words that differ by one sound only (ship/sheep, thin/fin)
157. Sound discrimination task: student marks which sound they hear in a word play
158. Sound-to-word: each phonics connects to real example words (sh → she, ship, shop)
159. Sound-in-context: phonics practiced inside real sentences
160. Shadow reading: student reads aloud while audio plays — matching rhythm
161. Speed build-up: start slow, repeat 3×, build up to native speed
162. Phonics flash: rapid review of all 44 sounds — keep and recycle
163. Sound spelling map: each sound maps to 1–3 different spellings
164. Sound mapping: list the 1–3 spelling patterns for that sound
165. Phonics game: "I say the sound — find the word with that sound"
166. Phonics game: student says a word, teacher identifies the sound it starts with
167. Phonics quiz: mini quiz after every sound group — 5–10 questions
168. Phonics score tracked in app: right/wrong tracking
169. Sound mastery threshold: 80% correct = mastered, advance to next sound
170. Failed sound: graphed, revisited in 2 more days
171. Blend sounds → word: bonus step — 3–4 sound blends into a single word
172. Segment word → sounds: student breaks apart a word they already know
173. Sentence reading: phonics practiced inside graded reading sentences
