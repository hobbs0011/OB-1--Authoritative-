Room 3 — Comfort Zone Test

Five real academic and presentation scenarios. Read each passage aloud and record. No preparation — just read it cold, naturally. Michael is listening for how you sound under real conditions. / Năm kịch bản học thuật và thuyết trình thực tế. Đọc to từng đoạn và ghi âm. Không chuẩn bị — đọc tự nhiên ngay lần đầu tiên.

01
Class Presentation Opening
Academic · Formal

"Good morning everyone. My name is [your name], and I'm presenting today on the role of artificial intelligence in modern healthcare. I'll cover three key areas: current clinical applications, ethical considerations, and the future outlook for Vietnam. My research is based on a review of twelve peer-reviewed studies and two case studies from hospitals in Hanoi and Singapore."

Read this passage aloud and record. / Đọc to đoạn văn này và ghi âm.

02
Email to Professor
Academic · Formal Email

"Dear Professor Nguyen, I hope this message finds you well. I am writing to request a brief extension for the Research Methods assignment due this Friday. Unfortunately, I have been managing a family health situation this week that has significantly impacted my study schedule. I would sincerely appreciate the opportunity to submit by next Monday morning. I want to assure you that the work is substantially complete, and I am committed to delivering my strongest effort. Thank you for your understanding and continued support this semester."

Read this passage aloud and record. / Đọc to đoạn văn này và ghi âm.

03
Seminar Opinion Statement
Academic · Persuasive

"In my view, universities in Vietnam are producing graduates who are technically competent but communicatively limited. The issue is not intelligence — our students are hardworking and capable. The gap is that our education system has historically rewarded silent memorisation over active expression. A student can score ninety percent on a grammar examination and still freeze completely in a real seminar discussion. Closing that gap between passive knowledge and active fluency is the most urgent challenge we face."

Read this passage aloud and record. / Đọc to đoạn văn này và ghi âm.

04
Group Project Introduction
Academic · Team Presentation

"Before we begin the presentation, let me briefly introduce our team structure. My role today is to cover the introduction and research methodology. My teammate will walk you through the data analysis and key findings. Our third member will present the conclusions and strategic recommendations. We've structured the session to take approximately fifteen minutes in total, with five minutes reserved for your questions at the end. Please feel free to raise questions as we go."

Read this passage aloud and record. / Đọc to đoạn văn này và ghi âm.

05
Job Interview Answer
Professional · Interview

"I'm currently in my final year studying International Business Administration. Over the past four years I've developed strong skills in financial analysis, cross-cultural communication, and strategic planning. During my internship last summer at a logistics company, I was responsible for preparing English-language reports for international partners and participating in weekly conference calls with clients in Singapore and Japan. That experience confirmed this is the career direction I want to pursue."

Read this passage aloud and record. / Đọc to đoạn văn này và ghi âm.

Room 4 — AI Fluency

The future belongs to people who can use AI fluently — in English. This room assesses where you are and where you can go. / Tương lai thuộc về những người có thể dùng AI thành thạo — bằng tiếng Anh. Phòng này đánh giá bạn đang ở đâu và có thể đi đến đâu.

Part 1
Written Response / Trả lời viết
80–120 words

Describe how you currently use AI tools in your studies or daily life. If you haven't started yet, describe what you know about AI tools and whether you think they would help you academically. / Mô tả cách bạn đang dùng AI trong học tập hoặc cuộc sống. Nếu chưa dùng, hãy mô tả những gì bạn biết về AI và liệu bạn nghĩ nó có hữu ích không.

Part 2
Spoken Response / Trả lời nói
60–90 seconds

Record your answer to this question: How would fluent English + AI skills change your career opportunities in Vietnam over the next five years? / Ghi âm câu trả lời: Tiếng Anh thành thạo + kỹ năng AI sẽ thay đổi cơ hội nghề nghiệp của bạn ở Việt Nam như thế nào trong 5 năm tới?
