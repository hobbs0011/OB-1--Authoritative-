The Language Advantage

In Vietnam today, English is no longer just a school subject — it is an advantage. Young people who speak English with confidence earn more, travel more freely, and access opportunities that others cannot reach. But most students spend years studying grammar rules and still freeze the moment they need to speak.

Why? Because schools teach English as a code to crack, not a voice to find. The students who break through are the ones who stopped waiting to be perfect and started speaking anyway. They made mistakes, got corrected, and kept going. That is the only path.
