The Story

From Calgary to the World

A journey through business, sales, recruitment, education, and AI — forged across four continents.

The Foundation

Grew up in a traditional Christian household and was heavily involved in youth programs. Learned at a very young age how to communicate and work with children of all ages — a skill that would define the next decade of my career.

Business & The Foundation

Background in Business / Marketing / Training across Canada, England and Australia before teaching. Built the foundation through commercial insurance, marketing, life insurance, and international recruiting — mastering sales and public speaking across four continents.

America → England → Australia → Vietnam

Extensive sales and public speaking training across America, England, Australia, and Vietnam. Moved to England to continue in life insurance, which led to a year in Australia managing recruiting for an Israeli company providing cutting-edge tech to Aussie farms.

The Recruiter & Team Builder

Professional recruiter and team builder across moderators, cyber security, marketing, and dev teams. Grew investor community from 5 to 20,000. Launched 4 tokens, bringing in over $20 million in investment through a unique marketing strategy of paying investors to spread the project — versus pump-and-dump influencers.

Les Brown & The Stage

Personally selected in my early 20s to be part of one of the world's top motivational speakers, Les Brown. This is where I honed the voice and presence that would later captivate thousands of students.

Vietnam & The Classroom

5 years teaching experience in Vietnam, with thousands of students ranging from 1 year old to 70. Trained in Montessori methods, Cambridge, and taught IELTS to grade 12 for 3 years.

The Founder

Founded NET (Natural English Training) and PrAiS (Phoenix Rising Ai Solutions). NET delivers English mastery through the Tiger Protocol. PrAiS builds gaming, corporate workflow solutions, and chat bots with memories 1000X longer than Gemini and GPT chatbots.
