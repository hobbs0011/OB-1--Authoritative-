**Combat mechanics:**
- **Damage calculation:** base damage × amplification - reduction
- **PvP:** Instinct (auto-dodge), Bounty/Honor ranking system, level scaling
- **Stuns:** core mechanic — land a stun, combo into damage
- **Dodging:** Instinct ability auto-dodges attacks (but can be broken)

**Mobile input:**

- Tap/hold to attack
- Joystick for movement
- Auto-camera combat focus

**Why kids love it:**
- Power progression is DEEP — hundreds of hours of upgrades
- Flashy VFX on abilities
- PvP flexing with rare fighting styles
- Social: crew system, trading

- **Weapon tiers:** budget early weapons → mid-tier → endgame weapons
- **Enemy scaling:** increasingly difficult zombies per wave

**🔴 THE COMBAT LOOP WE NEED:**
1. Wave starts → zombies spawn
2. Player aims + shoots / swings melee
3. Kill → coins/drops
4. Between waves → spend coins on weapons/upgrades/defenses
5. Harder wave → repeat
6. Boss every X waves → requires team coordination
7. Day 99 = final boss / endgame

### Stage 8 — PvP Arena / Fighting Games Breakdown ✅ COMPLETE

#### Combat Warriors — The Parry Bible
- **Parry (F key):** half-second window. Miss = 1.5s self-stun. Hit = 3s enemy stun
- **Parry recharge:** 3s on miss, 1s on hit (rewards skill)
- **Roll/Dash (E):** i-frames for dodging
- **Stomp (Q):** follow-up on stunned enemies
- **Glory Kill (G):** finisher animation on low HP enemies (< 20 HP)
- **Slide (Shift+C):** movement tech
- **Weapon skills:** mythical weapons have special abilities requiring 400+ damage to activate
- **MOBILE:** all mapped to virtual buttons

#### Blade Ball — Simplicity = Virality
- **ONE mechanic:** ball flies at you, tap to deflect, redirects to nearest player to your cursor
- **Miss = eliminated.** Last player standing wins.
- **Abilities:** purchasable powers that modify deflection (paid with Robux/coins)
- **Mobile:** tap anywhere = block/parry, tap ability button = activate
- **Cosmetics:** heavy monetization through skins, effects, trails
- **Why 80-200K players:** dead simple to learn, hard to master, pure timing skill

#### Da Hood — Street Brawler
- Click to punch, combo chains, ragdoll physics
- Social + combat combined

#### The Strongest Battlegrounds — Anime Brawler
- Character abilities from anime, combo-based
- Flashy VFX, competitive PvP

**🔴 WHAT WE STEAL FOR BOSS FIGHTS:**
- **Parry window** from Combat Warriors — tap at right moment to deflect boss attack → stun window → damage phase
- **Simplicity** from Blade Ball — one primary input, deep skill expression
- **Roll/dodge** from Combat Warriors — E to dodge boss AoE attacks
- **Glory Kill finisher** from Combat Warriors — satisfying kill animations on tough enemies

### Stage 10 — Low-Compute Combat Design ✅ COMPLETE

**Target device: $100 Android phone (2GB RAM, low-end GPU)**
Roblox's Asian growth = Thailand, Indonesia, Philippines, Vietnam = budget phones. This is OUR market.

**Performance budget:**

| Element | Budget | How Top Games Handle It |
|---------|--------|------------------------|
| **Max enemies on screen** | 8–12 zombies | Survive Wave Z caps visible enemies, spawns off-screen in queue |
| **Particle effects** | Minimal — 2-3 particles per hit max | Pre-baked animations > real-time particles. Flash + color shift > particle clouds |
| **Shadows** | OFF by default on mobile | Dev Forum consensus: shadows = #1 FPS killer on low-end. Toggle off. |
| **Part count (combat area)** | < 500 active parts | Use MeshParts, minimize unions, StreamingEnabled mandatory |
| **Combat VFX** | Billboard GUI + color flash | Damage numbers as BillboardGui, not 3D objects. Screen flash on hit. |
| **Animation quality** | 15 FPS animations acceptable | Roblox humanoid animations are already optimized. Keep custom anims simple. |
| **Network** | Client-side hit detection + server validation | All top combat games use client prediction. Server authoritative for damage. |

**Key optimization rules from Dev Forum:**
1. StreamingEnabled = ON (only loads nearby content)
2. Never use `.Touched()` for combat hitboxes — use `workspace:GetPartsInPart()` or raycasting
3. Client-side hit detection with server validation = responsive + secure
4. LOD: swap detailed models for simple meshes at distance
5. Render distance: cap at 500 studs for combat relevance

### Stage 11 — Combat Feel on Mobile ✅ COMPLETE

**The "Juice" Checklist — what makes kids FEEL the hit:**

| Effect | Cost | Impact | Used By |
|--------|------|--------|---------|
| **Screen shake** (camera offset) | FREE — code only | 🔴 CRITICAL — makes every hit feel powerful | Every top combat game |
| **Hit flash** (enemy turns white/red for 1 frame) | FREE — color property | 🔴 CRITICAL — instant visual confirmation | Blox Fruits, Combat Warriors |
| **Knockback** (enemy pushed back) | FREE — velocity change | 🔴 CRITICAL — physical impact feedback | All melee games |
| **Damage numbers** (floating text) | LOW — BillboardGui | 🟡 HIGH — satisfying number feedback | Blox Fruits, RPG-style games |
| **Hit sound effect** (crunch/slash) | FREE — audio | 🟡 HIGH — audio = 50% of feel | All combat games |
| **Kill effect** (explosion/dissolve) | LOW — particle burst | 🟡 MEDIUM — satisfying kill confirmation | Survive Wave Z, Dead Rails |
| **Combo counter** ("3x COMBO!") | LOW — UI text | 🟢 NICE — skill expression feedback | Combat Warriors, anime games |
| **Haptic vibration** | FREE — HapticService | 🟢 NICE — physical feedback on mobile | RIVALS, some games |

**Rule: < 100ms from tap to visual feedback.** Client-side prediction makes this possible. The server confirms damage later, but the player SEES and FEELS the hit immediately.

| Camera Type | Used By | Mobile UX | Our Fit |
|-------------|---------|-----------|---------|

**🔴 OUR WEAPON MONETIZATION MODEL:**
- **Layer 1: Gacha crates** — Robux to buy weapon crates with randomized rarity (Common → RIZZLORD). This is the casino. Proven by Steal a Brainrot ($90M+), Grow a Garden (#1 earner). Randomized rewards + intermittent reinforcement + FOMO from limited-time crates.
- **Layer 2: Cosmetic skins** — weapon skins, kill effects, damage number colors, trails. Pure flex. No gameplay advantage. Proven by RIVALS (#1 earner).
- **Layer 3: Soft power** — game passes for +10-20% damage, faster reload, extra inventory slot. Enough to feel worthwhile, not enough to break balance.

**Our implementation:**
- Difficulty spike at Day 15, 30, 50, 75 = natural purchase triggers
- Boss fights create "I keep dying" moments = revive purchases
- Weapon gacha after boss kills = "I almost got it" moments
- Guild leaderboards = social comparison spending

**🔴 THE SWEET SPOT (our model):**

- **10% convenience** — inventory slots, auto-collect, XP boosts. Time savers.

**Why this works:** Blox Fruits does this EXACTLY and it's the #1 sustained combat earner. RIVALS proves cosmetic-only works for FPS. We're PvE (not PvP), so soft power matters LESS because you're fighting AI zombies, not other players. P2W complaints are weaker in PvE.
