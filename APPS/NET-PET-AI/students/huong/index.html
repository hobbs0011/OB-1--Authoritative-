Vocabulary

Accommodation
A place where you can stay when you are visiting a place.
Amateur
Someone who does something as a hobby rather than as their job.
Bureaucracy
A system in which a lot of official work is done by people who follow fixed rules and procedures.
Conundrum
A difficult problem that is hard to solve.
Dilemma
A situation in which you have to choose between two difficult things, each of which could cause problems.
Ephemeral
Lasting for a very short time.
Futile
Having no hope of success; useless.
Gregarious
Enjoying the company of other people.
Hypothetical
Supposed or imagined but not real.
Intricate
Having many parts that are complicated and connected in a difficult way.

Listening Exercises

Exercise 1: Short Conversation

Listen to the conversation between two friends planning a weekend trip.

Questions:

  1. Where are they planning to go?
  2. What time do they plan to leave?
  3. Who will drive?

Exercise 2: News Report

Listen to a news report about a recent scientific discovery.

Questions:

  1. What was the discovery?
  2. Who made the discovery?
  3. What are the implications of this discovery?

Exercise 3: Podcast Episode

Listen to a podcast episode discussing the benefits of meditation.

Questions:

  1. What are the main benefits of meditation?
  2. How long should one meditate?
  3. What are some tips for beginners?

Exercise 4: Customer Service Call

Listen to a customer service call about a product issue.

Questions:

  1. What is the issue with the product?
  2. What solution did the customer service representative offer?
  3. Was the customer satisfied?

Exercise 5: Lecture Snippet

Listen to a snippet from a university lecture on environmental science.

Questions:

  1. What topic was being discussed?
  2. What were the key points mentioned?
  3. What further reading was suggested?
