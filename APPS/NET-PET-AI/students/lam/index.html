Reading Practice

Level A1   Short texts & comprehension

📖 Text 1 — My Friend Minh

Minh is my friend. He is five years old. He likes superheroes and ice cream. His favorite superhero is Spider-Man. Minh speaks Vietnamese and he is learning English. He is very smart and very funny.

Comprehension:

  1. How old is Minh?
  2. What does Minh like?
  3. What languages does Minh speak?

📖 Text 2 — Learning English

Learning English is fun but sometimes hard. I practice every day. I listen to songs in English. I watch videos. I read short stories. I speak with my teacher. Mistakes are okay. Every mistake helps me learn. I am getting better every week.

Vocabulary Check:

Find these words in the text: practice, listen, mistakes, better. Write one sentence using each word.

📖 Text 3 — At the Coffee Shop

A: Hello! Can I have a coffee, please?
B: Sure. Small or large?
A: Large, please. With milk.
B: Anything else?
A: No, thank you. How much is it?
B: That's fifty thousand dong.
A: Here you go. Thank you!
B: You're welcome!

Role Play: Practice this dialogue with a partner. Then try ordering something different.
