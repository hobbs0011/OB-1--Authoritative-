OUR METHODOLOGY

N.E.T. Beginner

THE TRANSFORMATION — MOUSE → TIGER

Why This Works

Natural English Training at the beginner level is about building a foundation brick by brick. Vietnamese is not the enemy — it is the bridge. We use it constantly at this stage to make sure every new word and concept is truly understood before we practice it in English.

The goal is not to eliminate Vietnamese thinking. The goal is to build enough English confidence that Quy starts choosing English first. That happens gradually, session by session, word by word.

OBI — NET Coach

Three Core Principles

  • Vietnamese as Your Bridge:

    Translation is your scaffolding, not your crutch. Every new word is explained in Vietnamese first so the meaning is solid, then practiced in English. We are not skipping Vietnamese — we are using it as a launchpad.

  • Listen Then Speak:

    Hearing English correctly comes before speaking it. We repeat, mimic, and drill sounds before we build sentences. The ear leads the mouth. Michael's voice is the model — copy the rhythm, the speed, and the sounds exactly.

  • 42 Phonics Sounds:

    English has 42 sounds, not 26 letters. Many of these sounds do not exist in Vietnamese. We learn them one group at a time through repetition and tongue twisters — so reading and speaking start to connect at the sound level.

Session Architecture

INTRO — 2 min

Michael greets in English. Quy responds. Vietnamese help is always available. The goal is just to start — one sentence is enough. Sets a safe, low-pressure tone for the session.

VOCAB — 5 min

5 new words introduced with Vietnamese meaning first, then the English word, then a simple example sentence. Quy repeats each word out loud until the pronunciation is close. No memorisation pressure — repetition does the work.

PHONICS — 5 min

One sound group practiced through repetition and tongue twisters. Michael models — Quy mimics. Focus on mouth position and breath, not meaning. This is muscle memory training.

PRACTICE — 8 min

Simple conversation using only the vocabulary learned so far. Questions are short. Answers can be one word or one sentence. Vietnamese rescue is always available — the point is to attempt English first.

What Parents & Managers Should Know

The sessions look simple. That is by design. Repetition and mimicking at the beginner level builds the neural pathways that make fluency possible later. Every repeated word is a wire getting stronger. Do not underestimate how much is happening under the surface.

At this stage, Vietnamese subtitles while watching English content are completely fine. Listening to Michael's audio reviews daily is the highest-impact home practice. Volume of English input now creates the fluency breakthrough later.
