Simple Work Phrases

  • "Here is the invoice."

  • "The payment is [amount]."

  • "Please check the total."

  • "I need the receipt."

  • "Can you send the report?"

  • "The balance is [amount]."

  • "This is a mistake. Please correct it."

  • "When is the payment due?"

  • "I will send the file today."

  • "Please approve this expense."

Pronunciation — Numbers

  • "Thirteen" vs "Thirty" — thir-TEEN vs THIR-ty (stress changes meaning)

  • "Fifteen" vs "Fifty" — fif-TEEN vs FIF-ty (stress changes meaning)

  • "Million" — MIL-yun — 2 syllables only

  • "Thousand" — THOU-zand — the TH sound

  • "Percent" — per-SENT — the C is an S sound

In accounting, one wrong number can cause a big problem. Say your numbers slowly and clearly. Always.
