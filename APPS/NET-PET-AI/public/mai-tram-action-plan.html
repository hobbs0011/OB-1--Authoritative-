Home Practice

The Compound Effect

At entry-level intermediate, the foundation is built. Now it needs to be activated daily. The vocabulary is there — the goal of this plan is to make English the reflex, not the translation. That only happens through consistent daily use outside of sessions.

15 intentional minutes every day beats two hours once a week. At this level, the difference between progress and plateau is daily exposure. Read, speak, listen — in that order — every single day.

OBI — NET Coach

Daily Habits

Daily — 5 min

Read Aloud

Read one page of English content aloud. Professional articles, news, or any English document. Focus on pronunciation, not comprehension speed.

Daily — 5 min

English Journal

Write 3–5 sentences in English about your day or something that happened at work. No correction tools. Imperfect English is the goal at this stage.

Daily — 15 min

English Listening

Watch or listen to English content. English subtitles are acceptable if needed, but try without first. Podcasts, YouTube, TED talks, sitcoms. Topic does not matter — enjoyment keeps you consistent. Accent exposure and natural speech rhythm are what you are training.

Weekly Targets

Weekly

One English Work Email

Write at least one work email entirely in English. Does not need to be sent. Draft it, then review against your session notes for targeted patterns.

Weekly

Speak First

In at least one meeting or conversation this week, make your first comment in English before switching to Vietnamese. Even one sentence counts.

Ongoing

Vocabulary Log

Keep a running list of 5 new English words per week from your work context. Review the list at the start of each session.
