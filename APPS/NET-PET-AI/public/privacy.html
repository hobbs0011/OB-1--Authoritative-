Privacy Policy

Effective: May 2026 · Last Updated: May 21, 2026

What We Collect

When you use Natural English Training ("NET"), we store your profile name, email, English learning goals, profession, and hobbies. Every OBI chat exchange, vocabulary practice result, and session interaction is recorded to improve your learning continuity.

Why We Collect It

All data is used exclusively to personalize your English learning experience. OBI uses your history to maintain context across sessions, track your progress (Mouse → Tiger), and generate personalized lessons. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

How We Store It

Your data is stored in Cloudflare Workers KV and Durable Objects, encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest. Authentication uses SHA-256 hashed passwords. Session state is ephemeral and never written to disk outside the worker runtime.

Third-Party Services

Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser localStorage to persist your login session and UI preferences. No third-party tracking cookies are used. Our Chrome extension stores session hints locally in your browser only.
