Session context

Workplace English for Vietnamese corporate staff. Focus: spontaneous conversation, HR vocabulary, client communication, and KISS-style output. No scripts. No Vietnamese. No perfectionism.

Operating rules
  • No scripts. Staff may not bring prepared questions into session.
  • No Vietnamese. Class runs in English only. Bridges are forbidden.
  • KISS output. Short beats long. Clear beats smart-sounding.
  • Volume + Velocity. Speak more, faster. Grammar follows fluency.
  • Permission over Perfection. Mistakes are required. Silence is the failure.
  • 3-day rule. Miss three prep days and the session becomes review, not progression.
