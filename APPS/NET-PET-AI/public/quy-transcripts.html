Transcripts Pending Publication

Session recordings are reviewed and processed before publication to this portal. The review process typically takes 24–48 hours after each session. Transcripts will appear here once verified.

Publication Process

  1. Full Recording Review — Each session recording is reviewed in full using Deepgram diarisation to separate speaker channels.

  2. Speaker Verification — Only student speech segments with clear attribution are selected for publication.

  3. Coherence Check — Clips are only published when they represent a complete, contextually clear exchange.

  4. Publication — Verified clips are published here for review and as reference material for home practice.

Why Transcripts Matter

Reading your own spoken English forces a different kind of reflection than listening to yourself. The grammar errors and vocabulary choices that are invisible in real-time conversation become immediately visible in text.

We use transcripts as a mirror: not to create self-consciousness, but to build awareness of specific patterns that appear repeatedly. Repetition in errors means a pattern to target. Improvement in transcripts means the drill is working.
