HOW YOU CAN SUPPORT HIM AT HOME

Minh's confidence is growing fast. Three simple ways to keep the momentum going — no pressure, no homework:

  • Ask about his creations: When he builds something with Legos or toys, ask "What's the problem?" and "How do we solve it?" — the same way we do in class.
  • Celebrate the attempts: If he uses an English word or phrase at home, praise the confidence — not the grammar. Correction comes later; right now confidence is everything.
  • Follow his interests: Talk about the helicopter on Shopee. Ask what kind of shark he would be. When the topic is his choice, the English flows by itself.
