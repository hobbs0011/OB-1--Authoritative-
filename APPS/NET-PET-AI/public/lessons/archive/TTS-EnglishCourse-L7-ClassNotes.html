Communication Tip: Expressing Opinions

The goal today is to learn how to express your **perspective** clearly, but always respectfully. It's vital to have a **view**, but never to aggressively assert your way is **always** right. We must **remember** that a good discussion involves listening, not just talking.

4. Core Reading Exercises: Contextual Practice

Communication Focus: Respectful Debate

When in a discussion, you must not be afraid. In my opinion, the best way to start is by being polite. It's easy to say "**I strongly believe that**" if you Feel very sure, but remember that others may not See eye to eye with your View. The way I see it, being open to their Perspective is essential for a productive conversation.

Sometimes in business, you may need to "**Take a stand**." This means you must be firm in your Believe. For example, "**If you ask me**, we need to make this change right now." Or, "**From my point of view**," this proposal will not work. WheReckon something is true, express it with confidence.

Topic Focus: Energy and Business

The need for renewable energy is clear. Corporations are investing heavily ioffshore wind because onshore wind has space limitations. We focus on sustainability to reduce our carbon emissions and total carbon footprint, moving away from fossil fuels to minimize environmental impact.

Companies grow through merger or acquisition. Buying shares on the stock market makes you a shareholder, granting ownership. Smaller firms often choose a partnership, while large projects may use a joint venture.

5. Role-Playing Scripts (Application Practice)

Energy Policy Debate

[Determined] **Analyst A:** I **strongly believe that** investing in **offshore wind** has a better long-term return than relying on **fossil fuels**.
[Skeptical] **Analyst B:** The **way I see it**, the upfront cost is too high for the current budget.

Corporate Strategy

[Confident] **CEO:** Our **corporation** needs to move on this **acquisition** now. We must **Take a stand** for future growth.
[Cautious] **CFO:** I **reckon** a simple **partnership** might be safer than a full **acquisition**.

Environmental Metrics

[Concerned] **Manager:** Our **carbon footprint** is still too high. We must prove our commitment to **sustainability**.
[Practical] **Team Lead:** Agreed. **From my point of view**, reducing **carbon emissions** is our immediate priority.

Investment Strategy

[Eager] **Advisor:** **If you ask me**, buying these new **shares** gives you great **ownership** right now.
[Thoughtful] **Investor:** I **feel** the **stock market** is too volatile. What's the **perspective** on this group?

Renewable Portfolio

[Analytical] **Engineer 1:** The **environmental impact** of **onshore wind** is easier to manage than **tidal energy**.
[Opposing] **Engineer 2:** I **believe** **tidal energy** is the future. We don't **See eye to eye** on the best **renewable energy** source.

Joint Business Decisions

[Collaborative] **Director:** We formed this **joint venture** to develop **solar energy** in the desert.
[Direct] **Partner:** I need you to **Speak your mind** about the expected return for our **shareholder**s.
