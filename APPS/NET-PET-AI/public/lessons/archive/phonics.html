Pronunciation Phonics Guide 👑

Decoding the 42 Sounds of English

This comprehensive guide is based on **Jolly Phonics**, a fun and multi-sensory synthetic phonics method. By learning the 42 main sounds and their corresponding actions, we can master English sound production, significantly improving both **pronunciation** and **reading fluency**.

The 5 Key Skills of Jolly Phonics

A holistic approach to literacy and speech mastery.

1. Learning the Sounds

Mastering the 42 phonemes with actions.

2. Learning Letter Formation

Properly writing the letters using guided movements.

3. Blending

Pushing sounds together to read new words.

4. Identifying Sounds in Words

Segmenting words for accurate spelling and writing.

5. Learning Tricky Words

Memorizing high-frequency words with irregular spellings (e.g., 'the', 'said').
