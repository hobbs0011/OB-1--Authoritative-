Enhanced Learning: Class Customization

To maximize learning effectiveness and provide a more personalized experience, we propose splitting participants into two distinct groups based on their current English proficiency:

Lower Intermediate & Beginner Students

This group will focus on building foundational grammar, vocabulary, and confidence in basic communication.

Higher Intermediate & Advanced Students

This group will engage in more complex discussions, advanced business scenarios, and intensive practice of specialized skills.

This approach allows for tailored activities, more individualized attention, and fosters a supportive environment within smaller class sizes, ensuring optimal progress for all participants.
