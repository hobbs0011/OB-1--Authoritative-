Role Snapshot

Mai Tram wears two hats at BlueTech. As HR, she handles recruitment, interviews, onboarding, and internal communications. English is essential for both: professional emails, meeting foreign clients at events, and presenting the company.

Key Phrases

  • Introducing yourself: "Hi, I'm Mai Tram from BlueTech. We provide [service]. Do you have a moment?"

  • Asking for business card: "Could I get your card? I'd love to follow up with you."

  • Following up after event: "It was great meeting you at [event]. I wanted to follow up and share more about what we do."

  • Scheduling interview: "I'd like to invite you for an interview on [date]. Does that work for you?"

  • Feedback after interview: "Thank you for coming in. We'll be in touch within [X] days."

  • Sending proposal: "Please find our proposal attached. Let me know if you have any questions."

  • Checking on deal: "I wanted to check in — have you had a chance to review our proposal?"

Fluency Drill

Practice these questions with OBI. Answer immediately — no thinking time.

  • "What does BlueTech do?" → Practice answer in 2 sentences

  • "How many people work at your company?" → 1 sentence answer

  • "What's your role?" → 1 sentence answer

  • "Are you looking to hire right now?" → Yes/No + 1 reason

  • "What makes BlueTech different?" → 2-3 sentence pitch

Speak before you're ready. The goal is not a perfect answer. The goal is an answer that comes out.

Coach Note

Mai Tram's dual role is an asset in English learning. HR trains her to ask good questions. Sales trains her to listen for what the other person actually wants. Both skills translate directly into English conversation. She already has the instincts — the work is letting them out faster.
