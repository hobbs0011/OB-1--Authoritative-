ACTION PLAN

Home Practice

The Compound Effect

Sessions alone are not enough. Language acquisition requires daily exposure at a volume that far exceeds what one weekly or bi-weekly session provides. This plan is the multiplier.

15 minutes per day of intentional practice compounds faster than 2 hours once a week. The key word is intentional: passive English (background music, TV) has low return. Active English (reading aloud, writing, speaking) has high return.

OBI — NET Coach

Daily Habits

Daily — 5 min

Read Aloud

Read one page of English content aloud. Professional articles, news, or any English document. Focus on pronunciation, not comprehension speed.

Daily — 5 min

English Journal

Write 3–5 sentences in English about your day or something that happened at work. No correction tools. Imperfect English is the goal at this stage.

Daily — 15 min

English Listening

Watch or listen to English content without Vietnamese subtitles. Podcasts, YouTube, TED talks. Topic does not matter. Accent exposure matters.

Weekly Targets

Weekly

One English Work Email

Write at least one work email entirely in English. Does not need to be sent. Draft it, then review against your session notes for targeted patterns.

Weekly

Speak First

In at least one meeting or conversation this week, make your first comment in English before switching to Vietnamese. Even one sentence counts.

Ongoing

Vocabulary Log

Keep a running list of 5 new English words per week from your work context. Review the list at the start of each session.
