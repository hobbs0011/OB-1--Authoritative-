Frequently Asked Questions

What is the word limit on the free plan?

The free plan includes 10 words per day to practice and learn. You can upgrade to any paid plan for unlimited access.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan at any time?

Yes, you can change your plan at any time. When upgrading, you'll get immediate access to new features; downgrading takes effect at the next billing cycle.

Is there a free trial for the paid plans?

We offer a 7-day free trial on the NET plan. After that, you'll be charged monthly unless you cancel.

What does the AI tutor cover?

The AI tutor provides personalized lessons on vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation, and conversation practice, adapting to your level and goals.

How does the TEAM plan work with multiple users?

The TEAM plan includes 5 seats. An admin can invite team members, monitor progress through the admin console, and view aggregated analytics for the entire group.
