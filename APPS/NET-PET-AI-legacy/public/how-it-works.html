6-Month Roadmap

From A1 to B1 — what the journey looks like month by month.

A1 Month 1–2
A2 Month 3
B1 Month 4–6

A1 — Foundation

500 words, present tense, daily greetings. Build the habit — 15 min/day, fear-free speaking practice from day one.

A2 — Bridge

1,000 words, past/future tense, simple opinions. Real situational dialogues. Confidence grows — 30 min/day focus windows.

B1 — Fluency

2,000+ words, complex grammar, B1 Cambridge-pass capability. Full conversation flows, work-email ready.
