Section 1

Ignite (Confidence)

  • 2-minute warm-up in English only.
  • Emotion sentence drills: happy, frustrated, proud, curious.
  • Quick win phrase to set voice energy.
Section 2

Phonics (Precision)

  • Target 6-8 sounds per class from the 42-sound map.
  • Minimal pairs and rhythm claps.
  • Phone recording for before/after comparison.
Section 3

Reading (Flow)

  • Intermediate starter paragraph reading.
  • Stress and chunking, not word-by-word fear.
  • Track comprehension threshold: target 60%+
Section 4

Speak + Reflect (Ownership)

  • 90-second speaking challenge from the reading topic.
  • NET-PET-AI reflection prompt + spaced repetition card set.
  • Micro-homework with one clear checkpoint.

Passage A - Morning Momentum

Every morning, Huong takes a short walk before work. She checks the weather, drinks warm tea, and opens her learning app. On the bus, she listens to one English clip and repeats three key sentences. She does not need perfect understanding. She only needs steady progress and clear sound practice.

Passage B - Work and Confidence

At work, Huong joins meetings with people from different teams. Sometimes she feels nervous, but she keeps speaking. She uses simple structure, clear voice, and active listening. After each meeting, she writes three useful phrases and practices them with emotion: calm, excited, and professional.

Passage C - Weekend Reset

On Saturday, Huong records a two-minute voice journal. She talks about one challenge and one win from the week. Then she reads one paragraph aloud and checks rhythm, word stress, and difficult sounds. Small sessions every day create big results over time.
