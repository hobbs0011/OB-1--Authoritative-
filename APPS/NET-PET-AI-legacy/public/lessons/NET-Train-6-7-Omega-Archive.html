01 // MISSION BRIEFING

01 // Tóm tắt nhiệm vụ

The year is 202X. The internet did not just crash. It mutated. A digital-biological virus known as Brainrot has infected 99% of the human population.

Society has collapsed. People have lost the ability to think. They have become NPCs in a glitched reality. They wander the ruins, muttering viral catchphrases and hunting for attention.

02 // THE COLLAPSE

02 // Sự sụp đổ

THE EVENT

It started with "The Algorithm." It was designed to keep people watching screens. It worked too well. It rewrote human neural pathways. The first patient, Tyler Chen, watched 72 hours of short-form content without blinking. He became Patient Zero.

OCT 2024 // THE OUTBREAK

Patient Zero infects Ohio University. The military attempts to quarantine the state. The quarantine fails due to "Skill Issue."

DEC 2024 // THE SILENCE

Global communication networks fall. The internet becomes a hive-mind for the infected. The Rizzlords emerge as commanders.

FEB 2025 // PROJECT TRAIN 6-7

The last engineers build a logic-powered locomotive. It is the only place where the virus cannot reach.
