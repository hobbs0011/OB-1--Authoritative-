03 // VOCAB VAULT

03 // Kho từ vựng

Master these five signal-decryption keys. Without them, every transmission is noise.

🗝️ KEY 1 IDIOM

A common phrase where the meaning is completely different from the individual words.

EXAMPLE: "Break a leg" = Good luck (NOT a wish for injury)
Thành ngữ — cụm từ có nghĩa khác với nghĩa đen
🪞 KEY 2 METAPHOR

Saying something IS something else to create a vivid picture. No "like" or "as" needed.

EXAMPLE: "Time is money" = Time is valuable (NOT literally currency)
Ẩn dụ — nói một thứ LÀ thứ khác
🎭 KEY 3 SIMILE

A comparison using "like" or "as" to describe something by connecting it to something else.

EXAMPLE: "Fast as lightning" = Extremely fast
So sánh — dùng "like" hoặc "as"
💥 KEY 4 HYPERBOLE

Extreme exaggeration that is NOT meant to be taken literally. Used for emphasis or humor.

EXAMPLE: "I'm so hungry I could eat a horse" = Very hungry
Phóng đại — nói quá để nhấn mạnh
🧊 KEY 5 LITERAL

The exact, dictionary meaning of words. No hidden message, no tricks — just face value.

EXAMPLE: "The cat sat on the mat" = Exactly what it says
Nghĩa đen — đúng nghĩa của từ
