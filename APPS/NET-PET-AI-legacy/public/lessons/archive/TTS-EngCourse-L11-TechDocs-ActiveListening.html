💡 Communication Tips for Active Listening

🔥 Warm-Up: Review & Focus

Ice Breaker: The Misread Instruction

The trainer will share a brief, funny story about a time they misread a simple, non-technical instruction (like a recipe or furniture assembly manual) that led to a small, comical disaster.

Discussion Questions:

  • What is one instruction, written or verbal, you completely misinterpreted recently, and what was the result?
  • Have you ever worked on a technical document that was so confusing you just gave up and asked for verbal clarification?
  • How important is it to ask a clarifying question immediately versus waiting until you are stuck?

Video: The Power of Active Listening (5-7 min)

Video Title: "The Power of Active Listening: 5 Techniques to Engage"

Post-Video Discussion:

  • The video mentioned 'mirroring' or 'paraphrasing'—how does that one technique specifically help ensure accuracy when discussing technical specifications?
  • In a virtual meeting, what nonverbal cues (like eye contact, nodding, using the chat) can engineers use to show they are actively listening to the project manager?
  • What is the most common distraction (internal or external) that prevents you from having deep concentration during a long, complex technical presentation?
