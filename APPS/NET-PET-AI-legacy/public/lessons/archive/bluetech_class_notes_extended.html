🧩 1. The Great Blu-Tech Mystery

Bí Ẩn Về Cấu Trúc Công Ty

The Nine Companies Problem

We opened class with what might be the most confusing question in corporate Vietnam: How many "Blu-Techs" are there, exactly?

📊 The Investigation

Michael discovered what appeared to be nine different "blues" when everyone thought there was only one company. The names included variations like:

  • Blue Technique (ingredients division?)
  • Blue Lifestyle (healthy products sales?)
  • Blue [???] (several other variations)
Có vẻ như có ít nhất 9 công ty/bộ phận khác nhau với tên "Blue" trong một tòa nhà!
Michael:
"I thought there was only one Blu-Tech, but I see nine different blues! What is happening here?"
Quy:
"Blue technique ingredient... and blue lifestyle and sale healthy product..."
Michael:
"So it's like... different divisions? Or different companies in the same building?"

🎯 Corporate Structure Vocabulary

  • Division - A major section of a company (e.g., Sales Division, Marketing Division)
  • Department - A smaller unit within a division
  • Subsidiary - A separate company owned by a parent company
  • Branch - A location of the same company in a different place
Từ vựng về cấu trúc doanh nghiệp - rất quan trọng khi giải thích công ty của bạn!

Getting to Know the Team

[00:01:28]

Quy's Journey

Michael asked whether Quy was new to the company or new to the department.

✅ Professional Response Pattern
"I am new to [Department], but I am not new to the company. I have been an intern here for one year."
Cách trả lời chuyên nghiệp khi được hỏi về vị trí trong công ty.
Why This Matters: This response clearly shows your history and value to the company. It tells people you're experienced with the company culture, even if you're learning a new role.
[00:03:12]

The Dog vs. Cat Question

In a lighter moment, Michael asked the classic question: "Would you prefer a dog or a cat?"

Quy:
"I prefer dog, but I don't have any pet now."
Michael:
"Dogs are loyal. Cats are... independent. I like that about this answer!"

🗣️ Small Talk Tip

Questions like "dog or cat?" are common in English-speaking work culture. They help people connect on a personal level. Other common questions:

  • "Coffee or tea?"
  • "Beach or mountains?"
  • "Morning person or night owl?"

These questions have no wrong answer - they just help start conversations!

🎥 Understanding 5D Cinema

3D Movies: Depth perception with special glasses

4D Movies: 3D + physical effects (moving seats, water, wind)

5D Movies: 4D + additional sensory effects (smells, temperature changes, etc.)

Rạp chiếu 5D = 3D + ghế rung + hiệu ứng vật lý + hiệu ứng giác quan khác!

🗺️ Describing Travel & Virtual Experiences

Virtual experiences: ✅ "I got to see many countries." ✅ "It felt like I was traveling around the world." ✅ "It was like being there in person." Real experiences: ✅ "I traveled to [place]." ✅ "I visited [number] countries." ✅ "I experienced [culture/activity]."

⚠️ CRITICAL PREPOSITION ERROR
❌ "I walked ON the sea."

This means you walked on top of the water like a miracle! 🚶‍♂️💧

✅ "I walked BESIDE the sea."
✅ "I walked ALONG the beach."
✅ "I walked BY the ocean."
Đi dạo BÊN bờ biển / DỌC bãi biển (KHÔNG phải "trên" biển!)
Why this matters: Prepositions of place in English are very specific:
  • ON = on the surface (on the table, on the roof, on the water)
  • BESIDE/BY = next to something
  • ALONG = following a path or edge

Cultural Note: In English-speaking work culture, light teasing among friends is common and shows comfort/closeness. However, know your audience - this only works with people who understand you're joking!

🗣️ 4. Language Upgrades & Corrections

Sửa Lỗi & Nâng Cấp Ngôn Ngữ

❌ Error #1: The Flying Boat

❌ "I rode a flying boat."
✅ "I rode a roller coaster."
Tàu lượn siêu tốc = Roller Coaster
Full Context:
  • Roller Coaster: The amusement park ride with tracks that goes up and down
  • Flying Boat: An actual vehicle - a seaplane that can land on water

Related vocabulary:

✅ "thrill ride" - any exciting amusement park ride ✅ "ferris wheel" - the big circular ride ✅ "carousel" / "merry-go-round" - the spinning horses ✅ "bumper cars" - the cars you crash into each other

❌ Error #2: Walking on Water (Jesus Mode)

❌ "I walked on the sea."

This literally means walking on top of the water! 🚶‍♂️💧✨

✅ "I walked beside the sea."
✅ "I walked along the beach."
✅ "I walked by the ocean."
✅ "I took a walk near the water."
Đi dạo bên cạnh/dọc theo bờ biển.
Prepositions of Place with Water:
✅ ON the water = on the surface (in a boat, surfing) ✅ IN the water = submerged or swimming ✅ BY/BESIDE the water = next to it on land ✅ ALONG the water = following the shoreline ✅ NEAR the water = close to it ✅ UNDER the water = beneath the surface (diving)

Example sentences:

  • "We sailed on the ocean."
  • "I swam in the sea."
  • "Our hotel is beside the beach."
  • "We walked along the coastline."

⚠️ Error #3: Greeting Confusion

❌ "Nice to meet you." (to someone you already know)
✅ "Nice to see you again."
✅ "Good to see you."
✅ "Great to see you again!"
"Meet" chỉ dùng cho lần đầu gặp. Sau đó phải dùng "See".
The "Meet" vs. "See" Rule:
FIRST TIME meeting someone: ✅ "Nice to meet you." ✅ "Pleased to meet you." ✅ "It's a pleasure to meet you." SEEING someone AGAIN: ✅ "Nice to see you again." ✅ "Good to see you." ✅ "Great seeing you!" ✅ "It's been a while!" (if it's been a long time)

Why this matters:

Using "meet" with someone you already know suggests you don't remember them! It can be awkward in professional settings.

Professional alternatives:

  • "Hello again!" (casual)
  • "Good morning/afternoon." (neutral)
  • "How have you been?" (friendly)

✅ Upgrade: Professional Self-Introduction

✅ "I am new to [Department], but I am not new to the company."
✅ "I have been an intern here for one year."
✅ "I started in [Department] in [Month], but I've been with the company since [Year]."
Cách giới thiệu chuyên nghiệp về vị trí trong công ty.
Why this structure is powerful:

This response accomplishes multiple things:

  • ✅ Shows you're learning but experienced with company culture
  • ✅ Explains your history without being defensive
  • ✅ Sets expectations appropriately (new to role, but not new to company)

Full template for introductions:

"Hi, I'm [Name]. I'm new to [Department/Position], but I've been with [Company] for [Time Period] as [Previous Role]. I'm excited to learn about [New Area] while bringing my experience in [Previous Area]."

Mission 2: The Highlight Sentence

Speaking Practice
📝 Your Assignment

Task: Prepare ONE sentence about your upcoming week

Structure to use:

"This week, I am excited to [ACTION] because [REASON]."

💡 Examples to Inspire You

✅ "This week, I am excited to sleep because I am very tired." ✅ "This week, I am excited to finish my project because I've been working on it for two months." ✅ "This week, I am excited to visit my family because I haven't seen them in a while." ✅ "This week, I am excited to learn Python because it will help me automate my work." ✅ "This week, I am excited to try a new restaurant because my friend recommended it."
Ví dụ về cách sử dụng cấu trúc câu này.

🎯 Grammar Focus: "Excited to" + Verb

Structure breakdown:

[Subject] + [am/is/are] + excited + to + [base verb] + because + [reason] Components: - Subject: I, We, She, He, They, etc. - Excited to: Shows enthusiasm about future action - Base verb: sleep, eat, visit, learn, work, etc. - Because: Explains your reason (optional but recommended)

Alternative structures you can practice:

✅ "I'm looking forward to [verb+ing]..." ✅ "I can't wait to [base verb]..." ✅ "I'm eager to [base verb]..." ✅ "I'm thrilled about [noun/verb+ing]..."

Why practice this?

  • Commonly used in professional settings (meetings, emails, presentations)
  • Shows enthusiasm and positive attitude
  • Great for small talk and building rapport
  • Natural way to share plans and goals
⚡ Challenge Mode (Optional)

Try to think of THREE sentences using this structure about different areas of your life:

  1. Work/Career: Something professional you're excited about
  2. Personal/Fun: Something for enjoyment or relaxation
  3. Learning/Growth: Something new you want to try or learn
