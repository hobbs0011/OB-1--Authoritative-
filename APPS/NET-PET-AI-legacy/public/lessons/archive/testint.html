👥 PART 1: The Meeting Derailer

You are leading a critical project update meeting. 10 minutes in, a senior colleague (Mark) interrupts you.

MARK (Skeptical):
"Sorry, but I have to stop you. I just don't see how this new timeline is realistic. Are we sure this is the right plan at all?"

YOUR ACTION:

🎯 PART 2: YOUR TASK (Writing)

Imagine you chose **Option [B]**. The meeting is now over. Write a short, professional follow-up email to Mark to address his "parked" concern. (2-3 sentences)

📚 Resources & Vocabulary

  • To "derail" (v.): To stop a process or plan from succeeding.
  • To "park" an idea (v.): To set an idea aside to be discussed later.
  • Agenda (n.): A list of items to be discussed at a meeting.
  • Realistic (adj.): Seeming real or achievable.
  • Technical Debt (n.): The long-term cost of choosing an easy (limited) solution instead of a better, more time-consuming one.
  • Consensus (n.): A general agreement.
  • Action Items (n.): Tasks assigned to someone during a meeting.
