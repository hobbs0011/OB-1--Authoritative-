2. Language Focus
Corrections & structures you should remember
EN

🚤 A. Vocabulary — The “Flying Boat” Incident

❌ “I rode a flying boat.”
✅ “I rode a roller coaster.”

Useful words: thrilling, terrifying, adrenaline, loop-the-loop.

🌊 B. Prepositions — The “Jesus” Incident

❌ “I walked on the sea.” (miracle)
✅ “I walked beside the sea / along the beach / on the sand.”

👋 C. Greetings in a Professional Environment

❌ “Nice to meet you.” (if you already know the person)
✅ “Nice to see you again.”

Rule: “meet” = first time only. After that, say “see”.

🏢 D. Talking About Your Job History

Structure: “I am new to the [department], but I am not new to the company.”

Good example: “I have been an intern here for one year.”

📅 E. Future Plans

Useful structures:

  • “I will relax.”
  • “I am going to watch movies.”
  • Natural native phrase: “Nothing special, just working.”
VI

🚤 A. Từ vựng — “Flying Boat”

❌ “I rode a flying boat.” (thuyền bay)
✅ “I rode a roller coaster.” (tàu lượn siêu tốc)

Từ vựng thêm: thrilling (hồi hộp, phấn khích), terrifying (rất sợ), adrenaline, loop-the-loop.

🌊 B. Giới từ — “Jesus đi bộ trên nước”

❌ “I walked on the sea.” (như phép màu)
✅ “I walked beside the sea.” / “I walked along the beach.” / “I walked on the sand.”

👋 C. Chào hỏi lịch sự trong môi trường công việc

❌ “Nice to meet you.” (khi đã quen nhau)
✅ “Nice to see you again.”

Quy tắc: “meet” dùng cho lần đầu; từ lần sau dùng “see”.

🏢 D. Nói về kinh nghiệm làm việc

Cấu trúc: “I am new to the [department], but I am not new to the company.”

Ví dụ tốt: “I have been an intern here for one year.”

📅 E. Kế hoạch sắp tới

Cấu trúc hữu ích:

  • “I will relax.” (Tôi sẽ nghỉ ngơi.)
  • “I am going to watch movies.” (Tôi định xem phim.)
  • Cách nói tự nhiên: “Nothing special, just working.” (Không có gì đặc biệt, chỉ đi làm thôi.)

Input · Speaking · Confidence

  • Find and watch Episode 1 of The Office (US version).
  • Use English subtitles only (no Vietnamese).
  • Focus on the atmosphere and relationships, not translating every word.

Question for next class: Is Michael Scott smart or stupid? Do the employees respect him?

⭐ Mission 2 — Highlight Sentence

Prepare one sentence about your upcoming week using this structure:

“This week, I am excited to [ACTION] because [REASON].”

Examples:

  • “This week, I am excited to sleep because I am very tired.”
  • “This week, I am excited to eat seafood because I love crab.”
