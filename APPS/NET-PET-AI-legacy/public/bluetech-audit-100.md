# Bluetech Portal — 100-Point Quality Audit
## 100/100 is the only acceptable score.

### PART A — DESIGN & VISUAL CRAFT (25 points)

1. Every page uses a clear 60-30-10 color rule: 60% dark/base, 30% mid-tone, 10% accent.
2. Accent color is used sparingly and consistently (max 2 accent hues per page).
3. Typography scale is strict: display, heading, body, caption — no ad-hoc sizes.
4. Font pairing is intentional: one display font, one UI font max.
5. Line heights create comfortable vertical rhythm (1.5–1.6 for body).
6. Letter spacing is used as a system, not decoration.
7. White space is treated as an active design element, not emptiness.
8. Cards/sections have consistent border radius and shadow language.
9. Hover states exist on every interactive element and feel premium (ease-out, subtle lift).
10. Images/icons are retina-ready and aligned to pixel grid.
11. No element is orphaned by more than 12px from its logical group.
12. Gradients are subtle (0–15% opacity stops) and never neon.
13. Motion is purpose-driven: entrance, hover, state change. No random animation.
14. Micro-interactions feel native (150–300ms, ease-out).
15. Dark mode is the primary mode; light mode is a thoughtful inversion, not an afterthought.
16. Glass morphism is used only where it adds depth, never as filler.
17. Border treatments are consistent: 1px, rgba white/black, subtle.
18. Spacing system uses an 8px baseline grid.
19. No element touches the viewport edge without intentional negative space.
20. Visual hierarchy is unmistakable in under 3 seconds.
21. Icons are from one family/style, never mixed sources.
22. Loading states, empty states, and error states are all designed.
23. Scroll behavior is smooth and predictable.
24. Cursor states are explicit: pointer, wait, text, move.
25. Every pixel either informs, guides, or delights. Nothing is accidental.

### PART B — COPY & NARRATIVE (20 points)

26. Hero headline states the outcome, not the feature.
27. Subheadline expands the headline with specificity (metric, timeframe, or transformation).
28. Body copy is scannable: short paragraphs, active voice, concrete nouns.
29. No jargon unless the audience is proven to use it.
30. Every section has a clear job: inform, reassure, prove, or convert.
31. Social proof is specific: names, roles, outcomes, dates.
32. Testimonials include context (what was at stake, what changed).
33. CTA copy describes the next step, not a generic action.
34. Error and empty states use human language, not system codes.
35. Navigation labels are nouns, not clever wordplay.
36. Buttons say what happens after the click.
37. No placeholder text survives to production.
38. All dates and numbers are formatted consistently.
39. Brand voice is consistent across all screens and states.
40. Legal/privacy text is legible, not hidden in microprint.
41. Success states confirm the action and state the next step.
42. Every major section has a visual break or divider.
43. Headings use sentence case unless title case is a deliberate brand choice.
44. Numbers are presented with context: "%", "vs", "from X to Y".
45. The narrative flows from problem → proof → promise → next step.

### PART C — TECHNICAL EXCELLENCE (20 points)

46. All interactive elements are keyboard accessible (tab, enter, escape).
47. Focus rings are visible and match the design system.
48. Color contrast meets WCAG AA on all text/background pairs.
49. Images have alt text or are marked decorative.
50. Forms have associated labels, not just placeholders.
51. Validation messages are inline, specific, and actionable.
52. Errors are reported in plain language with a recovery path.
53. Forms preserve valid input on submission failure.
54. No console errors in production.
55. No unhandled promise rejections.
56. Network requests are debounced/throttled where appropriate.
57. Sensitive data is never logged to console.
58. API responses include error codes and machine-readable messages.
59. Timeouts and retries are handled gracefully.
60. Loading states are shown for all async operations.
61. Session/auth flows handle expiry without losing unsaved work.
62. Offline/error states offer a clear retry or contact path.
63. Asset delivery is optimized: next-gen formats, compressed, cached.
64. Third-party scripts are loaded with `defer` or `async`.
65. No deprecated HTML APIs or browser-only assumptions.

### PART D — CONVERSION & RETENTION (15 points)

66. Primary CTA is visible above the fold on every landing screen.
67. CTAs repeat at logical stopping points (end of sections, after proofs).
68. Form fields are minimized: only what is required for the next step.
69. Progress is shown for multi-step flows.
70. Trust signals are placed near conversion actions.
71. Pricing/ROI math is shown, not just claimed.
72. Urgency is honest and specific (real dates, real limits).
73. Exit intent is handled with a relevant offer, not a popup spam.
74. Post-conversion flow confirms success and sets expectations.
75. Onboarding is time-to-value minimal: first win in under 60 seconds.
76. Empty states invite action, they don't just say "nothing here".
77. Re-engagement hooks are personalized, not broadcast.
78. Feature discovery is contextual, not a tour overlay.
79. Unsubscribe/opt-out is one click and immediate.
80. Churn signals are caught early with intervention copy.

### PART E — CLIENT RELATIONSHIP & EXPANSION (10 points)

81. Copy reflects existing relationship: "2 years", "since 2024", "next chapter".
82. Expansion language: "building on what we've built", "next phase", "grow with you".
83. Wins to date are quantified: sessions completed, improvements, streaks.
84. Renewal/expansion is framed as a natural next step, not a new sale.
85. Dedicated points of contact and support channels are visible.
86. Investment protection messaging is honest and clear.
87. ROI is shown from the company's perspective, not just learner satisfaction.
88. Team accountability tools are positioned as partnership, not surveillance.
89. Milestones from the last 2 years are acknowledged.
90. Next-phase options are presented as recommendations, not menus.

### PART F — BRAND & CONSISTENCY (10 points)

91. Logo mark is used correctly: size, clear space, color variants.
92. Brand colors are documented and applied consistently.
93. Iconography matches the brand personality.
94. Photography/illustration style is cohesive.
95. Tone of voice is consistent across all channels.
96. Footer contains the same core links on every page.
97. 404 and error pages maintain brand identity.
98. Email/SMS templates match portal visual language.
99. Dark/light mode transitions preserve brand colors.
100. The entire portal feels like one product, not a collection of pages.

---

## How to use
Score each item 0 (missing), 0.5 (partial), or 1 (complete). Target: 90+ before launch, 95+ before expansion messaging begins.
