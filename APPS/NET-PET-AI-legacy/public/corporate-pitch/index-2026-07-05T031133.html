What teachers miss

Most programs teach grammar. You already know grammar. The real block is speaking under pressure — meetings, calls, presentations — without freezing.

My method removes the fear first, then builds the skill. Permission over perfection. Confidence before accuracy.

The method

Four principles, a daily habit, and a safe environment. No textbook grammar drills.

🧠

Learn like a native

Children learn by listening first, then mimicking. We do the same: listen to English you already understand 60%+ of, then speak naturally.

💬

Break the grammar barrier

You spoke Vietnamese before you learned grammar rules. Communication comes first. Grammar follows naturally when you're actually using the language.

🔁

Spaced repetition

Study today, review tomorrow, then 3 days, then 1 week. The system remembers what you forget — so you actually keep it.

Daily habit: 45/15

45 minutes of listening to content you enjoy — YouTube, podcasts, TV — plus 15 minutes speaking. No extra commute. No textbook. Just habit.

Safe environment

Vietnamese professionals often freeze because mistakes feel public and dangerous. This method removes shame. You speak, get corrected gently, and move on.
