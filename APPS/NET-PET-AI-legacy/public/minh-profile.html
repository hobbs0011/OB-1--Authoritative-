Baseline & Progress

Age & Baseline: 5 Years Old. Highly articulate in Vietnamese, but initially incredibly selective about who he interacts with. Grandparents' involvement occasionally increased pressure.

Focus Areas: Correcting Phonics (CVCs), Applied Vocabulary, Confidence Building & Spontaneous Speaking.

Initial State: Minh filtered his thoughts through translation, locking up entirely around new faces due to pressure and a fear of mistakes.

Current State: Expressive, energetic, and increasingly comfortable. He initiates dialogues naturally rather than waiting to be prompted.

Key Breakthrough: "Projection". By allowing him to speak through his stuffed teddy bear, the social pressure of direct adult-interaction is entirely removed.

Parent Review Note: Minh performs best when the adults around him respond with curiosity and encouragement rather than test-style pressure. The more consistent that environment is across both parents, the faster his confidence stabilizes.
