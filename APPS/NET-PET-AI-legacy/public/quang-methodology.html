METHODOLOGY

Natural English Training · OBI System

Core Philosophy

NET — Natural English Training — teaches English through real-world stakes. The session is the career move. Every interaction in English carries weight because it connects directly to Quang's actual goals: passing interviews, landing freelance work, presenting with authority.

Grammar is incidental. Fluency comes from doing real things in English — negotiating, pitching, explaining, defending ideas. The language emerges from authentic need, not from worksheet completion. When Quang builds his matcha marketing deck, he is not "practicing English" — he is building his future, and English is simply the tool he uses to do it.

Quang's Teaching Vehicle

The matcha marketing project is Quang's primary vehicle — a real school assignment transformed into an English marketing campaign. Every session revolves around producing tangible deliverables: market analysis write-ups, brand positioning statements, pitch scripts, visual briefs. The school project becomes the English output engine.

Career setup sessions form the secondary vehicle. These include LinkedIn profile optimization, Fiverr gig creation, portfolio structuring, and outreach message drafting — all conducted in English. The governing principle: real output > textbook repetition. Quang is not rehearsing; he is producing assets that will live beyond the classroom.

The Specificity Protocol

Quang's main growth edge is specificity. Michael's standing rule: "Don't repeat my words back to me — apply them." Every session includes a moment where Quang must produce an original application of a concept, not a paraphrase of what was just said.

This protocol is the core of the methodology for him. When a principle is introduced — say, a marketing framework or a communication tactic — Quang must immediately generate his own example from his own context. He must take the abstract and make it concrete through his unique lens. This is where real cognitive ownership happens, and where English stops being a subject and becomes a medium of thought.

Phonics Track — S/SH Minimal Pairs

S/SH Minimal Pairs are Quang's dedicated phonics work. These drills target the distinction between /s/ and /ʃ/ — a critical articulation point that currently triggers Korean language detection in Gemini's speech analysis. The pronunciation goal is clear: pass the Gemini language detection test as a native-like English speaker.

Anchor words build from slow deliberate articulation to natural conversational speed. Each pair is practiced in isolation, then in carrier phrases, then in spontaneous sentence generation. The progression is: control → speed → automaticity.

Strategic Laziness — 5-Step Plan

Quang's productivity framework, developed in class, applies the principle of minimum effective dose to study and work habits. The goal is not to do more — it is to do only what moves the needle.

  1. Centralize tasks — Gather all commitments into one visible system; eliminate scattered to-do lists and mental notes.
  2. Identify crucial tasks — Apply the 80/20 filter: which 20% of tasks produce 80% of the results? Ruthlessly separate signal from noise.
  3. Schedule study blocks — Assign fixed, non-negotiable time windows for deep English work. Calendar before convenience.
  4. Automate reminders — Use tools (phone alarms, calendar notifications, recurring prompts) to remove cognitive load from remembering.
  5. Review and eliminate — Weekly audit: what tasks consumed time but produced zero impact? Cut them without guilt.
