The problem — and how this program addresses it

The problem: Staff at Oktava can write passable English but cannot sustain a spontaneous 10-minute conversation. By minute 8 they go silent and wait for prompts. In real client calls, meetings, and negotiations, that silence is the loss.

The root cause: Staff prepare scripts before meetings. When the other person goes off-script, they have nothing. They also think in Vietnamese and translate — which is slow and produces unnatural output. And they prioritize "sounding smart" over being understood.

The intervention: This program eliminates all three barriers at once. No pre-written material. No Vietnamese in class. No incentive to sound perfect. The only goal: communicate the idea, directly, in the fewest words possible.

The result Session 1 measured: 16 words acquired, 7 used spontaneously, 1 flag identified and corrected (question-well depletion). Staff who practiced KISS-style output showed measurably better conversational stamina within a single 90-minute session.

For Mr. Minh — talking points with the Oktava CEO

  • Different from last teacher: Previous teacher taught grammar and IELTS-style output. This program teaches workplace communication — the only kind that affects your bottom line.
  • Measurable from day one: Session 1 produced 7 live vocabulary acquisitions and identified one specific bottleneck (spontaneous question generation). You can see the data.
  • Homework is mandatory: One hour of English listening per staff member, per day. Non-negotiable. Without this the in-class time is wasted.
  • No scripts, ever: Staff are explicitly forbidden from bringing prepared questions. This forces them to learn to think in English — the only path to genuine fluency.
  • The 3-day rule: Miss three consecutive days and the session becomes a review, not a progression. This maintains accountability without being punitive.
  • ROI framing: Communication efficiency = faster meetings, fewer misunderstandings, better client relationships, staff who can represent the company in English without panic.
