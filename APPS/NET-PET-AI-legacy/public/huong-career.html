Professional Phrases

  • Opening a vendor email:

    "I'm reaching out regarding the upcoming [event name] on [date]."

  • Requesting a quote:

    "Could you please provide a quote for [service] for approximately [X] guests?"

  • Confirming a booking:

    "I'd like to confirm the booking for [date] — please send the contract at your earliest convenience."

  • Following up:

    "Just following up on my previous email — could you confirm availability by [date]?"

  • Briefing a client:

    "Here is the event brief for your review. Please let me know if you'd like to make any changes."

  • Day-of coordination:

    "We're on schedule — please make sure the setup is complete by [time]."

  • Post-event:

    "Thank you for your support at [event]. We'll send the debrief report within 48 hours."

Speed Control Practice

  • "Simultaneously"

    si-mul-TAY-nee-us-ly — 6 syllables, don't compress.

  • "Logistics"

    lo-JIS-tiks — stress the middle, don't drop the S ending.

  • "Coordination"

    co-or-di-NAY-shun — 5 syllables, hold each one.

  • "Registration"

    re-jis-TRAY-shun — land the T before the shun.

  • "Sponsorship"

    SPON-ser-ship — 3 clear beats.

Read this list aloud once slowly before every vendor call. Your clients hear your credibility in your delivery.
