## The Corporate Classroom Format

Corporate NET sessions differ fundamentally from consumer classes:

- **Duration**: 60 minutes (vs. 90 minutes consumer). Executives do not have 90-minute attention budgets.
- **Session frequency**: 3x per week minimum for measurable progress.
- **Class size**: Maximum 8 participants. Corporate groups above 8 are split.
- **Vocabulary focus**: Business English exclusively — negotiation, email, presentation, meeting facilitation.
- **Pace**: Accelerated. Corporate students are high-functioning adults. Slow pacing insults them.

The Michael Method applies in full — stealth correction, emotional translation, tribal engagement — but calibrated for professional adults, not students.
