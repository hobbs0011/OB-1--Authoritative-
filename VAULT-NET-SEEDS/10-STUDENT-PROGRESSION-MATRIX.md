## Tier Definitions and Behavioral Markers

**MOUSE**
- Refuses to speak without direct prompting
- Translates internally before every sentence (3-5 second delay visible)
- Answers in single words or nods
- Avoids eye contact during English moments
- Shuts down when corrected publicly

**CUB**
- Attempts sentences even when uncertain
- Makes consistent grammar errors but pushes through
- Asks clarifying questions in English (not Vietnamese)
- Laughs at their own errors instead of shutting down
- Volunteers answers 30-50% of the time unprompted

**TIGER**
- Initiates English conversation without prompting
- Creates English moments outside class (texts, work, family)
- Uses idioms and slang contextually and correctly
- Corrects their own errors mid-sentence
- Encourages other students to speak

---

## Promotion Criteria

**Mouse → Cub**: Student must produce 5+ unprompted full sentences in a single session on two consecutive weeks. Error rate irrelevant — volume and courage matter.

**Cub → Tiger**: Student must demonstrate spontaneous English usage outside classroom (reported or observed), correct own errors in real-time, and use at least 3 idioms correctly in context over two sessions.

---

## Demotion Protocol

Regression is tracked, not punished. A student is flagged for downgrade review when:
- Self-initiation drops below 20% for 3 consecutive sessions
- Student reverts to single-word answers after achieving Cub-level speech
- Attendance drops below 2 sessions per week for 2 weeks

Demotion review is private — never announced in group settings. Face-saving is law.

---

## What Triggers a Promotion Assessment

- Teacher observation flag: student meets promotion behavior markers
- CHATTY PRIME behavioral data: session logs show consistent target behaviors
- Both signals must align before assessment is scheduled

Assessment is a celebration, not a test. The student is told: *"We think you're ready for the next level."*

---

## Student Behavioral Database

CHATTY PRIME logs per session:
- Words used spontaneously (not prompted)
- Error types and frequency
- Self-correction instances
- Peer interaction rate
- Idiom and slang deployment

Data is used for promotion decisions, curriculum adaptation, and churn prediction.

---

## The Promotion Ceremony

Promotion is marked with a public acknowledgment in class. The student's new tier is announced. Tigers receive a digital Tiger badge. The group celebrates. Identity shift is the goal — the ceremony makes it real.

## Timeline Estimates

- Cub → Tiger: 8-16 weeks
- Tiger maintenance: ongoing — Tigers are invited into the Tiger Circle community
