## The Weekly Lesson Structure

Every NET lesson follows the 10-5-5-3 physics. No deviations. The structure is: vocabulary delivery (10 words), idiom injection (5 idioms), slang contextualization (5 slang terms), and 3-game rotation. This is not a suggestion — it is the chassis. Every lesson runs within 60 minutes. Timing is non-negotiable.

---

## Vocabulary Selection Criteria

The 10 words per lesson are not random. Each word must pass four gates:

1. **Frequency Rank** — Must appear in the top 3,000 most-used English words in business or daily conversation contexts relevant to Vietnam.
2. **Contextual Utility** — Can it be used in at least 3 different real-life situations this week?
3. **Emotional Resonance** — Does the word carry weight? Words like *ambition*, *momentum*, *deadline* land differently than *obtain* or *utilize*.
4. **Cross-Tier Application** — A Mouse can use it to survive, a Cub can use it to practice, a Tiger can deploy it with precision.

Words that pass only 2 of 4 gates are rejected. Substitutes are pulled from the weekly theme bank.

---

## Idiom Ranking System

Five idioms per lesson, ranked by a combined score of:

- **Street Frequency** (how often heard in casual conversation)
- **Professional Utility** (how often needed in workplace Vietnam)
- **Memorability** (rhyme, rhythm, surprise factor)

Top-ranked idioms ship first in the lesson. Lower-ranked idioms are introduced later when cognitive load is lower. No idiom is introduced cold — each is anchored with a story or a visual.

---

## Slang Tier — Contextual Appropriateness Gates

Five slang terms per lesson, split into two categories:

- **Street Slang** — safe for Tiger use in peer contexts, flagged for Mice as "observe, don't use yet"
- **Professional Slang** — appropriate in BlueTech corporate settings, cleared for Cub and Tiger deployment

Mice are never penalized for not using slang. Slang is presented as an unlock, not a requirement.

---

## The 3-Game Rotation System

Games always rotate in this sequence: **Competition → Collaboration → Creation**. The same game never repeats in consecutive lessons.

- **Competition Game** — Adrenaline encodes vocabulary. Speed rounds, point systems, winner recognition.
- **Collaboration Game** — Social proof. Teams must use this week's words to win together.
- **Creation Game** — Students generate original sentences, stories, or dialogues using the week's vocabulary. Ownership is the goal.

---

## Tier Adaptation Protocol

| Tier | Game Rule Complexity | Content Depth | Freedom Level |
|------|---------------------|---------------|---------------|
| Mouse | Simplified rules, visual aids | Core word usage only | Structured prompts |
| Cub | Standard rules, some autonomy | Context + idioms included | Semi-guided |
| Tiger | Full rules + self-moderation | All 10+5+5 deployed | Creative freedom |

---

## Assessment Integration

Assessments are embedded inside games. Teachers track:
- Word usage frequency per student (during games)
- Error types (pronunciation vs grammar vs confidence)
- Spontaneous usage (did the student use the word outside the game prompt?)

No separate test. No quiz sheet. Assessment is invisible to the student.

---

## Weekly Pacing Guide — 4-Week Month

- **Week 1**: Foundation theme — introduce anchor vocabulary
- **Week 2**: Application theme — same vocabulary in new contexts
- **Week 3**: Stress theme — vocabulary under pressure (debates, roleplay)
- **Week 4**: Mastery theme — student-led review, Tiger-generated content

---

## Content Theming System

Each week has a theme that anchors vocabulary selection. Examples: *Money & Ambition*, *Teamwork Under Pressure*, *Making Deals*, *Describing People*. Themes connect words to real Vietnamese life contexts — not textbook abstractions.
