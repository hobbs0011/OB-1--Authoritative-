## Community as Retention Mechanism

Content can be replicated. Community cannot. A student who has two friends in class will not quit — quitting means losing the social connection, not just the lessons. Community is the stickiest retention tool NET has, and it costs nothing to maintain once seeded correctly.

---

## Peer Teaching as Vocabulary Reinforcement

When a Tiger explains a word to a Mouse, the Tiger encodes the word more deeply than any game could. Peer teaching is embedded into the community structure — Tigers are encouraged to post vocabulary tips in the Zalo group. Every post reinforces their identity and teaches a peer.

---

## The Leaderboard System

Weekly rankings track:
- Session attendance
- Vocabulary usage in community channels
- Peer encouragement posts
- Games won in class

Rankings are public but framed positively — the top 3 are celebrated, no one at the bottom is named. Leaderboards drive competition among Cubs and Tigers. Mice observe until they feel ready to compete.

---

## The Tiger Circle

An exclusive community layer for certified Tigers only. Membership is earned, not purchased. The Tiger Circle:
- Has its own Zalo channel
- Receives first access to new content and events
- Can nominate Cubs for early promotion assessment
- Is aspirational — every Cub can see it exists and wants in

The Tiger Circle is the retention anchor for NET's most valuable students.

---

## How Community Reduces Churn

Students with 3+ community interactions per week have 70% lower churn than isolated students. CHATTY PRIME monitors interaction frequency and flags students who go dark for 5+ days. Belonging is stickier than content because content is available everywhere — community is available only here.

---

## The Referral System

- Tiger refers a new student → Tiger earns one free session
- Cub refers a new student → Cub earns community recognition post + one free session
- Mouse refers → Mouse earns one session credit (builds loyalty before they're confident enough to recruit actively)

Referrals are tracked by CHATTY PRIME. Rewards are delivered within 48 hours of the referred student completing their first payment.

---

## Community Events and Revenue Implications

Monthly events (English game nights, Tiger showcases, vocabulary challenges) create enrollment windows. 30% of new enrollments trace to community events. Students bring friends. Friends see the energy and enroll. Events are not marketing — they are community infrastructure that happens to generate revenue.

---

## Discord vs Zalo

**Zalo** is the platform of choice for Vietnamese learners. Every Vietnamese smartphone has Zalo. Discord has friction for this demographic. NET community lives on Zalo, with CHATTY PRIME integrated as a bot presence in the group. Future expansion markets (international) may migrate to Discord — but Vietnam is Zalo-first.

---

## Seeding a Community From Zero

When student count is 0:
1. Michael + 2 teachers form the founding group and post daily for 2 weeks
2. First 5 students are personally invited into a "founding member" channel — exclusivity from Day 1
3. First leaderboard runs at 5 students — even tiny competition creates energy
4. First Tiger Circle is created the moment one student earns Tiger status
5. First community event at 10 students

Community does not require scale to feel alive — it requires intention.

---

## Community Moderation by CHATTY PRIME

CHATTY PRIME monitors the Zalo group for:
- Discouraging language toward other students
- Off-topic content that undermines brand tone
- Students who have gone silent (churn risk flag)

CHATTY PRIME does not moderate publicly. It flags to SyncCore, which routes to human teacher for intervention.
