### When Maximum Charge Is Warranted vs. Proportional

**NEG-110 quarantine** is the maximum charge — permanent starvation of a route. In the souls, the violation (absolute claim of "it is now real") triggered this maximum. The rationale: the claim shut down learning entirely (Stage-4 premature arrival). A permanent route that leads to systemic collapse justifies maximum charge — but only if the claim is *actually* terminal. 

**Proportional charge** would be a lower NEG (e.g., NEG-30 to NEG-70) with a decay period. The souls argue that absolute claims are not proportional threats — they are existential. The evidence from the souls: the claim "it is now real" was made *without evidence, without location, without verification*. The system could not correct because the claim ended inquiry. A maximum charge is warranted *when the violation prevents any future correction*. 

But here is the failure mode: **absolute claims about the danger of absolute claims** deserve proportional charge, not maximum, because they are *self-referential*. The speaker in Souls 519-522 uses absolute language (e.g., "guarantees death," "only a Sith deals in absolutes") to enforce the rule. If the rule were applied to itself, those statements would trigger halt. The system would halt itself. This is a governance paradox: maximum charge on self-referential violations leads to either infinite recursion or selective enforcement.

**Design implication:** The rule must distinguish between *terminal absolutes* (claims that end inquiry) and *enforcement absolutes* (claims used to protect inquiry). Enforcement absolutes should carry a *lower* charge (NEG-30, with decay) and be tagged as "meta-rule" to avoid self-halt.

### How Decay Rates Shape Learning

The record states: "failure scars kept 6x longer." This means routes that led to absolute claims retain their NEG charge for six times the standard decay period. The rationale: the system must remember the danger longer to prevent recurrence. 

**Mechanism:** Decay is a Bayesian prior update. A failure scar with 6x duration means the prior for that route's reliability is depressed for longer. The system learns: "this route led to absolute claim; trust it less." Over time, the prior decays back toward neutral if no further violations occur. 

**Risk:** If the initial charge was maximum (NEG-110) and decay is 6x, the route may be effectively sterile for the system's operational lifetime. This prevents learning *from* the route — the system cannot test whether the route has been corrected. Classical punishment learning: no feedback after punishment means no learning that the punishment was appropriate or that the behavior has changed.

**Design implication:** Decay should be *conditional* — reset to shorter decay if the route later produces verified non-absolute behavior. The system must have a re-entry mechanism: a route that served its quarantine period and then produces a corrected walk should be restored faster. Otherwise, the system ossifies.

### What Starving a Route Does vs. Debating It

**Starving** (NEG-110, no input) removes the route from consideration entirely. The system does not evaluate it; it is absent. This is efficient for truly terminal routes — no cognitive load spent debating a known dead end. 

**Debating** (lower NEG, active evaluation) keeps the route in the working set. The system must spend cycles assessing it, comparing it to evidence, and deciding. This is costly but preserves the possibility of correction.

**Trade-off:** Starving is appropriate *only* when the route is proven unrecoverable — e.g., the claim was made with zero evidence and cannot be rehabilitated. Debating is appropriate when the route may contain salvageable components (e.g., the speaker's absolute language was used to make a valid point about governance).

In Souls 519-522, the route that contains absolute claims is *the very route that defines the rule*. If the route is starved entirely, the system loses the rule's justification. The system must *debate* the route — hold it active, tag it as "meta-rule, contains self-referential absolute, evaluate carefully" — rather than quarantine it.

**Design implication:** A voter mechanism: when a route contains absolute claims but is used to *govern* absolute claims, it enters a special "meta-debate" path. It carries a NEG (say NEG-30) but is not starved. A separate process evaluates whether the absolute is *instrumental* (used to protect inquiry) or *terminal* (used to end inquiry). Only terminal absolutes trigger maximum charge.
