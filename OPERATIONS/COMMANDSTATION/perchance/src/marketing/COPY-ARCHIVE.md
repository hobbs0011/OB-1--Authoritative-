# COPY-ARCHIVE — the distillery's record

The distillery is `src/copy.js`. This file is its extraction rules, its
sources, and its standing debts. Append-only: a new stock names its source and
date; nothing here is ever rewritten.

## Extraction rules (standing)

1. **Transcribed, never polished.** A stock line reads exactly as the codex or
   creative rendered it. If the source has a typo, the typo ships with a
   `[sic]` note in the record, not a silent fix.
2. **The business's claims ride as the business's words.** 80-vs-30, the
   Lakeview free diagnostic, the Puppy Dog Close — carried verbatim, never
   upgraded into station measurements. `lane:offer` and `lane:proof` lines are
   quarantined by lane for this reason.
3. **Vietnamese is copied off the pixels, never retyped from memory.**
   Diacritics are precise or the line does not enter stock.
4. **No stock without a source.** Every line traces to `src/sites/net/CODEX.md`
   (page) or `CREATIVES.md` (piece). The page/piece is named below.
5. **The voice protocol is Gospel 444, codex p14** — Canadian Clarity, Radical
   Candor, Permission Culture, anti-shame, one ask per piece.
