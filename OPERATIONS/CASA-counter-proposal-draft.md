Dear Anh Minh,

Thank you for the offer. I want to make this work. The curriculum authority you confirmed is the role I was looking for, and the visa support removes the biggest friction. That said, the terms need to move before I can sign.

## Role

**Title:** Head of English Curriculum and Instruction
- Final authority on all English teaching materials, methods, assessment, and parent communication
- You make the final business decisions. I make the final English decisions. That is the division.
- Miss Hien has zero authority over the English program. No exceptions.

## Compensation

My hard floor is **500,000 VND/hour gross** (400,000 VND/hour net after 10% tax).

**Full-Time Option:**
- 160 hours/month
- Gross: 80,000,000 VND/month
- Net after tax: 72,000,000 VND/month
- Effective rate: 500,000 VND/hr gross / 450,000 VND/hr net

**Part-Time Option:**
- 80 hours/month
- Gross: 40,000,000 VND/month
- Net after tax: 36,000,000 VND/month
- Effective rate: 500,000 VND/hr gross / 450,000 VND/hr net

Either option works at this rate. Full-Time is better if you want me fully embedded. Part-Time is better if you want me at my highest energy for the kids and the program.

## Non-Negotiables

1. **Classroom observation:** Miss Hien may observe my classes if she schedules it with me in advance. Maximum once per week. Observational only — not evaluative. No photos or video of me or the students without my explicit permission for each instance. No behind-the-back reporting to Ang Bao.

2. **Breaks:** Morning break 15–30 minutes. Afternoon break 15–30 minutes. Not a single 2–2.5 hour block in the middle of the day. I need real recovery time to perform at the level you want. Eight hours on wooden chairs with no real break is not sustainable.

3. **Curriculum:** I design and control all English curriculum, materials, vocabulary lists, and teaching methods. Cambridge templates are a reference, not a mandate. If the curriculum says "teach coding to two-year-olds," that recommendation will be overridden. The kids learn language they can actually use in play and daily life — not words selected because they look impressive on a brochure.

4. **Monday and Wednesday evenings:** Protected. I have a commitment at Lakeview on those nights. This is not negotiable.

5. **Work permit:** School covers all costs. No repayment clause if I complete the two-year contract.

6. **Visa:** Two-year guarantee tied to performance and contract completion.

## What You Get

- A teacher the kids ask for by name. Not "teacher is friendly" — they actually look for me.
- Parent relationships I maintain personally — you already hear the feedback. That feedback converts to enrollment.
- Curriculum that actually works for the age group. I'm not teaching "program" and "bug" to children who can't say "hello." I'm teaching words they encounter in play, on the playground, in their daily lives — and building the sentences around them.
- Someone who shows up early, stays late when needed, and treats this as a partnership — not a paycheck. I'm not just here to collect a salary. I'm here because I see the path you're building and I want to help build it.

## What I Need From You

1. Which compensation option you prefer
2. Written confirmation of the terms above
3. Updated contract reflecting all of this

I'm not signing for 50M. I'm not signing for 80 hours a week at a rate that means I'm making less than I can earn privately. I'm signing for a role where I can deliver the results you want without resenting the job.

Let me know.
