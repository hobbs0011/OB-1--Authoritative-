These sources document interactive classroom management and language instruction sessions led by Michael Hobbs using the Gemini AI assistant across 7 distinct class groups at a Vietnamese high school. The materials include complete student rosters, vocabulary drills, discipline protocols, one-on-one oral assessments, career path debates, and the teacher's "Strategic Laziness" philosophy.
