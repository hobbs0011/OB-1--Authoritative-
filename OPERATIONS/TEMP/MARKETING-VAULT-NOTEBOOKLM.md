### 3.2 Typography

| Font | Usage | Vibe |
|---|---|---|
| **Orbitron** | Headlines, hero text, impact statements | Cyberpunk, futuristic, bold |
| **Rajdhani** | Subheadings, secondary headlines | Technical, clean, modern |
| **Inter** | Body text, descriptions, long-form, Vietnamese text | Glassmorphism, readable, professional |

**Typography rules:**

- Headlines: BIG. Oversized. Fill the visual space. Orbitron in uppercase or title case.
- Body: Inter at readable sizes. Never cramped. Generous line spacing.
- Numbers and stats: Orbitron or Rajdhani. Make numbers LARGE and prominent.
- Vietnamese text: Use Inter. Ensure proper diacritical mark rendering (ă, â, đ, ê, ô, ơ, ư + all tone marks).
- Never use serif fonts, handwriting fonts, or Comic Sans.

- **Strong emotional framing** — every image tells a story, creates a feeling

**Social media dimensions:**

## §5 TEACHING METHODOLOGY AS MARKETING CONTENT

### 5.1 Mouse → Tiger Progression — The Core Visual Motif

**Mouse Stage:** Silent, afraid, unable to produce English. Most Vietnamese children arrive here after years of center education that taught grammar rules but never gave permission to speak.

**Cub Stage:** Producing sounds, words, short phrases. Volume increases. Confidence builds. Mistakes celebrated, not corrected.

**Tiger Stage:** Speaks freely, loudly, with natural English rhythm. Initiates conversations. Thinks in English. No longer translates in head.

**Visual art:** Create a 3-stage progression graphic — Mouse (small, gray, quiet) → Cub (growing, purple, speaking) → Tiger (large, gold, roaring). "Con bạn đang ở giai đoạn nào?" (What stage is your child at?) — engagement hook.

### 5.2 The 4 Pillars — Michael's Teaching Method

> *Source: GOSPEL-444-TEACHING-METHODS-CONSOLIDATED.md (58KB) + NET-TEACHING-METHODOLOGY.md (40KB)*

| Pillar | What It Means | Visual Expression |
|---|---|---|
| **Emotional Translation** | Attach feelings to language, not rules | Show JOY on faces, not textbook pages |
| **Tribal Engagement** | Create belonging — "we" not "you" | Show groups, community, insider language |
| **Stealth Correction** | Fix errors without shaming | Show gentle calibration, NOT red marks |
| **Narrative Escalation** | Every lesson tells a story that builds | Show adventure, progression, chapters |

### 5.3 The Anchor Principle — Origin Quote

> *Source: RAW-GEMINI-DENNY-PHONICS-ANCHOR-PRINCIPLE-ORIGIN.md — Michael's own words*

"Focus on what they DO know, not what they don't." When a student struggles with CH/F/SH sounds, don't drill the failure. Find the sounds they CAN make. Anchor there. Build outward.

**Visual marketing:** "Chúng tôi bắt đầu từ điều con bạn ĐÃ BIẾT" (We start from what your child ALREADY KNOWS). Contrast with centers that start from what's wrong.

### 5.4 The 10-5-5-3 Lesson Physics

> *Source: LESSON-PHYSICS-DEEP-DIVE.md*

Every lesson: **10 vocabulary words, 5 idioms, 5 slang expressions, 3 games.**

- **Why 10 vocabulary?** Miller's Law — the human brain can hold 7±2 items in working memory. 10 pushes the upper limit without overwhelming.
- **Why 3 games?** Cognitive architecture: Game 1 = competition (adrenaline), Game 2 = collaboration (bonding), Game 3 = creation (ownership).
- **Why 60 minutes?** Attention arc: 15min warm-up → 30min core → 15min games.
- **Cut Protocol:** If energy drops, cut the planned content. Never push through boredom.

**Visual art:** "Lesson anatomy" infographic showing the 10-5-5-3 structure with brain science callouts.

### 5.5 Brain Rot Doctrine — Digital Age Positioning

> *Source: BLUETECH-NETWORKING-DINNER-TEAM-BUILDING-EXTRACTION.md*

TikTok and short-form content are **chemical addiction** — dopamine loops that destroy attention span. The **No Phone Protocol** is enforced in all lessons. But this doesn't mean anti-technology — OBI AI for structured practice vs. mindless scrolling.

**Visual marketing:** "Chúng tôi CẤM điện thoại trong lớp. Nhưng chúng tôi DÙNG AI sau lớp." (We BAN phones in class. But we USE AI after class.)

### 5.6 Jolly Phonics Foundation (Ages 5-7)

**Jolly Phonics** — systematic, multi-sensory approach teaching reading and writing through sounds (phonemes) rather than letter names. CVC word building: c-a-t, d-o-g.

Target parents searching "phonics cho bé." "Phonics — nền tảng đọc viết tiếng Anh."

### 5.7 Anti-Shame Doctrine

The word "wrong" is **NEVER** used in NET. The word is **"calibrate."**

- Never show red X marks, error symbols, or failure imagery
- "Không có SAI — chỉ có ĐIỀU CHỈNH" (There is no WRONG — only CALIBRATION)

### 5.8 Tiger Voice Protocol — The Mastery Signal

> *Source: VAULT-NET-SEEDS/15-TIGER-VOICE-PROTOCOL.md*

**5 markers:** Pace, Projection, Vocabulary Density, Confidence Signals, Zero Hesitation.

**3-Step Activation:** Ronaldo vs. Messi technique — Listen → Mirror → Own.

**Anti-Tiger patterns** (Vietnamese learners): whispering, covering mouth, looking down, "I don't know" before trying, translating every word mentally.

**Visual art:** 5 markers as visual checklist. Before: anti-Tiger patterns. After: all 5 markers. "Bạn sẽ NGHE sự khác biệt."

### 5.9 The "I Don't Know" Celebration

> *Source: DEC-6-2025-BLUETECH-BEGINNER-CLASS-RAW-TRANSCRIPT.md*

When a student says "I don't know," Michael celebrates it. "That's the most important phrase in English — it means you're HONEST."

**Visual art:** "Ở NET, 'Tôi không biết' là câu trả lời DŨNG CẢM NHẤT."

### 5.10 Selling Feelings, Not Features
