## Stage 96: Pre-Zenith Final Gate

Five stages remain. The Pre-Zenith Final Gate is the last checkpoint before the pipeline enters its terminal sequence. **Condition 1: All prior exit criteria verified.** Every exit criterion from Stage 1 through Stage 95 is confirmed satisfied. This is not a re-audit — the Forensic Audit Complete (Stage 94) has already done that work. This is a certification that the Stage 94 audit itself passed with full consensus, and that no new information has emerged since Stage 94 that would call any stage's status from Stage 1 through Stage 94 into question.

**Condition 2: Phoenix Pulse ≥92.** The system's current Phoenix Pulse, as reported by the Watchtower Network synthesis node, must be at or above 92 out of 100 at the time the gate is evaluated. **Condition 3: Multi-chain consensus confirmed.** All three hash chains — the SQLite ledger, the Merkle Lattice, and the external timestamp record — must agree exactly, with the most recent consensus check completed within the last 24 hours.

**Condition 4: All franchise locations green.** Every active franchise location must show a Franchise Performance Benchmark score above its minimum threshold. Any franchise location in corrective action, under a compliance hold, or showing a declining QDD trend is a gate blocker. Phoenix does not reach Zenith while any of its operating locations is struggling. The whole system must be healthy.

**Condition 5: Soul DNA sealed.** The Soul DNA Final Compilation (Stage 92) must be confirmed sealed — no documents in an open editing state, all hashes computed and verified, Zenith Readiness Statement signed.

**Condition 6: Golden Block intact.** The Golden Block Verification (Stage 91) must remain valid. The Golden Block's hash, as currently stored in the SQLite ledger at layer 250, must still match the Phase V Root Certificate. Any ledger anomaly at layer 250 is a critical gate blocker.

**Condition 7: Agent-100 physical authorization confirmed.** Agent-100 must be physically present and must authorize the gate opening with their offline cryptographic key — the key that has never been connected to any online system. This is not a delegation opportunity. Only Agent-100's physical key can open the Pre-Zenith Gate.

**Condition 8: Agent-99 operational readiness confirmed.** Agent-99 must issue a formal readiness declaration — a document stating that the system is operationally ready for Zenith and that no known unresolved operational issues exist that would compromise the final stages. Agent-99's declaration is countersigned by the 6-model jury with 100% consensus (not 95% supermajority — 100%, all six models, unanimous).

**Condition 9: Replication site operational.** The international replication site activated in Stage 88 must be operational, generating real student sessions, connected to the Global Trail Matrix, and passing its own local QDD health check.

**Condition 10: The Silence Protocol cleared.** For 72 hours before the gate evaluation, all non-essential system activity is suspended — no franchise onboarding, no curriculum updates, no infrastructure changes. This is the system proving it can sustain itself without any external input or maintenance — a final proof of autonomy before Zenith.
