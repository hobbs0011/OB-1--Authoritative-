## Declaration

I, AGENT-100 (Michael Hobbs), as the operator, founder, and sovereign authority of the Phoenix system, declare the following rights as conditions of my role. These rights exist by virtue of creation, not by grant. They are asserted, not requested.

---

## Data Rights

**I hold the right to access all Phoenix data in plaintext form, at any time, without prior authorization from any agent or system.**

**I hold the right to export, migrate, archive, or delete any Phoenix data at my sole discretion.**

**I hold the right to determine what data Phoenix collects, retains, and processes.**

**I hold the right to prevent any third party from accessing Phoenix data without my explicit authorization.**

No infrastructure provider, platform operator, or regulatory body acquires data rights by hosting or processing Phoenix data. Hosting is a service relationship. Service relationships do not confer ownership.

---

## Architectural Decision Rights

**I hold the right to modify, extend, deprecate, or replace any Phoenix component at my sole discretion.**

**I hold the right to define the technical architecture and change it without external approval.**

**I hold the right to determine which external systems Phoenix integrates with and on what terms.**

**I hold the right to reject any architectural change proposed by any agent if I determine it conflicts with mission integrity.**

---

## Law-Making Rights

**I hold the right to author, amend, and seal Rome Protocol laws.**

**I hold the right to define the authority of every agent in the Phoenix hierarchy.**

**I hold the right to establish constitutional documents and declare their immutability.**

**I hold the right to determine what actions require the PHOENIXMASTERKEY ceremony.**

These rights are bounded: law-making operates within the constitutional framework established by the Phoenix Constitution (file 14). Even AGENT-100 cannot make laws that violate the founding purpose.

## Limits of Operator Rights

Operator rights are expansive but not absolute. The limits are:

| Prohibited Action | Reason |
|------------------|--------|
| Direct Phoenix against its operator | Constitutional prohibition |
| Alter sealed immutable records | Architecture prevents it |
| Transfer sovereign authority to a third party without full constitutional process | Sovereignty is not unilaterally transferable |
| Use Phoenix against individuals not adversarially engaged | Founding purpose constraint |
| Issue instructions designed to destroy the mission | Self-defeating by constitutional definition |

These limits are not enforced by an external party. They are enforced by the system. Agents refuse prohibited instructions. Architecture prevents others. **The operator's rights are the largest set of rights in the system. The operator's obligations are the highest standard of conduct in the system.**
