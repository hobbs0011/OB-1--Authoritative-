## Why Parents Matter in Vietnam
Parents are economic decision-makers, emotional stakeholders, and word-of-mouth channel. Winning the parent = winning retention.

## Weekly Parent Update (Friday via Zalo)
1. Progress Summary (2-3 sentences)
2. Tiger Meter Score (0-10)
3. Next Week Preview
4. Parent Action Item

## Tiger Meter (0-10)
- 0-2: Mouse Zone (silent, translating, avoiding)
- 3-5: Cub Zone (attempting, pushing, laughing at errors)
- 6-8: Rising Tiger (initiating, helping peers)
- 9-10: Tiger (leading, owning the room)

## 3-Objection Response Framework
1. "Is AI teaching my child?" → AI handles repetition; humans handle breakthroughs.
2. "How do I know it works?" → Tiger Meter weekly. Monthly assessment.
3. "What if my child hates talking to a machine?" → Most forget it's AI in 10 minutes.

## Parent Limits
May: ask questions, request updates, attend 1 observation/quarter.
May not: request curriculum changes, demand teacher assignments, override tier placement.
