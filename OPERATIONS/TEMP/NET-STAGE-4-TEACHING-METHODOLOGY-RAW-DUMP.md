## [SECTION 1: FOUR PILLARS PEDAGOGY]

### PILLAR 1 — LEARN LIKE A NATIVE
- Natural acquisition vs grammar drilling
- 60% comprehension threshold (Krashen's i+1)
- Context-based learning
- Minimal explicit grammar
- Immersion methodology
- Sound and meaning before rules

### PILLAR 2 — COMPREHENSIBLE INPUT (60% Rule)
- Content must be 60-80% understandable
- Below 60% = student lost
- Above 90% = no growth
- Focus on context, not individual words
- Daily practice: 45 minutes listening
- Use subtitles strategically (VN→EN→None)
- Choose content student ENJOYS

### PILLAR 3 — JOLLY PHONICS (42 Sounds)
- Systematic pronunciation training
- 42 English phonics sounds
- Groups: s,a,t,i,p,n → c/k,e,h,r,m,d → etc.
- Sound-first approach
- Visual + auditory learning
- Repetition drills

### PILLAR 4 — SPACED REPETITION
- Today → Tomorrow → 3 days → Week → Month
- Language becomes muscle memory
- Timed interval review
- Automation through repetition

## [SECTION 3: MOUSE → TIGER TRANSFORMATION]

### MOUSE VOICE CHARACTERISTICS:
- Quiet, mumbles, trails off
- Apologizes constantly ("Sorry for my bad English")
- Translates from Vietnamese mentally
- Freezes with native speakers
- Studies grammar endlessly
- Perfectionist mindset
- Avoids eye contact
- Self-deprecating

### TIGER VOICE CHARACTERISTICS:
- Loud, clear, projects voice
- Makes mistakes without apology
- Thinks in English directly
- Approaches foreigners confidently
- Practices speaking first
- Growth mindset
- Strong eye contact
- Self-assured

### TRANSFORMATION PROCESS:
- Phase 1 (Week 1-2): Awareness - Identify Mouse behaviors
- Phase 2 (Week 3-6): Practice - Call out Mouse, praise Tiger
- Phase 3 (Week 7-12): Integration - Raise stakes, celebrate milestones
- Phase 4 (Month 4+): Mastery - Tiger becomes default

### TEACHER INTERVENTIONS:
- "You're using Mouse Voice" script
- "Speak louder" - force projection
- "No translation" - break habit
- "Mistakes are wins" - reframe errors

### ASSESSMENT METRICS:
- Volume level (1-10 scale)
- Apology count per class
- Eye contact frequency
- Voluntary participation rate
- Record audio samples monthly

## [SECTION 4: ANTI-SHAME PEDAGOGY]

### CORE PRINCIPLE: Confidence First, Accuracy Second

### PROHIBITED LANGUAGE:
- "Wrong"
- "Bad"
- "Stupid"
- "Failure"

### REQUIRED LANGUAGE:
- "Shift"
- "Calibrate"
- "Upgrade"
- "Refine"
- "Try again"
- "Good effort"

### FEEDBACK PROTOCOLS:
Sandwich Method: Praise → Correction → Praise
Example:
1. "Great job speaking for 30 seconds!"
2. "Let's fix this: 'I go' should be 'I went'"
3. "Your confidence is excellent!"

### EMOTIONAL SAFETY:
- Create safe space for mistakes
- Celebrate errors as learning data
- Never compare students negatively
- Make English FUN, not a chore
- Build community support

## [SECTION 5: CONFIDENCE ALGORITHM]

### THE 5-STEP LOOP:
1. Success Loop - Easy wins build momentum
2. Sound Priming - Activation sentences warmup
3. Momentum - Keep moving, no freezing
4. Humorous Pressure - Emotional memory creation
5. Reward - Verbal praise, laughter, group support

### ASSESSMENT TOOL: Mirroring Technique
- Teacher says: "I went to the park"
- Student repeats WITH CONFIDENCE
- Focus on projection and clarity
- No translation allowed

### ENERGY MANAGEMENT:
- Read the room constantly
- Adjust pace/activities based on energy
- High energy = competitive games
- Low energy = storytelling/listening

## [SECTION 6: PRONUNCIATION TRAINING]

### JOLLY PHONICS 42 SOUNDS (Groups):

- Group 1: s, a, t, i, p, n
- Group 2: c/k, e, h, r, m, d
- Group 3: g, o, u, l, f, b
- Group 4: ai, j, oa, ie, ee, or
- Group 5: z, w, ng, v, oo (long/short), y
- Group 6: x, ch, sh, th (voiced/unvoiced), qu
- Group 7: ou, oi, ue, er, ar

### DRILL METHODOLOGY:
- Visual cards with sound symbols
- Repetition (10-15 times per sound)
- Minimal pairs practice (R vs L, V vs W)
- Word stress exercises
- Sentence rhythm training

### COMMON VIETNAMESE SPEAKER ISSUES:
- /th/ sounds (THis, THat, THink)
- /r/ vs /l/ confusion
- /v/ vs /w/ confusion
- Final consonant deletion
- Past tense -ed pronunciation

## [SECTION 7: CLASSROOM TECHNIQUES]

### ACTIVE CONVERSATION (Not Lectures):
- 70% English / 30% Vietnamese (beginners)
- 90% English / 10% Vietnamese (intermediate+)
- Use visuals, gestures, context
- Repeat key phrases multiple times

### MIMIC-FIRST APPROACH:
- Listen to phrase
- Repeat exactly (intonation, rhythm, emotion)
- Don't analyze grammar
- Build muscle memory

### CHORAL REPETITION:
- Entire class repeats together
- Builds confidence through group safety
- Tiger energy encouraged

### PAIR PRACTICE:
- Students drill in pairs
- Peer support and accountability
- Low-stakes before full class
