# 16 — COMMUNITY GROWTH ENGINE

> **Authority**: AGENT-100 (Michael Hobbs)
> **Status**: IMMUTABLE / LOCKED

## Community as Retention
Content can be replicated. Community cannot. A student with 2 friends in class will not quit.

## Key Mechanisms
- **Peer Teaching**: Tigers explain words to Mice → deeper encoding for Tigers
- **Leaderboard**: Weekly rankings (attendance, vocab usage, games won). Top 3 celebrated, no one at bottom named.
- **Tiger Circle**: Exclusive community for certified Tigers. Earned, not purchased. Aspirational for Cubs.

## Churn Data
3+ community interactions/week = 70% lower churn than isolated students.

## Referral System
- Tiger refers → 1 free session
- Cub refers → recognition post + 1 free session
- Mouse refers → 1 session credit
Rewards delivered within 48h.

## Community Events
30% of new enrollments trace to community events. Events = enrollment windows.

## Platform: Zalo (Vietnam-first). Discord for future international expansion.

## Seeding From Zero
1. Michael + 2 teachers post daily for 2 weeks
2. First 5 students = "founding members"
3. First leaderboard at 5 students
4. Tiger Circle created at first Tiger
5. First event at 10 students
