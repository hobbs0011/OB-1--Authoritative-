# Thumbnail & Icon Spec — Skibidi Zombie: Survive the Brainrots

**GOAL: MAKE MONEY.** Thumbnail = #1 conversion factor on Roblox discovery. Bad thumbnail = invisible game.

## Game Icon (512x512)

### Requirements
- **Size:** 512x512 px
- **Format:** PNG, no transparency
- **Style:** High contrast, readable at 50x50 (mobile grid)

### Composition
- **Center:** Main character (player avatar silhouette) fighting a Skibidi Zombie
- **Background:** Neon green/purple gradient (brainrot energy)
- **Text overlay:** "SKIBIDI ZOMBIE" in bold, distressed font
- **Bottom text:** "SURVIVE THE BRAINROTS" smaller
- **Visual hooks:**
  - Explosion/energy effect behind the fight
  - At least one recognizable brainrot meme element (toilet, sigma pose)
  - English words floating as projectiles/power-ups

### Color Palette
| Color | Hex | Usage |
|-------|-----|-------|
| Neon Green | #00FF88 | Primary energy, health |
| Electric Purple | #9B59B6 | Brainrot aura |
| Hot Red | #FF4444 | Danger, enemies |
| Gold | #FFD700 | Coins, rewards |
| Deep Black | #111111 | Background base |
| White | #FFFFFF | Text |

### What NOT to do
- No text smaller than 20% of icon height
- No more than 5 words total
- No dark-on-dark (invisible on mobile)
- No generic "zombie" art — must be BRAINROT themed
- No copyrighted characters

## Roblox Discovery SEO Notes

- Thumbnail brightness: aim for 60%+ average brightness (dark thumbnails get fewer clicks)
- Face in thumbnail: human-like faces increase CTR by 15-20%
- Numbers in thumbnail: "100+ ENEMIES" type callouts increase curiosity clicks
- Update thumbnails every 2-4 weeks to stay fresh in algorithm
- A/B test: rotate 2 hero thumbnails weekly, track CCU changes
