## What Tiger Voice Means
Not perfect grammar. Not an accent. The decision to speak English like you own the room — with presence, pace, and zero apology.

## 5 Tiger Voice Markers
1. **Pace** — Deliberate, not rushed. Tigers slow down.
2. **Projection** — Volume signals presence, not loudness.
3. **Vocabulary Density** — "efficient" not "good". Precision over volume.
4. **No Filler Apologies** — Delete "I think maybe perhaps..."
5. **Zero Hesitation** — Commit before you finish. Correct mid-stream.

## 3-Step Activation Sequence
1. Lower the stakes (question they know the answer to)
2. Reflect confidence back ("That was Tiger. Did you notice?")
3. Escalate one level immediately

## Ronaldo vs Messi Technique
Tribal identity debate eliminates fear of speaking. Topic matters more than grammar.

## Measurement (0-3 per marker, max 15)
- 12-15: Tiger Voice active
- 6-11: Cub range
- 0-5: Mouse range

## Anti-Tiger Patterns to Eliminate
- Rising tone on statements (tonal transfer)
- "Right?" after every sentence
- Nervous laughter mid-sentence
- Internal Vietnamese translation producing simple English
- Over-apologizing before speaking
