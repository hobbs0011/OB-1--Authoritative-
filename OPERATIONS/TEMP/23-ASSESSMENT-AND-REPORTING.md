## Monthly Assessment: 20 min, stealth (embedded in game)
Students don't know they're being assessed. Three game rounds designed to elicit target behaviors.

## Five Competencies
1. Vocabulary Retention Rate
2. Idiom Usage Frequency
3. Speaking Fluency Score (AI-measured latency + completion rate)
4. Pronunciation Accuracy (AI phoneme analysis)
5. Confidence Index (teacher-rated 1-10)

## Student Progress Report (1 page, visual)
- Tiger Meter trajectory graph
- Five competency radar chart
- 3 wins in green
- 1 growth edge (opportunity, not failure)
- Current tier badge

## Parent Report (executive summary)
- Overall Progress %
- Next Milestone
- Recommended Action
