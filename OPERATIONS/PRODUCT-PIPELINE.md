## 4. FEATURE PIPELINE

### Face-Saving Protocol
> "Saving face is a huge thing in Vietnam"

Family shame and family glory are the most powerful accountability mechanics in the Vietnamese context. Public commitment — family knows you enrolled, family sees your progress — prevents quitting. This is not a Western motivational gimmick. It is cultural infrastructure.

**Implementation status:** `[ ] NOT BUILT`

Design notes when building:
- Opt-in public commitment at enrollment
- Progress visible to designated family contact (optional)
- Graduation event / certificate designed for family sharing

## 5. CHEAT VECTOR NOTE

**NOTE ONLY — no implementation spec here**

Known vector: students leaving YouTube on in background while playing Free Fire (or other games) to fake Chrome extension engagement without actually watching content.

Why this matters:
- Fake engagement = no actual learning
- No actual learning = 12-18 month graduation promise fails
- Promise fails = no testimonials, no referrals, no growth
- It also directly reflects on NET's ability to deliver results

This is not a morality issue. It is a business integrity issue. The product only works if students learn. Metrics that can be gamed without learning undermine the entire model.

Detection and mitigation strategy: tracked separately. Do not spec here.
