## Teaching Framework

- **10-5-5-3:** 10 vocab, 5 idioms, 5 slang, 3 games per session
- **Anti-Shame:** Say *calibrate*, never *wrong*
- **Scoring:** Mouse (0-49) → Cub (50-79) → Tiger (80-100)
- **The Michael Method:** Emotional Translation, Tribal Engagement, Stealth Correction, Narrative Escalation
- **Quality Test:** Student spoke 70%+? Every correction without "wrong"? ≥3 new vocab in self-generated sentences? Genuine laughter? Higher energy at exit?
