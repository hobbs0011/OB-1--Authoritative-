## Lesson Plan: "The BlueTech Interview"

### Warm-Up (5 min) — The Anchor Game
- Throw 3 sentences with nonsense words mixed in
- Both listen for the anchor word
- Example: "Yesterday I flibbergibbet ate BREAKFAST at the glorpnax office."
- Ask: "What word did you hear?" → BREAKFAST / OFFICE
- Quy catches anchors. Mai Tram catches full phrases.

### Act 1 (10 min) — Interview Role-Play
Mai Tram is the interviewer. Quy is the candidate.

**Round 1 — Easy (for Quy)**
| Mai Tram asks | Quy answers | Vietnamese bridge |
|---------------|-------------|-------------------|
| "What is your name?" | "My name is Quy." | Tên bạn là gì? |
| "How old are you?" | "I am 21 years old." | Bạn bao nhiêu tuổi? |
| "Where are you from?" | "I am from Phan Thiết." | Bạn đến từ đâu? |

**Round 2 — Medium (push both)**
| Mai Tram asks | Quy answers | Vietnamese bridge |
|---------------|-------------|-------------------|
| "Why do you want this job?" | "I want to learn new things." | Tại sao bạn muốn công việc này? |
| "What is your best skill?" | "I am friendly and active." | Kỹ năng tốt nhất của bạn là gì? |
| "When can you start?" | "I can start next week." | Bạn có thể bắt đầu khi nào? |

**Round 3 — Trick Question (Mai Tram's specialty)**
| Mai Tram asks | Expected struggle | Coaching |

### Act 3 (10 min) — 3 Games

**Game 1: Two Truths and a Lie (Office Edition)**
- Each person says 3 sentences about their work. One is false.
- "I have 5 meetings today." / "My boss is very tall." / "I eat lunch at my desk."
- Other person guesses the lie.
- Forces sentence construction + listening.

**Game 2: Speed Vocab Relay**
- Flash a Vietnamese word → both race to say it in English
- First to say it correctly gets a point
- Use today's 10 words + previous session words
- Mai Tram handicap: she must use it in a sentence, not just the word

**Game 3: The Complaint Department**
- One person is the angry customer, one is the employee
- Customer complains about something ridiculous at BlueTech
- Employee must calm them down using today's vocabulary
- Switch roles after 2 minutes
- Forces improvisation + real phrases

### Fireworks Finish (5 min)

**The Challenge:**
- Both open their phones
- Set a timer: 2 minutes
- Each must record a voice message IN ENGLISH to send to each other
- Topic: "Tell your colleague what you learned today"
- Play back together — celebrate, laugh, calibrate
- Screenshot the timer → proof of practice for OBI
