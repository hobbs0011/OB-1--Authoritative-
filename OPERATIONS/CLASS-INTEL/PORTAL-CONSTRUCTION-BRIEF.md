## 3. TEACHING METHODOLOGY — WHAT GOES IN THE PORTAL

### Core Principles (student-facing, these are the rules)
1. **KISS** — Keep It Simple, Stupid. Broken English that communicates > perfect grammar that freezes.
2. **Two Brains** — Vietnamese brain (strong) and English brain (weak). Never translate. Never bridge. Different parts of the brain. If you think in Vietnamese while speaking English, you freeze.
3. **No Vietnamese in class** — Even one word means you're thinking in Vietnamese. Banned for the full 90 minutes.
4. **No notebooks / no scripts** — Crutches. Kill active listening. If you wrote questions beforehand, you won't hear the answers.
5. **No phones** — Translation apps = brain rot. TikTok = context-switching death. Distraction + Vietnamese-brain bridge = double kill.
6. **1 hour listening/day** — 45 min active listening (podcast/YouTube, 60% comprehension minimum) + 15 min speaking. English subtitles OK. Vietnamese subtitles = forbidden.
7. **Spaced repetition** — Review vocabulary at increasing intervals. OB app tracks this automatically per student.
8. **Useful words only** — Ignore rare vocabulary. Master 500–1,000 core words in 10 contexts each. Bruce Lee philosophy: one move 10,000 times > 10,000 moves once.
9. **Listen to what you know** — Don't fixate on unknown words. Context carries meaning.
10. **WWWWH** — Who, What, Where, When, Why. "Why" is the most powerful question. It finds passion. Passion = hours of conversation.
11. **Follow-up questions** — The #1 weakness. Scripts die after 2 minutes. Active listening opens infinite follow-ups.
12. **Mistakes = guideposts** — Not failures. If you're not making mistakes, you're not speaking.
13. **Laugh at yourself** — Disarms the fear of losing face.
14. **Graduation goal** — Intensive: 1 year. Standard: 1.5 years. Max: 2 years. After that, you don't need us. Scam centers want eternal students. We want you gone.

### Conversation Technique: "Everyone's Favorite Topic"
- Every person's favorite topic is themselves.
- Technique: Ask open question → listen actively → find the "why" behind their answer → follow that thread.
- Result: You never run out of things to say. The other person feels heard. You become a great conversationalist without needing a script.
- **Cultural application for Oktava staff:** They run out of questions fast because they rely on pre-written lists. Teach them to mine answers for follow-ups.

### Cultural Notes (Vietnam-specific, student-facing)
- **Compliments:** Vietnamese deflect ("No, I'm normal"). Accepting a compliment modestly = "Thank you, I practiced a lot." Not "I'm the best."
- **"Sorry":** Rarely said unless truly at fault or afraid of lawsuits. Canadians say it automatically (fake). Mai Tram is unusually polite — she says sorry more than most.
- **"Lose face":** Being embarrassed in public. The biggest social fear. Portal must never publicly shame.
- **Emotions:** Vietnamese don't hide bad moods. If they ignore you, they're not mad — they just have bad emotion. Don't take it personally.
- **Line-cutting:** Older generation (40+) will cut lines. Younger generation respects queues more.
- **"How's it going?":** In Canada = hi. In Vietnam, they might actually tell you. Context matters.
