# Methodology Extract - Huong Class (2026-06-11)

## 1. Face-Saving Reframe
**Description:** Teacher transforms error anxiety into learning opportunity, explicitly stating mistakes are safe.
**Timestamp:** 08:14-08:17
**Quote:** "Hey. You don't need to be afraid to make mistakes. Right? You make mistakes here, get more comfortable with it."

## 2. The "Why?" Question
**Description:** Teacher repeatedly asks "why" to force student reasoning instead of passive acceptance.
**Timestamp:** 37:16, 46:18, 48:49
**Quote:** "Period goes at the end of a sentence. Why? Why do you put a dot or a period at the end of a sentence?"

## 3. Sandwich Correction
**Description:** Teacher praises before correcting, softens correction with humor/self-deprecation.
**Timestamp:** 01:37
**Quote:** "Hey, Obi. I told you to do that already, stupid. We fixed that already." (teacher applies this to student context too)

## 4. Shadow/Repeat Drill
**Description:** Teacher asks student to repeat phrases to model pronunciation and rhythm.
**Timestamp:** 24-26, 37:29-38:13
**Quote:** "Repeat. Repetition. Repetition. Yeah. Try to spell it. Don't think it. Just try to spell it."

## 5. Minimal Pair Contrast
**Description:** Teacher contrasts similar sounds to highlight pronunciation differences.
**Timestamp:** 32:15-32:17
**Quote:** "You've studied. No. No. No. That's worse... So it's you've studied."

## 6. Elicitation
**Description:** Teacher leaves gaps for student to fill, prompting active participation.
**Timestamp:** 24:09-24:11
**Quote:** "What's a pitch deck? What is a pitch deck?" (student responds: "Pitch deck")

## 7. Charge Words / Emotion Anchoring
**Description:** Teacher uses emotional intensity to lock vocabulary - "sell ice to Eskimos" story.
**Timestamp:** 31:07-33:30
**Quote:** "I could sell ice to Eskimos. It's my only talent in life... So that means you're very good salesperson."

## 8. Student Echo Request
**Description:** Teacher asks student to say whole sentences for fluency practice.
**Timestamp:** 27:32-27:55
**Quote:** "Come into the office early tomorrow. So we can hit the ground running. Right? Read that."

## 9. Humor as Teaching Tool
**Description:** Teacher uses jokes and sarcasm to make language memorable.
**Timestamp:** Multiple
**Quote:** "Don't let a British person ever try to tell you that's not a garbage can and it's rubbish"

## 10. Confidence Over Perfection
**Description:** Teacher emphasizes natural delivery over perfect grammar.
**Timestamp:** 58:56-58:58
**Quote:** "Basically, you're just gonna read what's up there. Right? So it's not in what you say as much as it's in how you say it."
