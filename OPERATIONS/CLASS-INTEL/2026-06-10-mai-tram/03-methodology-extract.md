# Methodology Extract - Mai Tram Class (2026-06-10)

## 1. Sandwich Correction
**Description:** Teacher softens corrections with humor and validation.
**Timestamp:** 01:20-01:30
**Quote:** "Oh, they suck. They suck. They suck. Right? S u c k. Yeah. You do know what's up."

## 2. The "Why?" Question
**Description:** Teacher asks "why" to probe understanding and reasoning.
**Timestamp:** 01:50, 01:52, 02:10
**Quote:** "Why? Why isn't it fun? Why does the Vietnamese never say, I'm sorry or I don't know."

## 3. Repetition Drill with Emotional Anchoring
**Description:** Teacher uses "cool" and "sucks" as foundational emotional vocabulary.
**Timestamp:** 17:32-17:55
**Quote:** "Cool. Cool. Cool. Cool. And suck. That sucks. Sucks. Yeah. That sucks. Oh, that sucks."

## 4. Elicitation Through Questions
**Description:** Teacher uses questions to draw out student responses.
**Timestamp:** 00:13-00:26
**Quote:** "Are you HR? Right? You are HR? So you have to get employee contract?"

## 5. Student Echo Request
**Description:** Teacher asks student to repeat phrases for fluency modeling.
**Timestamp:** 24:10-25:58
**Quote:** "Deck. Write down. Pitch deck. Write deck. Write down. Down. Pitch deck."

## 6. Minimal Pair Contrast
**Description:** Teacher contrasts "me" vs "I" for proper grammar.
**Timestamp:** 36:32-36:55
**Quote:** "Never say me. Just say I. When you're talking about yourself, way more common and proper."

## 7. Charge Words / Emotion Anchoring
**Description:** Teacher emphasizes emotional impact of "sucks" vs "that sucks".
**Timestamp:** 14:19-14:40
**Quote:** "That sucks. Oh, my my wife, she cheat on me. Oh, that sucks. Just kidding. Not so bad."

## 8. Confidence Over Perfection
**Description:** Teacher reframes speaking blocks as practice issues.
**Timestamp:** 48:50-48:55
**Quote:** "It's not it's not a knowledge problem. It's a practice problem."

## 9. Rhythm/Pause Training for Presentations
**Description:** Teacher models full stop vs comma for presentation delivery.
**Timestamp:** 40:10-40:29
**Quote:** "Comma is the hey. Half pause. Half pause. Because you're separating the ideas."

## 10. Self-Reflexive Correction
**Description:** Teacher encourages student to self-correct rather than be corrected.
**Timestamp:** 36:32-36:55
**Quote:** "People don't really care if you say 'me and Qui'. Me and Qui, we went to the store."

## 11. Cultural Context Teaching
**Description:** Teacher explains cultural background of idioms and expressions.
**Timestamp:** 14:30-15:58
**Quote:** "Stuffed animal. Teddy bear. What does stuffed mean? Stuffed. Step. Yeah. What does mean?"
