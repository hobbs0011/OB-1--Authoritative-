## Risk Assessment
- **Image paths**: Must verify all copied assets load correctly
- **Performance**: Adding images increases page weight — use lazy loading and proper sizing
- **Animation performance**: Use `transform` and `opacity` only for GPU-accelerated animations
- **Mobile**: Ensure all new sections work well on small screens
