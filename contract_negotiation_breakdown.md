# Contract Counter-Proposal

## 1. Financial Comparison

### Full-Time Offer
- 160 hours/month at 50,000,000 VND gross
- Gross hourly: 312,500 VND/hour
- Net hourly (after 10% tax): 281,250 VND/hour
- 36% below minimum floor of 440,000 VND/hour net

### Part-Time Offer
- 80 hours/month at 36,000,000 VND gross
- Gross hourly: 450,000 VND/hour
- Net hourly: 405,000 VND/hour
- Still below minimum floor

### Proposed Structure
- Base: 100 hours/month at 444,444 VND/hour gross
- Base net: 40,000,000 VND/month
- Hours above 100: 500,000+ VND/hour
- Respects minimum rate with flexibility

## 2. Key Terms

- Reporting: Ang Ming + Ang Bao only. Miss Hien is not the manager.
- Teaching assistant: Hobbs hires, trains, manages. Reports to Hobbs, not Miss Hien.
- TA probation: 2-week trial, then paid if performing. Hobbs reserves right to fire if not learning/progressing.
- TA recruitment: Hobbs can recruit replacement TA if current one doesn't work out.
- Classroom: No unscheduled observations. No peer eval by non-teachers. Full methodology control.
- Boundaries: Management stays out of class during instruction except emergencies.
- Work permit: Two-year repayment clause removed.
