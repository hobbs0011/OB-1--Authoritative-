## Point System
- Vocabulary Points: 10 pts per correct contextual use
- Idiom Points: 25 pts per correct idiomatic usage
- Participation Points: 5 pts per voluntary contribution
- Game Win Bonus: 50 pts
- Perfect Session: 100 pts

## Badge Hierarchy
- **Mouse Badge**: Attended first 3 sessions
- **Cub Badge**: 500 pts + teacher verification
- **Tiger Badge**: 2,000 pts + unprompted English initiation (2 sessions)
- **Legend Badge**: Tiger for 60+ days + 1 peer mentoring session

## Design Principle
Effort earns points, not attendance. Showing up is the floor.

## Rewards (Status, not cash)
- 500 pts: Early access to new content
- 1,000 pts: Featured in monthly success story
- 2,000 pts: 15-min Tiger Session with Michael
- 5,000 pts: NET Legend Wall (permanent)

## Seasonal Competitions
- Q1: Mouse Migration (most improved)
- Q2: Tiger Hunt Championship
- Q3: Idiom Invitational
- Q4: Legend Cup (annual cumulative)
