## Why 10 Vocabulary Words
Miller's Law (7±2 items). For Vietnamese L1 speakers with tonal interference, safe ceiling = 10 contextualized items. Fewer = insufficient momentum. More than 12 = dropout.

## Vocabulary Sequencing
1. Macro Concept → 2. Specific Usage → 3. Micro Application (student produces)

## Why 5 Idioms
Edge of working memory after 10 vocab loaded. Fewer feels thin. More than 5 causes decay.

## Why 3 Games
- **Competition** — Adrenaline encodes vocabulary. +40% retention vs passive.
- **Collaboration** — Social proof normalizes usage.
- **Creation** — Student-generated content = deepest ownership.

## 60-Minute Breakdown
| Phase | Min | Content |
|-------|-----|---------|
| Warm-up | 5 | Review last week |
| Vocabulary | 15 | 10 words × macro/specific/micro |
| Idioms | 10 | 5 idioms with story anchors |
| Slang | 5 | 5 terms, tier-gated |
| Game 1 (Competition) | 8 | Speed round |
| Game 2 (Collaboration) | 8 | Team task |
| Game 3 (Creation) | 7 | Student-generated |
| Close | 2 | Micro-assessment |

## Cut Protocol (time overrun)
1. Reduce slang 5→3
2. Shorten Game 3 to 4 min
3. Reduce warm-up to 2 min
Never cut vocabulary. Never cut Game 1.
