## The Human-AI Teaching Partnership Model

NET does not replace teachers with AI. It divides labor precisely. AI handles volume, consistency, and pattern. Humans handle culture, emotion, and escalation.

## Division of Responsibility

| Task | AI (CHATTY PRIME) | Human Teacher |
|------|-------------------|---------------|
| Vocabulary delivery | ✅ | Monitors |
| Idiom explanation | ✅ | Supplements with stories |
| Game facilitation | ✅ | Moderates culture dynamics |
| Stealth correction | ✅ | Confirms or overrides |
| Face-saving in class | ❌ | ✅ Required |
| Parent/family communication | ❌ | ✅ Required |
| Tier promotion decision | Recommends | ✅ Final authority |
| Crisis escalation | Flags | ✅ Handles |

## Teacher Quality Gate — TMST

5 questions, 5/5 to deploy:
1. What is the 10-5-5-3 formula and why does each number exist?
2. Define Mouse, Cub, and Tiger with three behavioral markers each.
3. Demonstrate Stealth Correction.
4. How do you handle a student who refuses to speak?
5. What is Tiger Voice and how do you activate it in a Cub?

## Training Phases
- Phase 1 (Day 1-2): Read all seeds, study method, shadow lesson, complete TMST
- Phase 2 (Day 3-5): Supervised delivery with debrief
- Phase 3 (Day 6+): Independent with CHATTY PRIME co-facilitation

## Performance Metrics (Weekly)
- Student Tier Progression Rate
- Retention Rate
- Tiger Voice Activation Rate

## Drift Detection
- Skipping game rotation
- Public correction without stealth technique
- Allowing Blue-tier language
- Shortcutting vocabulary below 10 words
- Student self-initiation drops below 30%
